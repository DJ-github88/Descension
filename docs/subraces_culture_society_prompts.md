# Mythrill VTT — Master Subrace Culture & Capital Establishment Architecture Prompt Book

> **Visual Directive & Style Mandates**:
> 1. **Style**: 1995 Fantasy Concept Art in the style of Samwise Didier, Always Be Chunky (ABC). Hand-drawn rough black charcoal and graphite draft linework with expressive, gritty cross-hatching, bold thick architectural silhouettes on clean off-white watercolor sketchbook paper.
> 2. **CRITICAL FOCUS SHIFT — IMMERSIVE CAPITAL ESTABLISHMENTS IN FULL ARCHITECTURAL FOCUS**:
>    - **DO NOT place giant characters in the foreground blocking the frame from an outskirt overlook or cliff edge.**
>    - The primary subject is a **wide-angle, deep-focus look directly INSIDE the main capital establishment / metropolis**.
>    - Prompts explicitly showcase:
>      * **How they traverse**: The streets, bridges, waterways, suspension catwalks, zero-G skyways, canal locks, water flumes, and elevators.
>      * **Where they shop & trade**: Bustling market plazas, tiered barter stalls, canal wharves, awning-shaded trading counters, and specialized merchants.
>      * **Where they hunt, fish & harvest**: Aquaculture pools, fish sluices, snail traps, terrace farms, mushroom grottos, and forging yards.
>      * **Where they study & govern**: Open-air archive colonnades, listening chambers, council amphitheatres, scriptorium towers, and observatories.
>      * **Stuff they use**: Race-specific tools, measuring instruments, weapons, talismans, ledger scrolls, tuning forks, and gear.
>      * **Citizens in Scale**: Multiple citizens matching their canonical T-pose & icon anatomy, depicted *at natural architectural scale* actively moving through the city living their daily lives.
> 3. **Strictly Monochromatic Draft**: Characters, architecture, buildings, stone, and props are rendered in pure black-and-white graphite and charcoal draft lines with gritty pencil shading. **Zero color fills on characters or architecture**.
> 4. **Single Dynamic Watercolor Splash**: The **ONLY** color in the entire image is the single explosive watercolor bloom in the subrace's signature palette blooming behind the central skyline/district with fluid paint splatters and bleeds.
> 5. **Anti-Text & Anti-Artifact Guardrails**: `STRICTLY NO TEXT, NO WORDS, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO LABELS, NO SIGNATURES, NO WATERMARKS, NO BORDERS, NO FRAMES`.
> 6. **100% Canonical Anatomical Lock**: Features, ears, eyes, tendrils, scales, feet, and gear match our approved T-poses and canonical benchmark icons exactly.
