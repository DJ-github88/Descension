# SUBREGION: TIDEFALL ARCHIPELAGO
### Continent: Iceheart Sea

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
A volcanic and coral island chain surrounded by warm and cold tidal currents. Home to thriving maritime trading towns.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Merryn Island Clans:** Fisherfolk, shipwrights, and trade barons.
* **Reef Myrathil:** Brightly colored aquatic humanoids inhabiting coral reefs.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **Tidefall Harbor:** Bustling island city built on volcanic basalt pillars.
* **The Coral Spire:** Deep-water temple dedicated to the Deity of Waters.
