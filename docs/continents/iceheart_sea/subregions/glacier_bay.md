# SUBREGION: GLACIER BAY
### Continent: Iceheart Sea

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
A sheltered icy gulf lined with massive glacial ice-walls. Calm, freezing waters used for whaling and ice harvesting.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Merryn Whalers:** Tough coastal hunters and oil refiners.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **Whalebone Bay Outpost:** Fortified coastal village constructed from ice-blocks and timber.
