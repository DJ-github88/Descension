# SUBREGION: SHATTERED FLOES
### Continent: Iceheart Sea

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
A treacherous expanse of shifting icebergs, frozen floes, and fog. A haven for smugglers, sea-pirates, and monstrosities.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Ice-Corsairs:** Outlaw sailors and smugglers.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **Floe-Haven:** Hidden pirate town built on a massive floating iceberg.
