# SUBREGION: THE FROZEN STRAITS
### Continent: Iceheart Sea

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
Main shipping lanes connecting eastern and western Mythrill. Navigable during summer months, but heavily guarded by ice-breaker naval fleets during freeze-up.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Merryn Seafarers:** Merchant sailors and ice-breaker captains.
* **Coastal Myrathil:** Aquatic humanoids offering pilot services through ice channels.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **Port Iceheart:** Monolithic island fortress port and naval base.
* **Lighthouse of the Sister Moon:** Ancient beacon guiding ships through lunar tides.
