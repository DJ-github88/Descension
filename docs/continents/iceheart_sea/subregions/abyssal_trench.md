# SUBREGION: THE ABYSSAL TRENCH
### Continent: Iceheart Sea

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
Plunging miles into the oceanic depths, the Abyssal Trench is a sunless world of immense pressure, thermal vents, and bioluminescent ecosystems.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Abyssal Myrathil:** Deep-sea bioluminescent aquatic humanoids.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **Thalassa Spire:** Ancient bioluminescent sunken city built inside the trench wall.
* **The Leviathan Rift:** Unfathomable deep fissure where ancient sea-beasts slumber.
