# SUBREGION: MISTRAL REACH
### Continent: Iceheart Sea

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
The open, wind-whipped outer sea expanse. Massive ocean swells, gale-force squalls, and deep-water sea creatures define this maritime wilderness.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Deep-Sea Mariners & Open-Sea Myrathil.**

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **Mistral Shoals:** Dangerous submerged reef system marked by tragic shipwrecks.
