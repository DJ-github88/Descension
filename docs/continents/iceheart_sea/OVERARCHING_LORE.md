# ICEHEART SEA — OVERARCHING LORE

## 1. CONTINENT OVERVIEW & BIOME
* **Biome:** Frozen Oceans, Island Chains, Glacial Icebergs & Deep Trenches.
* **Cosmic Connection:** Anchored to the gravitational and thermal influence of **The Sister Moon (Selunis)** — the never-quickened moon-daughter egg Keth Amar nested in during its approach, now frozen as the world's thermal buffer. Her tides govern the iceberg drifts, and Natural Wyrd phenomena — and, since Year 300, Predatory Wyrd storms and mimic-currents — ride the same pull.
* **Dominant Subraces:** **Merryn Humans** (seafarers & naval merchants), **Myrathil** (open-sea, coastal, and abyssal subraces, seafoam-born of the Natural Wyrd).
* **Major Factions:** The Iceheart Admiralty, Deep-Tide Coven, Myrathil Sovereign Council, and Brine-Bond smugglers who move Aex Shards by ship.

---

## 2. SUBREGION BREAKDOWN
1. **The Frozen Straits** — Navigable ice-choked sea-lanes, kept open by the moon's pull and contested by the Admiralty.
2. **Tidefall Archipelago** — Island chains inhabited by Merryn seafarers and Myrathil, where the monolith-shards lie drowned.
3. **The Abyssal Trench** — Deep ocean rifts housing ancient Myrathil cities and a Sundered Monolith — a great fallen shard of Aex's aegis.
4. **Glacier Bay** — Protected icy gulfs and whaling outposts.
5. **Shattered Floes** — Drifting iceberg fields and pirate hideouts, haunted by Predatory Wyrd mimic-ice.
6. **Mistral Reach** — Wind-swept outer sea-front where the Sleeping Soul's loom frays.
