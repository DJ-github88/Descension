# ICEHEART SEA — OVERARCHING LORE

## 1. CONTINENT OVERVIEW & BIOME
* **Biome:** Frozen Oceans, Island Chains, Glacial Icebergs & Deep Trenches.
* **Cosmic Connection:** Anchored to the gravitational and thermal influence of **The Sister Moon**. Governed by violent tides, iceberg drifts, and oceanic Wyrd phenomena.
* **Dominant Subraces:** **Merryn Humans** (seafarers & naval merchants), **Myrathil** (open-sea, coastal, and abyssal subraces).
* **Major Factions:** The Iceheart Admiralty, Deep-Tide Coven, Myrathil Sovereign Council.

---

## 2. SUBREGION BREAKDOWN
1. **The Frozen Straits** — Navigable ice-choked sea-lanes.
2. **Tidefall Archipelago** — Island chains inhabited by Merryn seafarers and Myrathil.
3. **The Abyssal Trench** — Deep ocean rifts housing ancient Myrathil cities.
4. **Glacier Bay** — Protected icy gulfs and whaling outposts.
5. **Shattered Floes** — Drifting iceberg fields and pirate hideouts.
6. **Mistral Reach** — Wind-swept outer sea-front.
