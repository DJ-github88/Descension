# NORDHALLA — OVERARCHING LORE

## 1. CONTINENT OVERVIEW & BIOME
* **Biome:** Polar Arctic Glacier, Frozen Chasms & Jagged Granite Peaks.
* **Historical Significance:** Site of **Blizzard's End**, the highest northern peak in Mythrill where Keth Amar devoured the royal heirs 150 years ago. Aex shattered here, sending **Aex Shards** raining across Mythrill.
* **Dominant Subraces:** **Skald Humans** (Northland tundra warriors), **Rime-Born**, **Frostbound**, **Corvani**.
* **Major Factions:** House Skalvyr, Glacier Keepers, The Nordhalla Syndicate (holders of a local Aex Shard).

---

## 2. SUBREGION BREAKDOWN
1. **The Glacier-Heart** — Polar ice fields, glacier archives, and ancestral holds.
2. **Rime-Spire Peaks** — Jagged mountain spires housing Blizzard's End massacre site and ancient monoliths.
3. **Frostbite Coast** — Frozen fjords, ironclad naval harbors, and whaling camps.
