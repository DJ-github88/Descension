# SUBREGION: THE GLACIER-HEART
### Continent: Nordhalla

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
The interior polar ice field of Nordhalla. Moving glaciers, miles-deep frozen chasms, and ancient ice-covered keeps dominate this frozen wilderness.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Rime-Born:** Survivors of ancient hunger pacts with stone-shadow skin.
* **Frostbound:** Blue-skinned subfolk who survived childhood exposure to arctic cold.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **The Hunger Glaciers:** Massive slow-moving ice sheets that crush ancient ruins beneath them.
* **Rimor's Hearth:** Buried pre-Binding keep with stubborn underground geothermal steam vents.
* **Glacier-Song Hermitage:** Solitary monastery where Augurs record Aex's silent resonance.
