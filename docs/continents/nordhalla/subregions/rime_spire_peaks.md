# SUBREGION: RIME-SPIRE PEAKS
### Continent: Nordhalla

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
Rime-Spire Peaks contains the highest, most desolate granite pinnacles on Mythrill. Perpetual blizzards and howling arctic winds scour the peaks. At the highest northern point lies **Blizzard's End**, the site where Keth Amar devoured the royal heirs 150 years ago.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Skald Humans:** Northland warrior clans who honor endurance as religion.
* **Corvani:** Raven-marked subfolk who nest in mile-high glacial eyries.
* **Nordhalla Syndicate Operatives:** Secretive faction exploiting a fallen Aex Shard.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS

### The Massacre Site
* **Blizzard's End Peak:** The desolate summit where the 6 heirs were devoured. The ground is permanently stained with cracked obsidian ice and pulsing Aex Shard resonance.

### Cities & Strongholds
* **Frosthold Citadel:** Capital seat of King-Jarl Halvar Skalvyr, carved directly into the granite face of Bearsbeard's Beak.
* **Syndicate Vault:** Underground fortress where the Nordhalla Syndicate harnesses a massive Aex Shard to forge frost-steel weaponry.

### Natural & Cosmic Landmarks
* **Bearsbeard's Beak:** The highest mountain peak on Mythrill.
* **Skadi's Col:** A narrow mountain gap with flesh-stripping blizzard winds.
* **The Frozen Archive:** A glacier-tomb city where ancestors stand upright in ice.
