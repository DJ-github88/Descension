# RIME-SPIRE PEAKS — Subregion Lore (nordhalla-glacier-heart)

The warm accident at the top of the world: a west-central pocket where the vents never failed, the pines never died, and everyone's secrets grow thick enough to hide in.

## GEOGRAPHY & THE IMPOSSIBLE PINES
The Icetalon Peaks rake the sky; at their feet, the Whispering Pine forest drinks geothermal steam through roots that never froze. Blue ice spires, black-sand beaches, and alpine winds that file the granite like patient knives. This is the only forest in the north that still grows — which makes every stand of it worth a small war, and several are ongoing.

## WHO HOLDS WHAT
* **Snowcall City** — the Dal's loud sibling: timber money, vent-leases, and the best smugglers' lawyers in the north. Officially royal. Practically for sale.
* **The Corvani Roosts** (Rook's Promontory, Ravencall Eyrie) — raven-marked messengers bound to the Corvid Fate-Spirits. They trade memory for passage, remember every deal ever made in their hearing, and charge the crown double.
* **Blóðhöll** — the Berserker trial-grounds, where the Hunger Pact is tested in governed fury. Elder Skalds still claim the right to decide who may carry the Pact — a claim the forges of Sundale quietly resent.
* **Þögn, the Still Crag** — the cliff where no wind blows and no sound carries. The Skald say the Warden's breath touched the mountain at the anchoring and froze it to a silent witness. Ice sculptures of unknown figures stand in attitudes of supplication. Nothing hunts here; nothing speaks either.
* **Gjaldhringr** — the glacial amphitheater whose walls carry the Bargain's runic text, written in ice so dense it reads as texture. Law-speakers come to argue precedent in front of the original signature.
* **Sválghjarta's Keep** — the Swallow-Heart fortress: a warm thing that eats heat and gives back silence, held by the Hungríd Cult, who believe hunger is the last honest teacher. The Keep grows one room colder each year, and the Cult grows one member stranger.

## EVERYDAY LIFE & THE LEDGER
Loggers fell Whispering Pine under armed guard; the logging camps pay Snowcall, Snowcall pays the crown, and the crown pretends the forest is infinite. Varmagrim and the vent-villages sell heat by the hour-shift. Frost-Tithe Cradle-Camps dot the passes, where midwives and Corvani messengers run the grim logistics of Rime-Born birth — and where the Ice-Cradles keep the surviving Frostbound children alive.

## FRICTION (adventures grow here)
* **The forest war:** Logging concessions overlap ancestral groves. The Øsling don't recognize paper claims; Snowcall's lawyers don't recognize anything else. Bodies keep surfacing in the pine-steam, neatly pressed.
* **Þögn's tenants:** The ice sculptures at the Still Crag have changed posture since last thaw. The Corvani refuse to deliver any message that mentions it.
* **The Cult's grocery bill:** The Hungríd have started buying grain — quietly, in quantity — which for a fasting cult is either heresy or logistics. Someone should find out which before winter.
* **Blóðhöll's succession:** Three elders, two candidates for Pact-bearer, and one trial-ground. The Berserker tradition decides by combat; the tradition's future decides by who survives the deciding.
* **The high passes:** Masked Acolytes out of Vespera's Crown work the trails, healing frostbite and doing odd jobs while their god looks through their eyes. The locals like them. That is the problem. (Do not confuse the Crown with the Corvani Perch — the raven-folk want very little to do with either.)
