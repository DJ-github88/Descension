# SUBREGION: THE FROSTBITE COAST
### Continent: Nordhalla

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
The rugged coastal perimeter of Nordhalla. Towering sea-cliffs, ice-locked fjords, and brutal coastal waves define this maritime frontier.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Skald Seafarers:** Ironclad whalers, raiders, and naval merchants.
* **Coastal Myrathil:** Cold-water aquatic humanoids dwelling in ice caves along the fjords.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **Ironfjord Harbor:** Fortified naval port housing Skald longships and whale-oil refineries.
* **Whaletooth Outpost:** Coastal hunting settlement built from mammoth bone and timber.
* **Screaming Fjords:** Deep sea-channels where howling winds mimic Aex's original song.
