# FROSTFANG WASTES — Subregion Lore (nordhalla-frostfang-wastes)

Everything beyond the Wall: whiteout glaciers, wind-scoured tundra, and the truth of what the north paid. The Øsling say if you can see the horizon, the weather is lying to you.

## GEOGRAPHY & THE HUNGRY ICE
A polar desert of shifting crevasses and perpetual blizzard, where summer lasts eight weeks and spends most of them apologizing. The ice here moves with what the locals politely refuse to call intention. Ancient barrows — the Heir-Mounds, the Chant-Mounds — break the white like teeth. Mammoth herds thread the passes, and the Jutul hold the deep granite, as they did before any human signature existed.

The Wastes hold the north's holiest and worst ground: Blizzard's End, the peak where the heirs died and the Mother shattered. The shard-fall struck thickest here. Even now, waste-walkers stake claims on glow-ice and defend them with everything, because a shard claim is a hearth that can't be besieged — only taken.

## WHO HOLDS WHAT
* **Øsling Clan Confederation** — mammoth-hunters and caravan-guides who never signed the Jarl's consolidation. Beyond the Wall, their word outranks the crown's ink. The Icechamber licenses their hunts; the Øsling accept the licenses as fire-starters.
* **The Stone-Speakers** — the last who still trade with the Jutul frost-giants, in the old way: gift for gift, grudge for grudge. Everyone else counts spearheads and distance.
* **Blizzard's End** — the massacre site, now a pilgrimage for the faith-mad and the historically honest. The Vigil wants it consecrated. The Scoured want it sealed. The Øsling want the crowds to stop frightening the mammoths.
* **The Heir-Mounds** — barrow-graves older than the Freeze, raising questions the houses refuse to answer. The bog-dead of other regions walk; the Mounds' occupants have, so far, only *listened*.
* **Rimor's Hearth & the Mute-Hearth** — one ruin still warm with old vent-magic, one ruin where the vent died and took the settlement's voice with it. Scavengers and silence-seekers, respectively.
* **Frost Chanters** — hermit-singers of the waste who keep the pre-Bargain songs. The Cleansing hasn't reached them yet. The Acolytes already have.

## EVERYDAY LIFE & THE LEDGER
The Wastes don't farm; they hunt, herd, and harvest the dead culture's leavings. Mammoth-hide, tallow, and shard-glow move south through Wall gates that tax them — which is why the Øsling routes that *don't* use the gates are the north's worst-kept secret and best-kept map. A waste-family's wealth is measured in dogs, arrows, and grandparents who remember where the crevasses moved.

## FRICTION (adventures grow here)
* **The shard rush:** Glow-ice claims attract miners, claim-jumpers, and the Wyrd-things that shard-light wakes. The Øsling solution — arbitration by elder — is losing to the newcomer solution — arbitration by crossbow.
* **The pilgrimage problem:** Faith traffic to Blizzard's End needs guides, guards, and grain the Wastes don't spare. The Vigil pays in indulgences. The Øsling have started asking what an indulgence weighs when your children are hungry.
* **What the Mounds heard:** The Stone-Speakers report the Jutul have gone quiet and the Heir-Mounds have not. Something under the barrows is answering questions nobody asked aloud.
* **The Acolyte kindness:** Out past the Wall, the Masked ones take no payment but favors owed. The waste-villages have begun to owe. A census-taker would find this fascinating; the crown sent none.
* **Whiteout season:** Eight weeks of "summer" approach, and every faction's logistics — legal, smuggled, and blasphemous — must move at once, through the same passes, under the same thaw-slides.
