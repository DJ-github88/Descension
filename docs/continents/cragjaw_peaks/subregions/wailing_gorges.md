# SUBREGION: THE WAILING GORGES
### Continent: Cragjaw Peaks

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
Narrow mountain chasms where howling mountain winds generate eerie, acoustic wails. Rich in mineral veins and ancient Groven stone carvings.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Tessen Humans:** Mountain scouts and cliff-dwellers.
* **Groven Earth-Kin:** Stone-skinned humanoids bound to ancient Primordial Earth deities.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **Cragjaw Citadel:** High mountain fortress guarding the central pass.
* **Viridane's Abandoned Vault:** The ruined ancestral hall left behind when the House of Viridane fled to Frostwood Reach.
