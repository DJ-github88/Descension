# SUBREGION: IRONSPIRE CANYONS
### Continent: Cragjaw Peaks

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
Iron-hued canyons filled with natural ironwood bridges, mining tunnels, and Groven subterranean citadels.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Groven Stone-Forgers:** Master alchemical stonemasons.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **Ironspire Hold:** Massive underground fortress carved into an iron-veined mountain peak.
