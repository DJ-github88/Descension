# CRAGJAW PEAKS — OVERARCHING LORE

## 1. CONTINENT OVERVIEW & BIOME
* **Biome:** Jagged Mountain Chasms, Underdark Tunnels & High-Altitude Canyons.
* **Geopolitical & Historical Context:** Ancestral homeland of the **Tessen Humans** and the stone-bound **Groven**. Formerly inhabited by the **House of Viridane** before they fled to Frostwood Reach.
* **Dominant Subraces:** **Tessen Humans** (mountaineers & stone-sentinels), **Groven** (Primordial Earth subraces).
* **Major Factions:** The High Citadel Council, Groven Stone-Lords, Mountain Sentinel Rangers.

---

## 2. SUBREGION BREAKDOWN
1. **Wailing Gorges** — Wind-sheared canyons echoing with howling mountain gusts.
2. **Ironspire Canyons** — Deep iron-rich mountain rifts and Groven stone-fortresses.
3. **Stormbound Heights** — High-altitude pinnacles exposed to elemental tempests.
