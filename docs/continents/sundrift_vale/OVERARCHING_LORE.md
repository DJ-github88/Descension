# SUNDRIFT VALE — OVERARCHING LORE

## 1. CONTINENT OVERVIEW & BIOME
* **Biome:** Starlight Steppes, Geothermally Warmed Valleys & Silver Savannas.
* **Geothermal Origin:** Warmed when the Ordan noble purge — a Blood Seal snapping under a forced purge — cracked Aex's aegis beneath southern Mythrill. The warmth that bled through turned steppe into savanna and gave the Vale its silver basins.
* **Dominant Subraces:** **Astril** (Stargazer & Brutish subraces, star-refugees whose trail led Keth Amar to Sol), **Disguised Ordan Humans** (thought extinct, living secretly in disguise — a lineage the Whispered Purge tried to erase and failed).
* **Major Factions:** The Brutish Astril Hegemony, Stargazer Sanctuary Council, Secret Ordan Resistance — and, in the basins, Mendicants who trade Aex Shards for starlight.

---

## 2. SUBREGION BREAKDOWN
1. **Starlight Steppes** — Rolling grassy plateaus illuminated by glowing cosmic flora.
2. **Ordan Ruins** — Ancient stone citadels where the Ordan massacre snapped a Blood Seal.
3. **Astril Enclaves** — High-tech crystalline star-cities built by the Astril, still listening for the predator they fled.
4. **Whispering Savanna** — Open plains where the Predatory Wyrd's whispers carry farthest.
5. **Silver Basins** — Geothermal hot spring basins and starlight reflection lakes where a Sundered Monolith — a great fallen shard — lies half-buried.
