# SUNDRIFT VALE — OVERARCHING LORE

## 1. CONTINENT OVERVIEW & BIOME
* **Biome:** Starlight Steppes, Geothermally Warmed Valleys & Silver Savannas.
* **Geothermal Origin:** Warmed when the Ordan noble purge cracked Aex's shell beneath southern Mythrill.
* **Dominant Subraces:** **Astril** (Stargazer & Brutish subraces), **Disguised Ordan Humans** (thought extinct, living secretly in disguise).
* **Major Factions:** The Brutish Astril Hegemony, Stargazer Sanctuary Council, Secret Ordan Resistance.

---

## 2. SUBREGION BREAKDOWN
1. **Starlight Steppes** — Rolling grassy plateaus illuminated by glowing cosmic flora.
2. **Ordan Ruins** — Ancient stone citadels where the Ordan massacre took place.
3. **Astril Enclaves** — High-tech crystalline star-cities built by the Astril.
4. **Whispering Savanna** — Open plains where Sacred Magic currents hum.
5. **Silver Basins** — Geothermal hot spring basins and starlight reflection lakes.
