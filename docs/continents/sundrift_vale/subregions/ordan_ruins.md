# SUBREGION: ORDAN RUINS
### Continent: Sundrift Vale

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
Ancient basalt and marble citadels. Site of the legendary *Night of the Bleeding Stars* where the Ordan assassin executed his bloodline under Brutish Astril orders.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Brutish Astril Inquisitors & Rogue Ordan Assassin ("Itachi").**

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **Citadel of the Bleeding Stars:** The blood-stained royal throne room where the Ordan nobility fell.
