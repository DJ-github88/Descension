# SUBREGION: ASTRIL ENCLAVES
### Continent: Sundrift Vale

Advanced crystalline star-cities powered by starlight conduits and levitating star-alloys.

* **Brutish Astril Rulers & Stargazer Astril Citizens.**

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
