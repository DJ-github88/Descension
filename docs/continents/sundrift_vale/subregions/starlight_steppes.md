# SUBREGION: STARLIGHT STEPPES
### Continent: Sundrift Vale

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
Rolling steppe plateaus warmed by subterranean thermal vents. Glowing starlight flora illuminates the landscape at night.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Stargazer Astril:** Peaceful scholars studying cosmic constellations.
* **Disguised Ordan Humans:** Living in secret under ordinary human identities.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **Starlight Observatory:** Crystalline star-tower watching the Sister Moon and void constellations.
