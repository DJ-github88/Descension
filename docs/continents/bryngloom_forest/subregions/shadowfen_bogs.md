# SUBREGION: SHADOWFEN BOGS
### Continent: Bryngloom Forest

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
Deep, lawless black-water marshes filled with toxic native miasma and shifting bog mists, with pockets of genuine Wyrd-corruption around the cultist holdouts.
