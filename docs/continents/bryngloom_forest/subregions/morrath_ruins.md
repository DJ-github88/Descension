# SUBREGION: MORRATH RUINS
### Continent: Bryngloom Forest

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
Submerged stone ruins housing the 7th Counterfeit Monolith — the Predatory Wyrd forgery standing in the place of the seal that was never written. Raised by House Morrath's forerunners, now tended by the Masked Acolytes as a whispering shrine to Keth Amar.
