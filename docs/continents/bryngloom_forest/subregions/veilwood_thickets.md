# SUBREGION: VEILWOOD THICKETS
### Continent: Bryngloom Forest

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
Sacred, mist-veiled ironwood and ancient oak groves grown by the Sleeping Soul's Natural Wyrd and shielded from Keth Amar's Predatory Wyrd by the Sleeping Soul itself. The Florae slumber here under that ward.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Florae:** Treant-like humanoids with erased memories.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **The Sacred Grove of Roots:** The primeval birthplace of the Florae.
