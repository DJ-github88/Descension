# SUBREGION: SUNKEN SPIRES
### Continent: Bryngloom Forest

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
Submerged stone spires rising out of boiling sulfur swamps. Warmed by Aex's thermal cracks beneath the bog.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Vreken:** Amber-eyed crypt keepers in sunken towers.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **Crypt-City of Bryngloom:** Submerged basalt city built around ancient Vreken mausoleums.
