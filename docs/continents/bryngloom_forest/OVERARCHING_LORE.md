# BRYNGLOOM FOREST — OVERARCHING LORE

## 1. CONTINENT OVERVIEW & BIOME
* **Biome:** Sunken Bog-Forest, Boiling Sulfur Marshes & Rotting Cypress Swamps.
* **Geothermal & Historical Origin:** When the Vreken progenitors extorted Aethil for Blight magic, Aethil cursed them with decay. Trying to break the curse, the Vreken slaughtered their nobility early in history. Aex cracked beneath Bryngloom, erupting boiling sulfur springs, steaming bogs, and volcanic vents that submerged their ancient stone spires into dark bogs.
* **Dominant Subraces:** **Vreken** (amber-eyed, long-eared crypt keepers), **Florae** (memory-wiped treant beings saved by the Nature Deity in secret groves).
* **Major Factions:** The Vreken Crypt-Guild, Florae Root-Keepers, House Morrath Impostor Cult.

---

## 2. SUBREGION BREAKDOWN
1. **Sunken Spires** — Drowned stone spires and ancient Vreken crypt cities.
2. **Blightmire Hollows** — Boiling sulfur bogs and Blight-corrupted hollows.
3. **Rotting Canopy** — Massive cypress canopy towers inhabited by swamp hunters.
4. **Morrath Ruins** — Site of the hollow false monolith built by House Morrath.
5. **Veilwood Thickets** — Protected sacred groves where the Florae slumber.
6. **Shadowfen Bogs** — Lawless deep marshes wracked by Wyrd mist.
