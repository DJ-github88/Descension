# BRYNGLOOM FOREST — OVERARCHING LORE

## 1. CONTINENT OVERVIEW & BIOME
* **Biome:** Sunken Bog-Forest, Boiling Sulfur Marshes & Rotting Cypress Swamps.
* **Geothermal & Historical Origin:** When the Vreken progenitors extorted Aethil for Blight magic, the Father cursed them with decay. Trying to break the curse, the Vreken slaughtered their nobility early in history — snapping a Blood Seal. Aex's aegis cracked beneath Bryngloom, erupting boiling sulfur springs, steaming bogs and volcanic vents that submerged their ancient stone spires into dark bogs. The Sleeping Soul's loom grew the forest; Predatory Wyrd now rots it.
* **Dominant Subraces:** **Vreken** (amber-eyed, long-eared crypt keepers), **Florae** (memory-wiped treant beings — the bloodline that refused the Whisper, saved by the Sleeping Soul's grove-ward).
* **Major Factions:** The Vreken Crypt-Guild, Florae Root-Keepers, House Morrath remnants — forebears of the Masked Acolytes who tend the Counterfeit.

---

## 2. SUBREGION BREAKDOWN
1. **Sunken Spires** — Drowned stone spires and ancient Vreken crypt cities.
2. **Blightmire Hollows** — Boiling sulfur bogs and Blight-corrupted hollows where the aegis is thinnest.
3. **Rotting Canopy** — Massive cypress canopy towers inhabited by swamp hunters.
4. **Morrath Ruins** — The Counterfeit Monolith — a Predatory Wyrd forgery standing in the place of the seal that was never written, erected by the Masked Acolytes' forebears to whisper for Keth Amar.
5. **Veilwood Thickets** — Protected sacred groves where the Florae slumber, warded by the Sleeping Soul of Mythrill.
6. **Shadowfen Bogs** — Lawless deep marshes wracked by Predatory Wyrd mist.
