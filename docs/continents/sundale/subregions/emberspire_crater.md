# SUBREGION: EMBERSPIRE CRATER
### Continent: Sundale

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
The fiery central volcanic caldera of Sundale. Plunging magma rifts, roaring sulfur geysers, and ash clouds dominate the landscape. Directly below lies Sol's protective casing.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Solari:** Ashen-skinned, black-eyed subterranean humanoids who guard Sol's breath.
* **Pyrofiend Mages:** Mages who channel Scathrach's vengeful Ember pacts.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **EmberSpire Citadel:** Monolithic basalt fortress built into the inner rim of the primary volcano crater.
* **The Vent of Scathrach:** Deepest volcanic rift where a corrupted fragment of Aex's hide resides, sealed from within.
* **The Sol-Forge:** Legendary magma-forge where Solari armorers craft weapons from ember-steel.
