# SUBREGION: ASHFALL BASIN
### Continent: Sundale

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
Surrounding Emberspire Crater, Ashfall Basin is a vast desert floor covered in black volcanic ash. Thermal updrafts create frequent ash-storms.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Subterranean Solari Clans:** Cave-dwelling Solari nomads.
* **Ashen Traders:** Human merchants operating caravans across the ash dunes.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **Subterranean City of Ashen-Vault:** A massive underground cavern city where Solari preserve ancient Ember lore.
* **Dust-Merchant Encampments:** Fortified trade posts built on basalt pillars above ash flows.
