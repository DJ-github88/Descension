# SUBREGION: SUNSCAR WASTES
### Continent: Sundale

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
A region of fused fulgurite glass dunes and desert canyons created by historical solar eruptions. The air shimmers with residual thermal magic.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Sunscar Nomads:** Hardy desert dwellers who harvest glass-crystals.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **The Glassed Plateau:** Miles of reflective fulgurite desert.
* **Sol's Fissure:** A glowing canyon where heat vents pulse in rhythm with Sol's dormant heart.
