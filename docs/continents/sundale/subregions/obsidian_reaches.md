# SUBREGION: OBSIDIAN REACHES
### Continent: Sundale

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
The coastal margin of Sundale. Black obsidian cliffs drop into boiling coastal waters where lava meets the sea.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Solari Coast-Guardians:** Naval warriors patrolling thermal sea-lanes.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **Black-Glass Harbor:** Port city carved directly into basalt sea-cliffs.
* **Lava-Fall Straits:** Spectacular coastal gulf where molten rock flows into the ocean.
