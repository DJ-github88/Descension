# SUNDALE — OVERARCHING LORE

## 1. CONTINENT OVERVIEW & BIOME
* **Biome:** Volcanic Ash-Deserts, Basalt Canyons & Magma Rifts.
* **Geothermal Heart of Mythrill:** Sundale lies directly above Sol's buried thermal shell. When Keth Amar devoured the heirs at Blizzard's End, Sol's uncontained heat eruptively vented through Sundale's core, creating active volcanoes, molten rifts, and obsidian desert plateaus.
* **Dominant Subraces:** **Solari** (ash-skinned subterranean guardians granted Ember magic), **Ashen Humans**.
* **Major Factions:** The Ember Vanguard, Order of the Sol-Blade, Scathrach Cultists.

---

## 2. SUBREGION BREAKDOWN
1. **Emberspire Crater** — The central active volcanic caldera where Sol's heat vents strongest.
2. **Ashfall Basin** — The black ash deserts and subterranean Solari cities.
3. **Sunscar Wastes** — The glassed desert wastes created by historical solar vents.
4. **Obsidian Reaches** — Jagged basalt coastal cliffs and lava-forged harbors.
