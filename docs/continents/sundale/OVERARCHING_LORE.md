# SUNDALE — OVERARCHING LORE

## 1. CONTINENT OVERVIEW & BIOME
* **Biome:** Volcanic Ash-Deserts, Basalt Canyons & Magma Rifts.
* **Geothermal Heart of Mythrill:** Sundale lies directly above the deepest wound in Aex's aegis. A Blood Seal snapped here early in the Slow Cracks — a severed lineage cracking the Mother's ward — and when the Blind Strike (Year 325) fractured the continental ward outright, Aex's broken aegis rained shards thickest through the core. That is why the continent is volcanoes, molten rifts and obsidian desert plateaus: it is the world-heart volcano breathing through a wound that never healed. Predatory Wyrd seeps heaviest where the aegis is thinnest.
* **Dominant Subraces:** **Solari** (ash-skinned subterranean guardians granted Ember magic through the Bloodline Pacts), **Ashen Humans**.
* **Major Factions:** The Ember Vanguard, Order of the Sol-Blade, Scathrach Cultists — and the Masked Acolytes, who hunt the shards that fell here as fallen pieces of the Mother.

---

## 2. SUBREGION BREAKDOWN
1. **Emberspire Crater** — The central active volcanic caldera where Sol's heat vents strongest: the world-heart's open wound.
2. **Ashfall Basin** — The black ash deserts and subterranean Solari cities built in the aegis's shadow.
3. **Sunscar Wastes** — The glassed desert wastes created when the Blind Strike's shard-rain vitrified the earth.
4. **Obsidian Reaches** — Jagged basalt coastal cliffs and lava-forged harbors, veined with cooling Aex Shards.
