# FROSTWOOD REACH — OVERARCHING LORE

## 1. CONTINENT OVERVIEW & BIOME
* **Biome:** Geothermally Warmed Pine Forest & Mist-Shrouded Wetlands.
* **Geothermal Origin:** When the Frostwood noble house attempted the subterranean "Artificial Sun" experiment and sacrificed their royal kin, the matrix collapsed into a thermal meltdown. This obliterated the royal lineage and cracked Aex's shell beneath Frostwood Reach, flooding the continent with geothermal hot springs, steam vents, and warm pine marshes despite its northern location.
* **Dominant Subraces:** **Thalren Humans** (archivists, mist-dwellers), **House of Viridane** (refugees who fled Cragjaw Peaks and uncovered the Aex crack truth), **Arch Mimir** (canopy aristocrats).
* **Major Factions:** The Sovereign Scribe-Ledger, House of Viridane Wardens, Mist-Sentinel Rangers.

---

## 2. SUBREGION BREAKDOWN
1. **The Ironheart Vales** — The warm south-central political heart and archivist stronghold.
2. **The Frostfang Wastes** — The sub-arctic northern granite tundra where geothermal heat thins out.
3. **The Drowned Fens** — The eastern misty marshlands, lawless bog sanctuaries, and ancient ruins.
