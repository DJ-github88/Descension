# FROSTWOOD REACH — OVERARCHING LORE

## 1. CONTINENT OVERVIEW & BIOME
* **Biome:** Geothermally Warmed Pine Forest & Mist-Shrouded Wetlands.
* **Geothermal Origin:** When the Frostwood noble house attempted the subterranean "Artificial Sun" experiment and sacrificed their royal kin, the Blood Seal snapped and the matrix collapsed into a thermal meltdown. The royal lineage was obliterated and Aex's aegis cracked beneath Frostwood Reach, flooding the continent with geothermal hot springs, steam vents and warm pine marshes despite its northern latitude. That crack was one of the first to teach the world what a severed lineage costs.
* **Dominant Subraces:** **Thalren Humans** (archivists, mist-dwellers), **House of Viridane** (refugees who fled Cragjaw Peaks after uncovering that a dead lineage cracks the aegis), **Arch Mimir** (canopy aristocrats) — all peoples the Natural Wyrd grew and Predatory Wyrd now wears.
* **Major Factions:** The Sovereign Scribe-Ledger, House of Viridane Wardens, Mist-Sentinel Rangers, and — in the places the fog hides — agents of the Unwritten.

---

## 2. SUBREGION BREAKDOWN
1. **The Ironheart Vales** — The warm south-central political heart and archivist stronghold, where the first crack still steams.
2. **The Frostfang Wastes** — The sub-arctic northern granite tundra where geothermal heat and Predatory Wyrd both thin out.
3. **The Drowned Fens** — The eastern misty marshlands, lawless bog sanctuaries and ancient ruins where the Sleeping Soul's loom is thinnest.
