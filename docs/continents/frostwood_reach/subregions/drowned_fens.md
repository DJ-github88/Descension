# SUBREGION: THE DROWNED FENS
### Continent: Frostwood Reach

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
The eastern lawless marshland of Frostwood Reach. Warm, hazy, and peat-choked, where geothermal steam mixes with thick marsh gas. Maps of the Fens fail constantly due to shifting peat mounds.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Fractured Mimir:** Maskless outcasts carrying glowing Mote lamps.
* **The Forgotten:** Undocumented human outcasts erased from the Sovereign Ledger.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **Wraithfen:** Haunted marsh where Fractured Mimir wander in endless memory loops.
* **The Shifting Fen:** A bog plateau that rearranges its topography during high mist tides.
* **Mistbarrow:** Pre-Thalreth burial mound generating its own micro-weather fog.
* **Thorn-Refuge Copse:** Hidden stilt-settlement of escaped outcasts.
