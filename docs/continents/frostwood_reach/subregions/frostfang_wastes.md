# SUBREGION: THE FROSTFANG WASTES
### Continent: Frostwood Reach

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
Located along the northern edge of Frostwood Reach, the Frostfang Wastes mark the boundary where geothermal heat vents fade into arctic granite tundra. Characterized by glacial moraines, sparse twisted ironwoods, and biting winds.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Stone-Tribal Humans:** Frontier hunters adapting to harsh sub-arctic terrain.
* **Skald Expatriates:** Garrisoned Northwatch soldiers and monolith Stone-Speakers.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS
* **Grevtholm Keep:** Fortified granite stronghold standing guard against northern waste threats.
* **Bearsback Summit:** Double granite mountain peak sacred to ancient stone hunters.
* **Iron Lake:** Frozen glacial basin concealing drowned pre-Binding ruins.
* **Stonespeakers' Camp:** Tents of outcast archivists preserving pre-Binding rune tablets.
