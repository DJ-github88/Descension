# SUBREGION: THE IRONHEART VALES
### Continent: Frostwood Reach

## 1. OVERVIEW & CLIMATE
The Ironheart Vales form the political and cultural core of Frostwood Reach. Warmed by subterranean steam vents from the ancient Artificial Sun meltdown, the vales feature petrified ironwood canopies, damp peat bogs, and thermal silt-rivers wrapped in perpetual white fog.

## 2. POPULATION & SUBRACES
* **Thalren Humans:** Ruling archivist caste obsessed with the Sovereign Ledger.
* **House of Viridane:** Descendants of the refugees who fled Cragjaw Peaks; guardians of the secret Aex crack truth.
* **Arch Mimir:** Canopy aristocrats wearing ancestral porcelain masks.

## 3. KEY LOCATIONS & LANDMARKS

### Major Cities & Strongholds
1. **Greymark Keep (Capital):** Built around the High Hearth's 400-year geothermal flame. Seat of the Sovereign Ledger and Jarl-Archivist Kaelen.
2. **Scribes' Tower:** A hollowed petrified ironwood monolith containing centuries of historical rolls and census logs.
3. **Viridane Sanctuary Grove:** A moonlit grove where Viridane scholars preserve forbidden rune-lore.

### Minor Settlements & Outposts
* **Skald's Landing:** Riverine trade post connecting Frostwood to northern sea routes.
* **Greythorn Copse:** Joint Thalren-Viridane defensive palisade along the southern pass.
* **Bog-Cranberry Stands:** Peat-harvesting hamlet built on thermal stilts.

### Natural & Geothermal Landmarks
* **The Ironwood Heart:** A massive glowing petrified ironwood tree rooted in a geothermal lake.
* **The Velling Pass:** The steaming mountain fissure leading south toward Sundale.
* **Bramble Heath:** Year-round crimson thorn-heath fed by hot mineral springs.

## 4. LOCAL WYRD TRADITION & BEASTS
* **Wyrd Manifestations:** **Gref** (face-stealer phantoms), **Gambrel** (broken-oath bog horrors).
* **Local Wildlife:** Silt-leeches, mist-sharks, thermal peat-elk.
