2026-10-22 · **Historical preparation, superseded by the authorized [A5 frozen contract](MYTHRILL_PROJECT_14_A5_CONTRACT.md).**
Main HQ explicitly selected owner acceptance for healing and authorized this bounded implementation.
The questions below are retained as preparation history, not current blockers.
Predecessor: [A4 exact creature source](MYTHRILL_PROJECT_14_A4_SOURCE_AUTHORITY_CONTRACT.md).
Existing target authority: [P12B implementation](MYTHRILL_PROJECT_12B_IMPLEMENTATION.md).

## Smallest proposed extension

Keep A4's current authenticated GM, exact placed creature incarnation, whole association and immutable
admitted version, explicit AP/mana price, one retained formula roll and one target on the same map.
Replace only the target subset with one current primary owner-PC attachment. Self, areas, chains,
conditions, reactions, recurring outcomes, equipment costs and new economies remain outside it.

**Damage:** reuse the existing P12B owner block offer/approval/declaration/piece/wear interface. A4
must not launch damage into a PC merely because the GM controls its source. The GM declares the hit
and its applicable damage/defense ruling, offers the exact action to the target owner, and the owner
records their block/no-block choice under existing timing/declaration rules. No synthesized consent.

**Healing:** use a separately named target acceptance branch. Equipment blocking, DR and wear do not
apply to healing. The target-consent policy below must be decided before this branch can execute.

## Identities and authority to freeze before implementation

- Source: A4 room/map/token/incarnation/revision, association hash, source-admission request ID,
  installed definition UUID/version/hash and current GM UID. No unrelated class binding or fake owner.
- Target: P7 room/map/token/incarnation/revision plus owner UID, canonical character ID, primary
  attachment, current room membership/session and verified ownership. Retired/replaced attachments or
  owner changes require a fresh exact owner decision; no redirection by character/template name.
- Offer: one immutable A4 attempt/roll/price and reserved command identity, plus GM hit/defense ruling
  and exact PC. Target approval binds the declaration revision, chosen available piece/no-block,
  retained face/die, exact offer hash and owner identity. Use P12B's proven sibling/carry-forward rules
  only where ledger evidence establishes that they apply; otherwise explicit retained-face revalidation.
- Current authority, non-revoked version/association, exact actors, Stunned, resources, target owner,
  declaration/piece and revisions recheck after awaits and at the final shared P7 stage.

## Final damage, payment and equipment conservation

Keep **GM pre-block adjudicated damage**, **equipment block reduction**, and **GM-confirmed final HP
damage** separately labelled and bound. Never subtract equipment DR twice or reinterpret a recorded
face. Existing Defending doubles reduction and prevents wear; armor wears on face1/2, shield on1;
wear reads the original face. Critical/nonphysical applicability retains the existing explicit GM
ruling/manual boundary and does not infer an automatic mitigation order.

One admitted P7 source+target consequence must install the exact A4 AP/mana payment, PC HP/tempHP,
applicable P12B equipment wear and receipt/approval consumption synchronously. No2-AP basic-strike
substitution, no source prepayment, no partial block wear on source refusal, no target HP without
source price. Temp-first payment/damage and healing cap remain shared. Explicit accepted amount0 may
pay the authored price; decline/cancel/unexecuted work spends nothing under current semantics.

Healing installs source payment and capped PC HP together, with no equipment entry. If damage and
healing require different target-consent workflows, keep those closed branches explicit; do not make
one generic bypass of the existing owner block contract.

## Custody and evidence gate

Use A4/P12B existing custody, not a third ledger. Retain source faces, target blocking faces, offer,
approval and command bytes before emission. Unknown/pruned outcomes block replacements. Close/reopen,
reload/rejoin, lost offer/approval/GM ACK and restart must recover exact status without automatic action;
known receipts are historical/silent. Changed admission/target/declaration needs an explicit transition
retaining compatible original evidence, and positive old-attempt closure. New epochs cannot silently
recreate old provenance. Distinguish live-only temporary rooms and writer-backed checkpoints.

Focused acceptance should cover real GM+owner+observer damage/block/no-block/wear, authored prices and
zeros, Stunned/owner replacement/stale source and target, competing spend, writer refusal, retained
revalidation, exact recovery and visibility. Healing gets its own positive/negative owner-policy cases.
Carry A4 creature→creature and P12B acceptance forward; no historical re-audit.

## Genuine decisions remaining

1. **Healing target acceptance:** must the exact PC owner explicitly accept healing, or may the current
   GM install an explicitly adjudicated healing result without a separate owner offer? Until decided,
   creature→PC healing remains unsupported. No blocking/wear is involved in either policy.
2. **Authored damage applicability:** select the first admitted damage subset and confirm which existing
   P12B explicit damage-type/critical ruling applies. Existing global mitigation-order/armor-versus-
   nonphysical open rules stay GM-adjudicated; this proposal does not settle them by implementation.

Target ownership and consent are authority requirements, not new gameplay benefits. No class roster,
heritage loadout, equipment economy, migration or deployment is part of this candidate.
