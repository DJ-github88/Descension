## 🎨 Golden Visual Rules & Prompt Directives

All concept art prompts in this compendium adhere strictly to two cardinal design systems:

### 1. 🏛️ Deep-Focus Architectural & Civic Life Prompts
* **Camera Composition**: Wide-angle interior or street-level thoroughfares in **FULL CRISP FOCUS**.
* **Zero Foreground Blockers**: Strict prohibition against giant foreground portraits or characters standing on balconies blocking 70% of the canvas. The architecture itself is the hero.
* **The 5 Vital City Elements**:
  1. *Traversal & Transit*: Bridges, flumes, aqueducts, zero-g currents, ropeways, elevators, canals, and stone stairways.
  2. *Commerce & Markets*: Open-air bazaars, trading booths, kiosks, merchant stalls, weighing scales, and cargo crates.
  3. *Harvesting & Industry*: Sluices, mines, aquaculture, kelp drying, blast furnaces, timber yards, and net mending.
  4. *Study, Mysticism & Governance*: Libraries, observatories, listening chambers, council rotundas, and shrines.
  5. *Stuff They Use*: Tools, tridents, sextants, astrolabes, calipers, balance scales, tuning forks, amphoras, and wicker creels.
* **In-Scale Citizens**: Citizens, guards, merchants, and mystics are rendered in natural human-to-building proportions bustling throughout the scene.

### 2. 👤 Heroic Single-Figure Character Sheet Prompts
* **Single Figure Rule**: Exactly one single heroic figure per prompt (NO turnarounds, NO dual front/back views, NO multiple characters).
* **Seen in Their Element**: Figures are rendered actively immersed in their authentic cultural habitat or working station (e.g., at an anvil, on a tossing deck, wading in rapids, surrounded by library stacks, perched in an observatory dome) rather than standing on an empty canvas.
* **Crude Atmospheric Sketch Scenery**: Background elements are drawn in the exact matching 1995 Samwise Didier draft style (rough charcoal/graphite linework, loose architectural drafts, gritty cross-hatching, natural sketchbook vignetting) to ground the character in their world.
* **1995 Samwise Didier Sketch Style (Always Be Chunky - ABC)**: Rough black charcoal and graphite draft linework with gritty, expressive cross-hatching isolated on clean off-white watercolor sketchbook paper.
* **Single Explosive Watercolor Splash**: The subject, clothing, gear, and sketched scenery are monochromatic; the ONLY color on the entire canvas is the signature race watercolor splash blooming dynamically behind the figure, tinting the linework and environment.
* **Zero Text**: Strictly no letters, words, labels, signatures, or watermarks.

### 3. 🔍 Standalone Self-Contained Clarity (Zero External Image Dependency)
* **Zero Filename References in Prompts**: Prompts must NEVER include local file names (e.g. `icon.png`, `benchmark.jpg`) or shorthand phrases like `(exact match to canonical benchmark...)`. The AI image generator has no access to local files and will hallucinate or waste tokens.
* **Full Verbal Anatomical & Architectural Specification**: Every prompt must be 100% self-contained, describing head-to-toe anatomy, exact facial features, skin tone/texture, costume layers, tools, and background architecture explicitly in plain language.
* **Aggressive Negative Guardrails for Non-Humanoid Races**: When generating humanoid subraces with elemental or aquatic traits (e.g. Riverlings, Florae, Vreken), always explicitly negate unwanted creature tropes: `STRICTLY NO LIZARDS, NO REPTILES, NO FROG-MEN, NO MURLOCS, NO AMPHIBIOUS BEASTS, NO ANIMAL SNOUTS. ALL CHARACTERS ARE DISTINCTLY ATTRACTIVE ATHLETIC HUMANOIDS WITH HUMAN FACES.`

1. **Copy the Prompt Verbatim**: Each block is enclosed in standard text formatting with exact negative constraints, architectural priorities, and artistic styles.
2. **Aspect Ratio Recommendations**:
   - **Architectural Cityscapes & Locations**: Use `16:9` or `3:2` for panoramic, deep-focus perspective.
   - **Heroic Character Sheet Concepts**: Use `3:4` or `9:16` for full-length vertical character standing art.
3. **Consistency**: Notice that each subrace prompt strictly references the approved canonical benchmark icons (`vtt-react/public/assets/images/races/`) and established lore from `vtt-react/src/data/races/`.
