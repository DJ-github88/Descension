# MYTHRILL: CANONICAL RACE & SUBRACE CODEX

> The single source of truth for all race lore, subrace origins, and cultural backgrounds in Mythrill.

---

# 1. THE COSMIC FRAMEWORK

* **Sol:** Infant unhatched sun encased in a protective thermal shell deep within Mythrill's volcanic core.
* **Aex & Aethil:** Celestial Mother & Father Deities. Forfeited Celestial status upon divine descent. Aex wrapped her celestial body around Sol. During the Blizzard's End massacre, her body shattered into **Aex Shards** raining across all continents.
* **The Sister Moon:** Slumbering moon frozen solid in orbit to act as a thermal buffer, anchoring **Rime Magic**.
* **Keth Amar:** Cosmic predator who devoured the 3 House heirs at Blizzard's End, was injured by Aex's core shield, and now drapes Mythrill in **Wyrd Magic**.
* **The 7th House (Masked Acolytes):** Secretly created by an **Unknown Dominator Deity** seeking world domination. Forged a **7th Counterfeit Monolith** to fool Keth Amar. Capital at *Vespera's Crown*. Playable origin carrying the **Watcher's Spark** (BG3-style Entity Watcher).
* **Aex Cracks & Geothermal Topography:** Lineage deaths (Vreken Bryngloom massacre, Ordan assassin purge, Frostwood Artificial Sun meltdown) cracked Aex's shell, creating regional hot springs, volcanic bogs, and thermal pine wetlands.

---

# 2. CANONICAL RACE & SUBRACE PROFILES

### 1. SOLARI (Former Humans — Ember Magic)
* **Origins:** Former mortal Human House granted Ember magic by Aex & Aethil. Their royal heirs were devoured by Keth Amar at Blizzard's End.
* **Anatomy & Culture:** Subterranean, ash-skinned, black-eyed nomads who keep Sol's volcanic hearth alive.
* **Parenting Philosophy:** *Heat is life; cold is death.* Children are raised in thermal caverns and forged in discipline.

---

### 2. VREKEN (Former Humans — Blight Magic)
* **Origins:** Former mortal Human House that extorted Aethil for Blight magic and was cursed with decay. In a desperate attempt to break the curse, they slaughtered their own nobility early in history, cracking Aex beneath Bryngloom.
* **Anatomy & Culture:** Long-eared, amber-eyed crypt keepers dwelling in Bryngloom's sunken spires.
* **Subraces:** Crypt-Guardians, Blight-Weavers, Sunken Scribes.

---

### 3. FLORAE (Original 7th House — Nature Magic)
* **Origins:** The original 7th Human House saved by a Native Nature Deity after discovering that dying heirs crack Aex rather than healing Sol.
* **Anatomy & Culture:** Treant-like humanoids with erased memories bound into ancient oak and ironwood bark.
* **Traits:** Memory-wiped, pristine nature guardians immune to Keth Amar's Wyrd mental probing.

---

### 4. ASTRIL (Star-Refugees)
* **Origins:** Cosmic star-refugees fleeing their destroyed homeworld, carrying advanced star-technology and innate **Sacred Magic**.
* **Subraces:**
  1. **Stargazer Astril:** Peaceful cosmic scholars studying stars and void constellations.
  2. **Brutish Astril:** Warlike conquerors wielding dark starlight weaponry who ordered the Ordan Purge.

---

### 5. HUMANS (5 Regional Subraces)
1. **Thalren Humans:** Dwellers of Frostwood Reach pine wetlands; archivists of the Sovereign Ledger.
2. **Skald Humans:** Northland tundra warriors of Nordhalla who honor endurance as religion.
3. **Tessen Humans:** Mountaineers and stone-sentinels of Cragjaw Peaks.
4. **Merryn Humans:** Seafarers and naval trade barons of the Iceheart Sea.
5. **Ordan Humans:** Thought extinct after the Astril-ordered purge by the Ordan Assassin ("Itachi"); surviving Ordan live in secret **disguised as ordinary humans**.

---

### 6. MASKED ACOLYTES (7th House — Playable Entity Watcher)
* **Origins:** Common folk gathered from minor settlements under dark hoods by the Unknown Dominator Deity.
* **Capital & Culture:** Capital at *Vespera's Crown*. Proselytizers and odd-jobbers roaming Mythrill.
* **Playable Origin (The Entity's Watcher):** Characters carrying the **Watcher's Spark**—their god sees through their eyes while the character maintains free will and receives cryptic divine perks.

---

### 7. GROVEN (Primordial Earth Humanoids)
* Bound to Primordial Earth Native Deities; stone-skinned mountain artisans and warriors.

---

### 8. MYRATHIL (Aquatic Humanoids)
* Born of the Deity of Waters; 3 variants: Open Sea, Coastal Reef, and Abyssal Deep.

---

### 9. MIMIR (Sculptor God Humanoids)
* Mask-wearing humanoids created by an insecure Sculptor God who endlessly altered their faces.

---

### 10. NETH (Altered Humans)
* Mutated via secondary Native Deity contract to cure realm-wide infertility; Mythrill's elven equivalent.

---

### 11. FEXRIC (Gnomish/Goblinoid Tinkerers)
* Brilliant, unscrupulous alchemists and tinkerers of the deep reaches.
