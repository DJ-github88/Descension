# MYTHRILL: CANONICAL RACE & SUBRACE CODEX

> The single source of truth for all race lore, subrace origins, and cultural backgrounds in Mythrill.
> **Canonical Status:** Ratified Master Lore Update (475-Year Master Timeline).

---

# 1. THE COSMIC FRAMEWORK & ANCESTRY ORIGINS

* **Pre-Star Mythrill (The Sleeping Soul & Natural Wyrd):** Mythrill is the loom of the Sleeping Soul. **Natural Wyrd** — collective belief, folklore, myth, fear and vow manifest into creatures — grew the native beasts (*Jutul*, *Glacier Wyrms*, *Thrumm*), land spirits (*Vaettir*, *Fossegrim*) and non-human peoples. Its hostile strain is **Predatory Wyrd**, Keth Amar's infection of that loom. Mortals had **zero magic** — they were underdogs who survived via forged steel, black powder, matchlocks, archery, tactical engineering, and folk taboos.
* **The Celestial Star-Fall and the Bloodline Pacts (Year 0):** Aex (the Mother) and Aethil (the Father) fled across the cosmos to hide their living, unhatched infant sun (*Sol*) in Mythrill's molten core from *Keth Amar*. Aethil anchored the celestial barrier around the atmosphere; Aex entombed Sol in the core with cosmic wards and **Bloodline Pacts**. Each pact is a **Blood Seal**. The Sister Moon (**Selunis**) — the never-quickened moon-daughter egg — was frozen in orbit as a thermal buffer (anchoring Rime Magic); Keth Amar nested in her shell during its approach.
* **The Bound:** Ancient mortal lineages who swore the Bloodline Pacts mutated into the Bound (*Solari*, *Vreken*, etc.); common folk received subtle physical adaptations while retaining traditional black-powder, cold-iron traps and melee tactics.
* **The Whispered Purge and the Blind Strike (Years 300–325):** Unable to breach Aethil's ward, Keth Amar spewed Predatory Wyrd onto the world (Year 300) and orchestrated the Whispered Purge — twenty-five years posing as Aethil to snap the Blood Seals. In Year 325 the **Blind Strike** (mortal name: Blizzard's End) crashed into the planetary ward. The **Secret Aegis** — the hidden lineage that erased itself from history — held; Keth Amar was wounded and repelled into the void. The impact fractured Aex's aegis into glowing **Aex Shards** — the seven greatest are the **Sundered Monoliths**, one of which is the Predatory Wyrd **Counterfeit**. Present day (Year 475) is the Freezing Era — cold because Sol is entombed in the core.
* **The 7th House (Masked Acolytes):** Keth Amar's whisper-cult, keepers of the Counterfeit Monolith. Capital at *Vespera's Crown*. Playable origin carrying the **Hollow Sight** — a Predatory Wyrd counterfeit of Morvane's true sight.

# 2. CANONICAL RACE & SUBRACE PROFILES

### 1. THE NETH (The Pitcher of Greed & Youth)
* **Origins:** Predates the Celestial Gods. Facing extinction due to a fading birth rate, they were guided by a forest spirit in Bryngloom Forest to a sacred **Well of Life**. Their subraces reflect how their ancestors approached the waters:
  * **High Neth (The Half-Filled Pitcher):** Followed instructions by filling the pitcher halfway. Became pristine, ethereal, but slightly frail, immortal-adjacent beings. They cherish children above all else, operating with intense, protective "helicopter parenting" rooted in the belief that youth holds infinite potential (Peter Pan philosophy).
  * **Pale Neth (The Stolen Urns):** Hoarded massive jars out of panic, over-saturating themselves into non-breathing, wraith-like subterranean hoarders.
  * **Hallowed / Rot-Bound Neth (The Well-Dippers):** Dove directly into the Well out of greed/panic. The raw power burned away their living vitality, turning them into true undead husks bound to the Old God's roots.

---

### 2. THE FEXRICK (Tinkers Who Dug Too Deep)
* **Origins:** Gnomish/Dwarvish master engineers of Cragjaw Peaks who relied on black powder, steam valves, and gear-work rather than magic.
* **The Incident:** Dug too deep in the mountains and uncovered an ancient Primordial God's vault/engine. They stole from it and tapped into its core to power their technology, cursing their bloodline with erratic twitching, manic paranoia, and an insatiable urge to build, tweak, and tinker.

---

### 3. SOLARI (Former Humans — Ember Magic)
* **Origins:** Former mortal Human High House granted Ember magic by Aex & Aethil. Their royal heirs were devoured by Keth Amar at Blizzard's End.
* **Anatomy & Culture:** Subterranean, ash-skinned, black-eyed nomads who keep Sol's volcanic hearth alive.
* **Parenting Philosophy:** *Heat is life; cold is death.* Children are raised in thermal caverns and forged in discipline.

---

### 4. VREKEN (Former Humans — Blight Magic)
* **Origins:** Former mortal Human High House that extorted divine desperation for ultimate power, receiving **Blight Magic**. In a desperate attempt to break the curse of decay, they slaughtered their own nobility early in history, cracking Aex beneath Bryngloom and creating sulfurous bogs.
* **Anatomy & Culture:** Long-eared, amber-eyed crypt keepers dwelling in Bryngloom's sunken spires.
* **Subraces:** Crypt-Guardians, Blight-Weavers, Sunken Scribes.

---

### 5. FLORAE (Original 7th House — Nature Magic)
* **Origins:** The original 7th Human House saved by a Native Nature Deity after discovering that dying heirs crack Aex rather than healing Sol.
* **Anatomy & Culture:** Treant-like humanoids with erased memories bound into ancient oak and ironwood bark.
* **Traits:** Memory-wiped, pristine nature guardians immune to Keth Amar's Wyrd mental probing.

---

### 6. ASTRIL (Star-Refugees)
* **Origins:** Cosmic star-refugees fleeing their destroyed homeworld, carrying advanced star-technology and innate **Sacred Magic**.
* **Subraces:**
  1. **Stargazer Astril:** Peaceful cosmic scholars studying stars and void constellations.
  2. **Brutish Astril:** Warlike conquerors wielding dark starlight weaponry who ordered the Ordan Purge.

---

### 7. HUMANS (5 Regional Subraces)
1. **Skald Humans (Nordhalla):** Northland warriors of the tundra. Commoners rely on matchlock firearms, black powder, cold-iron traps, and folk taboos to survive native beasts (*Jutul*, *Glacier Wyrms*, *Vaettir*, *Qalupalik*), while Infused House leaders bring celestial Rime magic to the shield-wall.
2. **Thalren Humans (Frostwood Reach):** Dwellers of pine wetlands; archivists of the Sovereign Ledger.
3. **Tessen Humans (Cragjaw Peaks):** Mountaineers and stone-sentinels of Cragjaw Peaks.
4. **Merryn Humans (Iceheart Sea):** Seafarers and naval trade barons navigating churning, storm-tossed seas.
5. **Ordan Humans (Sundrift Vale):** Thought extinct after the Astril-ordered purge; surviving Ordan live in secret **disguised as ordinary humans**.

---

### 8. MASKED ACOLYTES (7th House — Playable Entity Watcher)
* **Origins:** Common folk gathered from minor settlements under dark hoods by the Unknown Dominator Deity.
* **Capital & Culture:** Capital at *Vespera's Crown*. Proselytizers and odd-jobbers roaming Mythrill.
* **Playable Origin (The Entity's Watcher):** Characters carrying the **Watcher's Spark**—their god sees through their eyes while the character maintains free will and receives cryptic divine perks.

---

### 9. GROVEN (Primordial Earth Humanoids)
* Bound to Primordial Earth Native Deities; stone-skinned mountain artisans and warriors born of living stone.

---

### 10. MYRATHIL (Aquatic Humanoids)
* Born of the Deity of Waters; 3 variants: Open Sea, Coastal Reef, and Abyssal Deep.

---

### 11. MIMIR (Sculptor God Humanoids)
* Mask-wearing humanoids created by an insecure Sculptor God who endlessly altered their faces.
