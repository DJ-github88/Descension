## 3. Five Largest Remaining Capability Gaps

1. **Generic Buffs, Debuffs, Utility & Status Effects (vs. `authored-effect:v1`)**
   - *Gap:* The Wizard lets creators author full `buffConfig`, `debuffConfig`, `utilityConfig`, `purificationConfig`, `restorationConfig`, `transformationConfig`, and `statusEffectsConfig`, but `authoredAbility.js` refuses all of them. Only the 5 narrow `authored-effect:v1` sub-profiles (`stunned`, `movement`, `attribute`, `damageReduction`, `periodic`) execute.
   - *Concrete example:* Authoring a standard Wizard `"Shield of Faith"` (`effectTypes: ['buff']`, `+2 AC` or `+10 temp HP`) or `"Cleanse"` (`effectTypes: ['purification']`) saves and reopens in the Wizard, but refuses at compilation (`Exactly one damage or healing effect required`).

2. **Expanded Direct-Effect Saves Beyond Single-Part Direct Damage (D-SV2 / D-SV4)**
   - *Gap:* Single-part Direct Damage saves (`halves` `floor(final/2)` D-SV1 and `negates` `paid-at-cast` D-SV3) and GM-recorded `negates` saves on `movement`/`stunned` riders are supported. However, D-SV2/D-SV4 combinations (damage save next to a rider/periodic/another part), saves on healing or area spells, scaled/deterministic/missing-health formulas with direct saves, cooldowns or self targets with direct saves, saves on `periodic` or `damageReduction`, and automated VTT save rolling remain refused.
   - *Concrete example:* Authoring `"Ember Bolt + Movement Rider"` with both a direct-damage save and a rider save (`unsupported-combination-both-saves`) refuses whole (`only a damage-only single-target immediate spell reads it`).

3. **Area Shapes & Multi-Target Geometry Beyond Bounded Ranged Circle**
   - *Gap:* Only static ranged `circle` (5–60 ft, 1–8 creature targets, shared owner roll) is installed (`authored-area:v1`). Every other Wizard targeting mode (`multi`, `chain`, `cone`, `line`, `square`, `cube`, `sphere`, `cylinder`, `wall`, `trail`, `follows_caster`, `movable`) and any area spell with riders, saves, or PC targets is refused.
   - *Concrete example:* Authoring a `30 ft cone` breath weapon or a `line` lightning bolt, or adding a `Hobbled` movement rider to a `15 ft circle` frost blast, refuses at `definitionFromWizard`.

4. **Generic Passives, Spell-Driven Summoning, and Non-Immediate Spell Types (`REACTION`, `CHANNELED`, `TRAP`, `ZONE`, `STATE`)**
   - *Gap:* Only `spellType: 'ACTION'` + `castTimeType: 'IMMEDIATE'` compiles. Generic `PASSIVE` spells, `REACTION` / `CHANNELED` / `TRAP` / `ZONE` / `STATE` spells, and `summoningConfig` all refuse. Heightened Senses (`1c14d3d5...`) is a single hard-pinned descriptor, not a generic passive engine, and companion placement only moves already-recruited tokens outside combat.
   - *Concrete example:* Authoring a `"Summon Ember Wolf"` spell (`effectTypes: ['summoning']`) or a generic `"Aura of Warmth"` (`spellType: 'PASSIVE'`) saves in the Library but cannot compile, admit, or execute.

5. **Non-Follow-Up Triggers, Chaining, and Effect-Attached / Conditional Trigger Rules**
   - *Gap:* Out of ~70+ trigger types in `triggerData.js` and 6 trigger sections in Step 7, only 2 whole-spell follow-up cards (`follow_up_after_damage`, `follow_up_after_healing`) and 1 component-watch placement (`damage_direct` watching `follow_up_after_damage`) execute—and even their Spellbook registration UX, `TriggerOfferPanel` projection hardening, and dispatch selectivity are still **candidate-only** (not yet installed).
   - *Concrete example:* Authoring `"When below 50% HP, deal +1d6"` (`health_threshold` or `conditionalEffects`) or `"When Damage Taken, trigger Restoration"` (`buffDebuffTriggers`) is preserved in the draft and refused as manual play.

## 4. Plain-English Roadmap

1. **Land the queued trigger-UX & dispatch lanes first:** Install and App-verify the already-reviewed staged candidates (`TriggerOfferPanel` projection hardening → `spellbook-trigger-registration-workflow` → `trigger-dispatch-selectivity`), and run the already-prepared `healing-followup-app-witness` so the existing trigger engine is cleanly usable end-to-end.
2. **Unblock direct-damage/healing saves & `halves` rounding:** Record the creator decision on direct-part save scope and half-damage rounding (`saveBranch.js` / `authoredAbility.js`), which immediately unlocks classic save-for-half spells.
3. **Extend `authored-area:v1` to additional shapes & riders:** Add `cone` and `line` geometry profiles and allow `authored-effect:v1` riders on area spells once per-target vs. shared-roll and partial-commit rules are settled.
4. **Broaden `authored-effect:v1` toward common Wizard buffs/shields:** Add temporary HP shields (`hasShieldEffect`) and AC/attack modifiers to the canonical condition profile so standard Wizard `buff` and `debuff` spells don't require manual adjudication.
5. **Define creator rules for generic passives & spell-driven summoning:** Establish activation/cost/stacking rules for `PASSIVE` spells (beyond the single Heightened Senses descriptor) and ephemeral/combat-bound token lifecycle rules for `summoningConfig` (distinct from out-of-combat persistent companion placement).
