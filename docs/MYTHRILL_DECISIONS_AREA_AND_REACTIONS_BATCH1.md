# Area targeting and reaction policy: decision batch 1

2026-10-18 · **For creator decision. Nothing here is approved or implemented.** Sources: roadmap §6 (P13) and
§11, [MYTHRILL_NEXT_SLICES_CONTRACT_20261017.md](MYTHRILL_NEXT_SLICES_CONTRACT_20261017.md) §3.5 (G1–G6), published
Laws "Reaction Rules". These answers gate the
P12C-B2 area preview and the P13B reaction-window contract. Neither is authorized. Each decision is
independent, and each has a safe default that applies until it is answered.

Not asked again, because already confirmed in the published Laws (P10 "Reaction Rules"):
- a reaction names its moment and is usable only then, if payable then;
- you never have to react;
- an executed reaction pays its full listed price;
- follow-ups (Riposte, Shield Bash) belong to the character whose reaction succeeded;
- declarations are hidden from other players until resolution;
- the VTT window is eight seconds;
- no AC; authored costs stand.

Class reconstruction: the P13B contract will carry **typed class operations** (card escrow, thread token,
tile, order) for reaction payment, not a scalar cost. Class-specific reaction rules (for example Fate Weaver
link routing) wait for Class HeadQ's reviewed requirements; see

## A. Area targeting (gates the P12C-B2 preview; execution stays P14)

The preview only shows who looks inside. It never posts, rolls, spends or executes. Undecided cases show
"edge — GM decides".

### G1. Is the card range measured to the area's origin or to its nearest edge?

- **Example.** A 20-ft-radius circle on a card with range 60 ft. The caster places the centre 70 ft away, so
  its nearest edge is 50 ft away.
- **Origin (recommended).** Illegal at 70 ft. Easy to see and teach: "the point you choose must be in range".
- **Nearest edge.** Legal. Every area gains effective reach equal to its radius, so larger areas reach further.
- **Default until decided.** The preview measures to the origin and labels it "advisory — GM decides".

### G3. Does a creature count as inside when only part of it is covered?

- **Example.** A Large ogre covers 2×2 cells; one cell is inside the circle.
- **Any covered cell counts (recommended).** Matches how players read a template on a grid, and makes big
  creatures easier to hit.
- **Centre point only.** Simpler, but a Large creature can stand three-quarters inside and be missed.
- **More than half.** Needs counting partial cells, which is fiddly at the table.
- **Default until decided.** Partly covered creatures show as "edge — GM decides".

### G4. When a card lists no target restriction, does the area hit allies?

- **Example.** A storm circle covers two goblins and Ilse.
- **Yes, it hits everyone inside (recommended).** Honest to the card. The preview shows allies inside in a
  warning colour, so the caster sees the cost before committing.
- **Enemies only unless the card says otherwise.** Kinder, but it silently changes every area card without
  a restriction.
- **Default until decided.** Allies inside are highlighted, and the GM rules.

### G5. Where can an area's origin be placed?

- **Free point (recommended for circles and cones).** Matches how a template is placed by hand. Membership is
  computed from the exact shape.
- **Cell centre or grid intersection.** Makes membership unambiguous on a grid, but limits placement.
- **Default until decided.** Free placement; the preview shows the snapped alternative only if the GM turns
  it on.

**Deferred to a later batch:** G2 (do walls stop areas? Today the tool clips by the placer's sight) and G6
(elevation). Both stay "GM decides" in the preview.

## B. Reaction policy (gates the P13B contract)

Until decided, the window collects hidden declarations and the GM orders and charges them by hand.

### D13-1. What does a declared reaction cost if it never happens? (J4)

- **Example.** Ilse declares Parry against a goblin's swing. The goblin's attack is then cancelled by
  an ally's Interpose, so the Parry never resolves.
- **Nothing (recommended).** Only executed reactions pay. No reservation is lost. Players aren't punished for
  outcomes they couldn't see.
- **The full price.** Declaring is committing. This discourages "declare everything just in case".
- **Reserved, then released at window close.** Behaves like "nothing" (nothing is spent, so nothing is refunded), but the reserved amount can't be spent
  elsewhere in the same window. This matters for class resources: a reservation holds the exact typed item
  (card, token, tile) without spending it.
- **Default until decided.** Nothing is charged; the GM charges by hand if they rule otherwise.

### D13-2. Several people react to the same moment: in what order do they resolve?

- **Example.** The goblin attacks Ilse. Ilse declares Parry; Bram declares Interpose for her.
- **Redirects first, then defences, then everything else (recommended).** Interpose changes who is attacked
  before anyone defends. Ties within a group go to the defender's side first, then by initiative.
- **Initiative order.** Simple and table-familiar, but Ilse's Parry may resolve before Bram's Interpose moves
  the attack away from her.
- **GM orders each time.** Most flexible, and slowest.
- **Default until decided.** The GM orders. The window shows all declarations together, never in arrival
  order.

### D13-3. Can you react to an enemy's trigger during your own turn? (J5)

- **Example.** On Ilse's turn she moves away from an ogre. The ogre's Opportunity Attack triggers on her
  movement. Can Bram (not Ilse) Interpose? Can Ilse, mid-turn, Dodge that attack?
- **Yes, if the card's moment matches and you can pay (recommended).** Consistent with "each reaction names
  its moment". AP spent on reactions during your turn comes out of the same pool, so the trade-off stays.
- **No reactions on your own turn.** Simpler, but it makes Opportunity Attacks one-sided for the active
  character.
- **Default until decided.** The window opens for everyone whose card matches; the GM may disallow.

### D13-6. Can players pass explicitly, and can a window close early?

- **Example.** Only Ilse is eligible and she has decided not to react.
- **Yes: an explicit "Pass", and the window closes early when every eligible holder has declared or passed
  (recommended).** Keeps play moving; eight seconds stays the maximum, not a forced wait. Silence still counts
  as passing.
- **No Pass; always wait out the timer.** Uniform pacing, but slower, and the full wait can reveal that someone
  is thinking.
- **Default until decided.** A Pass button and early close.

**Deferred to a later batch:**
- D13-4 Raise Shield wording;
- D13-5 Parry compares face or total;
- D13-7 hidden declarations at a physical table;
- D13-8 Spell Reaction's 2 AP and Evade;
- the chain safety limits.

These need card wording or playtesting rather than a policy answer.
