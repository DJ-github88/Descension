## What is established

1. The current base catalogue is21 classes,1,026 unique authored spells and65 trees/1,219 nodes. Three Nereid aliases are compatibility views. Every registered tree has50 rank capacity and no missing local prerequisite reference; current spell pools have no missing/level-mismatched ID references. Functional behavior still needs review.
2. The heritage registry has25 records and102 native relationships; wrapped metadata is the current access authority over raw restrictions. Capability metadata/implemented flags do not prove all heritage effects execute.
3. Current pure P9 support evaluation admits **four spells**, not the whole catalogue: Marrow-Piercing Slug, Restorative Prayer, Opening Chord, Harmonic Strike. Spheres, notes and Devotion are the three supported class-payment domains. Affordability is a separate state decision; the level1 test fixture cannot yet pay the sphere recipe.
4. The pre-consolidation index registers30 classes. Old Gambler/Fate Weaver's63 spell IDs survive in Gambit. Old Huntress already requires a companion; old Deathcaller is HP/Blood Token/Ascension-heavy; old Exorcist is demon-binding; old Titan is calcification-heavy. Desired restored fantasies therefore require redesign, not archive restoration alone.
5. Magical damage tags in top-level damageConfig are strongly skewed: Arcane11, Primal12, Rime23 versus Blight115, Ember88, Storm73, Wyrd59 and Sacred38. These are per-spell/type occurrences, not damage magnitude, player-pick availability or actual execution. Arcanoneer remains an all-eight generalist.

## Strong proposed directions

- **Hunter** as one class, using a shared solo-viable quarry/evidence economy; companion specialization augments it without being a hidden baseline requirement. Preserve the old Quiet Trade and companion-bearing characters under compatibility/method migration.
- **Metamorph** as the transformation successor, with Shapeshifter a strong clear alternative. Anatomical slots and mutually exclusive capabilities better express adaptation than another build/vent bar. Groven mineral bodies and Mimir identity anchors require different cultural interpretation, not different unbalanced chassis.
- Restore **Gambler/Fate Weaver/Chaos Weaver** as wagers+escrow, reserved links and finite committed outcome distributions. Do not make all three Fortune/Debt variants.
- Restore **Deathcaller/Lichborne** as a named dead-entity command economy and finite-anchor self-undeath. Neither needs generic soul harvesting.
- Keep **Animist**, preferably narrowed to named ancestral presence/real memorial substrates; compare its two detailed revisions before moving inscriptions/medicine/nature into restored classes.
- Make **Augur/Oracle/Harbinger** earn distinct observed-sign, prepared-forecast and caused-calamity play. Rename Augur's Harbinger tree provisionally Ill Omen. Oracle still needs proof it is more than enhanced Ready.
- Investigate **Inscriptor, Titan, Exorcist, Covenbane, Witch Doctor, Primalist, Dreadnaught, Bladedancer and Monk** through complete independent dossiers. Familiar names are welcome; existence of old art/code is not enough.

## Highest-risk integration mistakes

Treating authored effects as executed; using local class normalizers as server validation; broadening pinned P9 shapes to admit custom spells accidentally; overwriting typed state through HUD direct-set; reroll/redraw on stale/unknown actions; copying all heritage natives to every successor; resetting class through infoSlice as a migration; duplicating summons on reload; pretending sequential per-target commits are atomic; erasing current fourth Martyr/Warden trees; losing resourceCost through export/template serialization.
