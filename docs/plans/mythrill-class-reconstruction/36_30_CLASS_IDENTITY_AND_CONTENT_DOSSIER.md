## Part 1: Phase 1 — Identity & Lore Reconciliation

### 1. Covenbane (`covenbane`)

#### A. Core Fantasy & Player-Facing Promise
The mutilated instrument of divine negation. An anti-sorcery tracker and executioner who carries cold bog-iron weapons, null-salts, and silver-calcified marrow to silence corrupt covens, sever hostile Wyrd channels, and shatter aberrant spellcraft.

#### B. Cultural & Historical Roots in Mythrill
Native to the misty peat-bogs of the **Bryngloom Forest**. Originates in the **Vreken** elven clans who carry the Ghost-Mycelium in their blood. In the fifth century of the Freezing Era, when the Marked Veil-Speaker Aedris succumbed to the mycelial "hush" (the Over-Lit epidemic) and turned to consuming acolyte lifeforce, her brother **Orven the Still-Handed** refused to allow political delay. He forged a blade of unworked bog-iron, bound his hands in salted leather, executed his sister to end her torment, and swore the **Barbed Vow**: to track down and eliminate any kinsman lost to aberrant sorcery.

#### C. Relationship to Magic, Power, Community, and Conflict
- **Magic:** Total negation. The Covenbane is a sterile void for magic. The silver deposits in their marrow and the alchemical null-salts they ingest prevent ambient ley-energy from taking root in their flesh.
- **Power:** Physical discipline, cold-iron conductivity, and kinetic dispelling.
- **Community:** Feared and alienated by their own kin. Regarded as tragic, necessary butchers whose presence smells of bitter brine and dead peat.
- **Conflict:** Relentless hunters of predatory Wyrd cults, rogue witches, and whisper-cult acolytes of the Counterfeit Monolith.

#### D. Visual Motifs & Resource Display
- **Visuals:** Barbed bog-iron longswords, salted leather duster coats, lead-lined salt vials, silver-scarred hands, blindfolds lined with lead-mesh.
- **Resource HUD Visual:** An iron-and-silver ledger bar featuring **Hexbreaker Charges (0–6)** represented as jagged, cold-iron barbed spikes that ignite with a pale leaden hue when charged.

#### E. Overlap & Demarcation
- **Covenbane vs. Exorcist:** Covenbane operates in the physical wild: tracking covens, ambushing spellcasters, slicing through barrier webs, and severing ley lines with cold iron. Exorcist operates in the intimate spiritual sphere: entering occupied homes, cleansing possessed mortal hosts, chanting litanies, and binding entities into salt circles.
- **Covenbane vs. Inquisitor (Retired):** Inquisitor was a dogmatic, judicial officer of the Solari dawn church (Fervor/Condemnation, courtroom trials, state executions). Covenbane is a solitary, self-mutilating swamp tracker enforcing the ancient Barbed Vow without state sanction.

#### F. Lore Categorization
- **Confirmed Lore:** Bryngloom Forest origin; Orven the Still-Handed; Barbed Vow; cold bog-iron and null-salts; Vreken mycelial hush.
- **Newly Proposed Lore:** The specific lead-mesh blindfold technique used to track residual thermal Wyrd leaks without succumbing to visual hexes.
- **Open Decisions (HeadQ):** Approval of the final payload boundary separating Covenbane's anti-magic tools from retired Inquisitor assets.

### 2. Exorcist (`exorcist`)

#### A. Core Fantasy & Player-Facing Promise
The solemn Sin-Eater and Entity Purger. Enters the terrifying intimate space where a foreign entity or Predatory Wyrd parasite has possessed a living mortal host, extracting the intruder through liturgical rites, salt circles, and sympathetic suffering while preserving the host's life.

#### B. Cultural & Historical Roots in Mythrill
Rooted in the monastic liturgists of **Frostwood Reach** and the border sanctuaries of **Sundale**. Founded during the Whispered Purge when Keth Amar's corrupt signals first drove common folk to madness. While crusaders fought monsters on battlefields, the first Exorcists entered quarantined farmsteads and sickrooms to wrench cosmic parasites out of screaming children.

#### C. Relationship to Magic, Power, Community, and Conflict
- **Magic:** Sacral expulsion and boundary enforcement. Channels the lingering protective memory of **Aethil's celestial vigil**.
- **Power:** The historical demon-binding mechanic ("Legion Mind", multi-demon enslavement) is **completely rejected per Settled HeadQ Decision CD09**. The Exorcist does NOT command or summon demons. Power comes from spiritual endurance, consecration of physical boundaries, and host protection.
- **Community:** Revered with dread. Communities view an Exorcist's arrival as proof that their home is contaminated by the unseen, but weep with gratitude when a loved one is restored whole.
- **Conflict:** The frontline defense against the Masked Acolytes' **Hollow Sight** and intrusive void-parasites.

#### D. Visual Motifs & Resource Display
- **Visuals:** Coarse off-white linen vestments, scriptural phylacteries bound to arms with copper wire, heavy iron censers emitting pungent juniper-and-salt smoke, consecrated lead seals.
- **Resource HUD Visual:** **Purification Sanctity (0–10)**, displayed as a central radiant silver Salt Circle that fills with concentric litany wards as rites progress.

#### E. Overlap & Demarcation
- **Exorcist vs. Covenbane:** Covenbane destroys the witch; Exorcist saves the host. Covenbane uses cold iron to execute; Exorcist uses consecrated salt to extract and cleanse.
- **Exorcist vs. Crusader:** Crusader is an armored warrior of holy wrath and vanguard conquest; Exorcist is a patient ritual surgeon confronting spiritual intrusions within living souls.

#### F. Lore Categorization
- **Confirmed Lore:** Expulsion and purification focus (CD09); rejection of demon binding; Frostwood Reach monastic roots.
- **Newly Proposed Lore:** The Rite of the Lead Reliquary (trapping extracted parasites in lead cylinders buried beneath consecrated salt pans).
- **Open Decisions (HeadQ):** Formal sign-off on the 3 new holy expulsion talent trees replacing the historical demonologist trees.

### 3. Dreadnaught (`dreadnaught`)

#### A. Core Fantasy & Player-Facing Promise
The Walking Fortress and Boiler-Plate Juggernaut. An immovable armored titan encased in heavy Groven plate, driven by an internal steam-furnace engine that converts kinetic shock into pressurized steam, venting superheated blasts and delivering unstoppable concussive hammer blows.

#### B. Cultural & Historical Roots in Mythrill
Engineered by the **Groven** mountain-clans and deep-miners of the **Cragjaw Peaks**. When the Blind Strike cooled the surface and geothermal heat retreated, the Groven invented pressurized boiler-suits to survive toxic fumes and volcanic eruptions in the deepest fissures, subsequently weaponizing them during the Jotkall siege wars.

#### C. Relationship to Magic, Power, Community, and Conflict
- **Magic:** None. Pure industrial and geothermal mechanics. The Dreadnaught relies on piston-driven servos, steam valves, and thermal mass.
- **Power:** Kinetic inertia, crushing mass, and boiler pressure.
- **Community:** The industrial pride of the Groven holdfasts. Dreadnaughts are the vanguard that clear subterranean tunnel collapses and hold narrow mountain gates.
- **Conflict:** Stood against glacier wyrms, deep-trench behemoths, and invading siege battalions.

#### D. Visual Motifs & Resource Display
- **Visuals:** Riveted black iron plate, glowing brass firebox grating on the chest, twin vertical steam exhaust stacks venting scalding vapor, massive tower shields with hydraulic ground-cleats.
- **Resource HUD Visual:** **Furnace Pressure (0–100 PSI)**, rendered as an authentic brass-rimmed mechanical pressure dial with a swinging needle, a green operating range (0–60), an amber surge range (61–90), and a red overheat/vent threshold (91–100).

#### E. Overlap & Demarcation
- **Dreadnaught vs. Martyr:** **Absolute demarcation required by Settled HeadQ Decision CD11.** Martyr is a divine sacrificial protector bound to the Witness-vigil of Aex, using Devotion (Tiers 1–6) and obsidian scars to absorb ally pain; Martyr retains the `MARTYR_IRONCLAD` tree (`icl_*` IDs). Dreadnaught is entirely mechanical, industrial, and thermal; Dreadnaught does not absorb ally wounds through empathy, does not use Devotion, and uses distinct `dread_*` IDs.
- **Dreadnaught vs. Berserker:** Berserker is unarmored or pelt-clad, relying on biological rage and speed; Dreadnaught is an encumbered, immovable mechanical fortress that dictates the tempo of the frontline.

#### F. Lore Categorization
- **Confirmed Lore:** Groven engineering heritage; Cragjaw holdfast roots; Furnace Pressure and thermal exhaust mechanics.
- **Newly Proposed Lore:** The Hydraulic Ground-Spike system (anchoring the suit directly into stone bedrock to become immune to forced displacement).
- **Open Decisions (HeadQ):** Final confirmation of the 3 independent tree names (`DREADNAUGHT_SIEGE_BASTION`, `DREADNAUGHT_FURNACE_ENGINE`, `DREADNAUGHT_JUGGERNAUT_MOMENTUM`).

### 4. Inscriptor (`inscriptor`)

#### A. Core Fantasy & Player-Facing Promise
The Lithic Scribe and Rune-Architect. Etches persistent, syntactic geometric glyphs and runic matrices directly onto floors, stone surfaces, and allied equipment, transforming the combat encounter into an interconnected tactical grid of activated triggers and warding barriers.

#### B. Cultural & Historical Roots in Mythrill
Descended from the ancient **Nethien** scribe lineages of the **Sundrift Vale**. The Nethien were the original recorders of the Bloodline Pacts; their ancestors discovered that raw magical currents could be stabilized against Predatory Wyrd corruption by binding them into physical, geometric lithic inscriptions.

#### C. Relationship to Magic, Power, Community, and Conflict
- **Magic:** Syntactic, physicalized runology. Magic is treated as a grammatical science carved into durable matter rather than spontaneous vocalization.
- **Power:** Spatial planning, perimeter control, and triggered chain reactions.
- **Community:** Respected as civil defense engineers, archivist-scholars, and boundary cartographers in northern fortresses.
- **Conflict:** Countering chaotic, shifting battlefield hazards by establishing predictable, immutable sanctuary zones.

#### D. Visual Motifs & Resource Display
- **Visuals:** Steel and diamond chisels, slate tablets slung across bandoliers, vials of lapis lazuli and silver-infused conductive ink, runic parchment ribbons trailing from shoulders.
- **Resource HUD Visual:** **Runic Resonance (0–10)**, displayed as a carved stone tablet with 10 recessed runic slots that illuminate with crisp cerulean script as runes are laid on the field.

#### E. Overlap & Demarcation
- **Inscriptor vs. Witch Doctor:** Inscriptor works with inorganic stone, metals, and grammatical geometric scripts (force, barrier, light). Witch Doctor works with organic materials: bog-mud, bone needles, wax, and sympathetic herbs (blight, poison, curses).
- **Inscriptor vs. Animist:** Inscriptor carves inanimate equations into stone. Animist speaks with conscious ancestral spirits and forest beings.
- **Inscriptor vs. Totemist:** Inscriptor etches 2D planar floor inscriptions and surface matrices. Totemist constructs and places 3D vertical standing conduits and pylons onto grid spaces.

#### F. Lore Categorization
- **Confirmed Lore:** Nethien scribe culture; Sundrift Vale origins; Runic Resonance; Runebinder, Enchanter, Glyphweaver specializations.
- **Newly Proposed Lore:** The Lexicon of Stone-Binding (the exact 8-character alphabet used to lock planar spells onto physical stone).
- **Open Decisions (HeadQ):** Tabletop ruling on maximum active runes on a single tactical grid square.

### 5. Witch Doctor (`witchDoctor`)

The Bog Apothecary, Effigy Crafter, and Miasma Weaver. Binds sympathetic curses through handcrafted wax-and-twine effigies, brews caustic swamp antidotes and paralytic venoms, and expels choking clouds of medicinal and virulent peat smoke.

#### B. Cultural & Historical Roots in Mythrill
Originates in the isolated swamp settlements and peat-dredgers of the **Bryngloom borderlands** and the **Sundale Marshes**. Born of desperate frontier survival against toxic spore-mists, venomous marsh insects, and waterborne decay.

#### C. Reconciliation of Historical Terminology (Mandatory HeadQ Protocol)
In strict accordance with the HeadQ directive (*"inspect and reconcile historical terminology with Mythrill’s own cultures. Do not copy real-world sacred titles into player-facing lore without explicit approval"*), the historical Haitian Vodou terms are permanently reconciled into authentic Mythrill world canon:

| Historical Term | Mythrill In-World Canonical Replacement | Design & Lore Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Bokor** | **Hexweaver / Effigy Crafter** | Avoids real-world sacred title. Represents the dark sympathetic art of binding wax poppets and bone needles to reflect enemy harm. |
| **Mambo** | **Bog Apothecary / Marrow Healer** | Avoids real-world sacred priestess title. Focuses on swamp-herb extraction, anti-toxins, and bone-setting. |
| **Houngan** | **Miasma Priest / Bane Shaman** | Avoids real-world sacred priest title. Represents the ritual mastery of burning swamp herbs, mask dances, and toxic area denial. |
| **Voodoo Essence** | **Bog Tallow / Fetish Wax (0–100)** | Replaces generic real-world term with the actual physical medium of their craft: rendering peat-wax and tallow for effigies. |

#### D. Visual Motifs & Resource Display
- **Visuals:** Willow-twig charms, dried clay animal masks smeared with charcoal, bone needles, dried marsh-moss bandages, copper cauldrons smoking over charcoal embers.
- **Resource HUD Visual:** **Bog Tallow / Fetish Wax (0–100)**, depicted as an antique clay crucible filled with molten green-black tallow, with needle markers indicating wax tiers.

#### E. Overlap & Demarcation
- **Witch Doctor vs. Toxicologist:** Toxicologist is an urban academic alchemist using glass alembics, mechanical gas canisters, and distilled mineral acids. Witch Doctor is a rural folk practitioner utilizing sympathetic magic, organic effigies, and burnt medicinal herbs.
- **Witch Doctor vs. Animist:** Witch Doctor manipulates inanimate sympathetic matter (clay dolls, tallow, dried roots) to afflict or heal living bodies. Animist communes directly with deceased human souls and ancestral courts.

#### F. Lore Categorization
- **Confirmed Lore:** Bryngloom and Sundale marsh origins; sympathetic doll and poison mechanics; rejection of real-world Vodou titles.
- **Newly Proposed Lore:** The Tradition of the Willow Poppet (the ritual by which a Witch Doctor binds a lock of hair or drop of blood to a wax figure).
- **Open Decisions (HeadQ):** Formal confirmation of the tree export names (`WITCH_DOCTOR_HEXWEAVER`, `WITCH_DOCTOR_APOTHECARY`, `WITCH_DOCTOR_MIASMA`).

### 6. Bladedancer (`bladedancer`)

#### A. Core Fantasy & Player-Facing Promise
The Kinetic Duelist and Steel-Nerved Flow Master. Engages in perpetual, fluid melee motion, chaining acrobatic dodges, precision deflections, and razor-sharp parry-ripostes with twin light blades to build rhythmic kinetic momentum that accelerates with every exchanged strike.

#### B. Cultural & Historical Roots in Mythrill
Developed by the **Skaldic** duelists of **Frostwood Reach** and the maritime fencing guilds of the **Iceheart Sea** free-ports. While plate-wearing knights struggled in shipboard combat and blizzard ice, bladedancers cultivated the "Dance of the Razor-Gale," turning slippery footing and enemy momentum into deadly advantages.

#### C. Relationship to Magic, Power, Community, and Conflict
- **Magic:** None. Pure physical reflexes, kinetic timing, and mastery of footwork.
- **Power:** Rhythm, momentum conservation, and exploiting enemy overextensions.
- **Community:** Celebrated as champions in dueling rings, ship captains' personal bodyguards, and theatrical martial performers.
- **Conflict:** Single-combat dueling, countering brute-force monsters by dancing beneath their reach.

#### D. Visual Motifs & Resource Display
- **Visuals:** Twin curved scimitars or sabres with swept hilts, lightweight leather armor lined with fluttering silk ribbons that accentuate movement, high-topped supple calfskin boots.
- **Resource HUD Visual:** **Momentum & Flow (0–10)**, displayed as a dual-arc kinetic compass whose crescent needle sweeps through four flow tiers: *Adagio (0–2)*, *Andante (3–5)*, *Allegro (6–8)*, and *Presto Apex (9–10)*.

#### E. Overlap & Demarcation
- **Bladedancer vs. Berserker:** Berserker uses brute fury, heavy two-handed axes, and absorbs damage to build Rage. Bladedancer avoids hits entirely through agile footwork, dual light blades, and tempo.
- **Bladedancer vs. Hunter:** Hunter is an ambusher and tracker utilizing bows, spears, and environmental camouflage. Bladedancer is a visible, centerpiece melee fighter who controls the center of the room.
- **Bladedancer vs. Druid:** Druid alters their biology into beast forms. Bladedancer relies strictly on human acrobatic skill and tempered steel.

#### F. Lore Categorization
- **Confirmed Lore:** Skaldic and Iceheart Sea origins; Momentum resource; Flow-Master, Duelist, Shadow-Dancer specializations.
- **Newly Proposed Lore:** The Principle of the Centrifugal Edge (the kinetic theory of redirecting large-creature impact force through curved scimitar guards).
- **Open Decisions (HeadQ):** Tabletop rule for momentum decay when moving fewer than 10 feet on a turn.

### 7. Totemist (`totemist`)

#### A. Core Fantasy & Player-Facing Promise
The Conduit Sculptor and Territorial Pylon Architect. Erects physical, ground-anchored stone and storm-iron effigy pylons onto the tactical grid, establishing resonant energy matrices, defensive perimeters, and elemental conductors across the battlefield.

#### B. Cultural & Historical Roots in Mythrill
Hails from the high wind-swept plateaus and storm-crags of the **Cragjaw Peaks**. When annual lightning storms and tectonic fissures threatened highland villages, lithic crafters carved monolithic standing stones to anchor and disperse ambient elemental fury into the earth.

#### C. Relationship to Magic, Power, Community, and Conflict
- **Magic:** Territorial conduit channeling. The Totemist anchors raw terrestrial and atmospheric currents into physical monoliths.
- **Power:** Zone architecture, conduit triangulation, and physical pylon durability.
- **Community:** Honored as village architects, boundary-wardens, and storm-tamers in the highlands.
- **Conflict:** Holding choke points, fortifying camp perimeters, and neutralizing aerial lightning strikes.

#### D. Visual Motifs & Resource Display
- **Visuals:** Heavy stone-working mauls, coils of conductive copper and storm-iron wire, carved miniature slate effigies slung at the waist, granite dust coating arms.
- **Resource HUD Visual:** **Conduit Attunement (0–10) & 3 Pylon Nodes**, rendered as a triangular lithic pedestal with 3 glowing socket conduits indicating active pylons on the board and central resonance attunement.

#### E. Overlap & Demarcation
- **Totemist vs. Animist (Critical HeadQ Demarcation):**
  - **Animist:** Internal spiritual communion, ancestral court voices, bone-wall shaping, beast specters, spirit erosion. 0 physical vertical pylon tokens.
  - **Totemist:** Physical 3D pylon tokens deployed onto specific grid tiles with individual Durability pools, aura radii, and geometric conduits connecting them. No ancestor communion; works with ambient elemental and lithic energy.

#### F. Lore Categorization
- **Confirmed Lore:** Creator-approved new direction; distinct from Animist; 0 historical code.
- **Newly Proposed Lore:** The Tri-Pylon Harmonic Principle (connecting 3 pylons in a triangle to create an enclosed resonant barrier field).
- **Open Decisions (HeadQ):** Approval of the proposed content lock and pylon placement action point costs.
