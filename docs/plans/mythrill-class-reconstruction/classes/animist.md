# Animist — retained under critical review

**DESIGN INTENT:** do not remove Animist. **Status: current evidence + two proposed revisions; final identity unresolved.**

## What the current class actually contains

**AUTHORED:**49 spells; Thornwarden, Spirit Binder, Stormscribe. Ancestral totems, spirit invocations, curses, bodily hosting, permanent runic networks and environmental manipulation. Its classIdentity explicitly describes three adapted traditions and three catastrophic burdens. Thornwarden's authored passive includes permanent undispellable zones; Stormscribe combines attack/check bonuses, lifesteal and Storm damage; Spirit Binder changes curses/invocations and beast summons. These are broad overlapping fantasies and powerful prose, not proven execution.

**CONFIRMED local contract:** Resonance0–20 with Spirit Erosion marker at15, four stages; class-specific payment planner and bounded local tracking (`animistResourceContract.js`). The normalizer does not execute all erosion penalties. **CONFIRMED provenance:** real preserved spirits/memory traces in substrates, memorial/spirit anchor and communion apprenticeship, Silent Throat/Bayar Wind-Throat. Native list is broad; see full25-row matrix, not a generic nature-class compatibility chart.

## Revision A — Animist as living memorial medium (preferred provisional)

Fantasy: commune with named ancestral presences through real carried substrates. Retain name **Animist**, consider **Spiritkeeper** if clarity wins. Alternatives Spirit Speaker, Medium, Ancestor Keeper. Combat: positioned communion/support; noncombat: memory, witness and navigation. Complexity medium-high.

Proposed resource is three **occupied spirit seats**, not Resonance heat. Prepare three named presences; host one, place one anchored invocation and leave one uncommitted. Each presence has a request and a finite permission. Moving it between host/anchor takes1 AP; one service per presence per own turn, each paid by caster AP. Consequence is an unmet obligation or disrupted substrate, not a random spirit-loss punishment for reaching15. No extra autonomous summon turns.

Trees: **Voice of the Road** carries an invocation with a moving group; **Archive Keeper** anchors a persistent witness at a prepared place; **Spirit Host** temporarily accepts a named presence's skill/ward without changing anatomy. Their target, movement and service decisions differ. Hosting is permission/voice, not Druid body replacement; an ancestor is not a Deathcaller combat minion. Druid's name imports no conventional nature/spirit role into this distinction.

Three-turn draft: T1 host navigator and place ward (two1-AP moves, host1/anchor1/free1); T2 use ward's one service and move navigator to a carried anchor (host0/anchor2/free1); T3 recall threatened ward to the free seat for1 AP, voluntarily losing its protection. Final services, obligations, numerical effects and tree nodes remain to specify.

## Revision B — Spiritkeeper as anchor-network conductor

Fantasy: build a small circuit between actual memorial objects and carry voices through it. Proposed new public name **Spiritkeeper**, with Animist retained as a possible tradition title/compatibility choice subject to approval. Resource is up to three physical anchors and two active directed channels. A channel occupies a connection until its named spirit has delivered a finite invocation. AP placement/relocation competes with casting.

Trees: **Way-Singer** relocates one carried anchor, **Shrine Warden** maintains one defensive fixed circuit, **Witness Binder** stores and releases testimony through a prepared channel. Unlike Inscriptor, the object must hold a real ancestral substrate and cannot freely encode any generic conditional spell. Unlike Fate Weaver, edges carry a spirit's invocation, not redistributed HP/roll outcomes.

Three-turn draft: T1 place anchorsA/B and openA→B; T2 spend one channel use to relay an ally ward while reserving second channel; T3 moveA, deliberately closing its old edge, then reopen under new terms. Exact range/LoE/expiry and point costs remain unresolved. This option risks visual/mechanical overlap with Fate Weaver and more engine work; compare prototypes before choosing it.

## Scope boundaries for both options

- **Witch Doctor:** prepared medicines/charms and reciprocity in living care; not every ancestor.
- **Primalist:** living ecological aspects/environment, not deceased witnesses.
- **Inscriptor:** written conditional clauses/glyph sequences, not automatic ancestral memory.
- **Deathcaller:** commanded manifested dead actors, not advisory presences with no independent turns.
- **Druid:** bodily transformation/adaptation, not a spirit's skill/voice; name approved, economy still under review.

Existing Stormscribe's best inscription content should be reviewed for Inscriptor; moving all Storm-tagged spells is not a defensible migration. Permanent undispellable zones need bounded limits and explicit engine status, regardless of which class keeps them.

## UI/authoring and decision

A compact triptych of named presence seals (A) or carried/fixed memorial anchors (B), with actual occupied/available/disrupted state. Expanded view teaches requests, remaining services, substrate and recall cost. Wizard needs structured service/anchor operations, not a Resonance number. Both require persistent effects, exact linked object identities, turn/reset and consent support beyond P9.
