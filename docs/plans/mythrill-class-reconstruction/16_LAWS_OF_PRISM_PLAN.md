## Required class section contract

Every final class chapter needs: one-sentence identity; real source/acquisition/native traditions; starting state and tabletop pieces; exact generation, reservation/spend, timing, reset, risk and expiry; three tree differences; example turns with before/after; universal AP/mana/weapon/check interactions; rare acquisition and body incompatibilities; multiplayer/manual execution status; edge cases and glossary.

Present **intended tabletop rule** and **VTT support state** side by side without making engine incompleteness a different game rule. A manually adjudicated rule can be valid, but must never be described as automatically executed.

## Separate proposed draft: Fate Weaver resource explanation

> You carry three thread tokens. A token is free, reserved in a link, or recovering. When you tie two willing lives, reserve one token and write the link's direction, event, amount and expiry. The link is not a promise to rewrite anything that has already happened.
>
> When an eligible event is about to resolve, decide whether the prepared link applies under its terms. A protective link divides a bounded amount of the original danger; it does not copy that danger into an endless chain. After it resolves, turn the token sideways until your next own turn. When a link expires unused, return its token to the free pool.
>
> At a physical table, place two named actor cards with one directed string and a one-use marker. In the VTT, the details list the same endpoints, terms and remaining use. Until linked consequences are supported, the GM resolves this procedure manually; an SVG thread is not proof that damage has moved.

This draft intentionally does not settle mitigation order or N-actor authority. Those rules must be approved before publishing the final chapter.

## Separate proposed draft: Hunter distinction

> A Hunter prepares an opening against one quarry. Read, Angle and Pressure are different kinds of evidence, not identical points. A companion may help obtain evidence, but a trained solo Hunter has real ways to create every opening needed by the Stalker path. Changing quarry abandons the old opening. Write the quarry's name and place the exact evidence symbols beside it.
