## Established relationships

- **CONFIRMED source declarations:** classes/index.js:6–9 and talentTreeData.js:77–95 explicitly identify Deathcaller + Lichborne → Revenant, Covenbane + Exorcist → Inquisitor, and Dreadnaught → Martyr Ironclad.
- **HISTORICAL exact spell continuity:** Gambler has 28 and Fate Weaver 35 top-level historical spells; all 63 IDs survive in current Gambit (65 total authored spells). This proves continuity of IDs, not unchanged semantics.
- **DESIGN INTENT:** Chaos Weaver is also a required independent restoration. Exact mapping of its historical spells/resources to Harbinger versus Gambit needs per-record tracing; the 63-ID result does not establish a three-way mechanical merge.
- **HISTORICAL:** Huntress has the same three tree IDs now used by Apex. Path-follow history connects Apex to earlier Huntress history; compare rename diffs before treating the present companion requirement as historical solo design.
- **HISTORICAL:** Bladedancer, Formbender, Inscriptor, Primalist, Witch Doctor, Titan, Oracle and Doomsayer had real registered data and wired talent trees. They were more than artwork labels. Exact mechanics transferred into current classes still need per-node/spell mapping.
- **UNRESOLVED:** a 68-icon inventory from a prior audit is not reproduced by counting a single snapshot. This snapshot has 63 top-level icon-directory files and 183 recursive files; HEAD has 481 recursive files. Formats, thumbnails, copies and aliases must be classified before calling any number a playable roster.
