# Naming and fantasy recommendations

All recommendations here are **PROPOSED** unless explicitly marked **DESIGN INTENT**. Ratings are editorial judgments, not implementation evidence.

## Approved creator naming direction

**DESIGN INTENT:** the transformation class is **Druid**. Metamorph is rejected; Shapeshifter and other alternatives are historical discussion only. This supersedes earlier naming recommendations, not the ongoing review of anatomical mechanics, specializations or native origins. [Exact scope](31_CREATOR_NAMING_DECISION_DRUID.md).

Selectively use recognizable established names to help players understand the roster. Distinctive mechanics, specialization behavior and Mythrill cultural origins remain the primary appeal. Familiarity does not require every class to be conventional and does not import another game's mechanics, worship, companions or heritage restrictions.

## Working candidate frontier

**DESIGN INTENT mandatory restoration:** Gambler, Fate Weaver, Chaos Weaver, Deathcaller, Lichborne and the transformation successor. **PROPOSED prototypes:** Hunter, Inscriptor, Titan, Exorcist, Covenbane, Witch Doctor, Primalist, Dreadnaught, Bladedancer, Oracle, Monk. **Existing retained/review identities:** Arcanoneer, Berserker, Harbinger, Chronarch, Inquisitor, False Prophet, Lunarch, Martyr, Minstrel, Plaguebringer, Animist, Pyrofiend, Spellguard, Toxicologist, Warden, Augur, Crusader.

This is a research frontier, not an approved final roster. Doomsayer is a real historical design to assess against Harbinger; Huntress and Formbender are important genealogies, not automatic additional release classes. Gambit/Revenant/Shaper are compatibility destinations only.

## Five-test review procedure

For every candidate, separately score fantasy, tactical decisions, resource economy, native-world foundation and specialization divergence. Require concrete evidence/examples rather than a total score that lets a failed dimension average out. Monk must demonstrate martial footwork/discipline without borrowing anatomy mutations; Titan must demonstrate Sacred/Arcane impact without being a larger Berserker; Dreadnaught must demonstrate equipment/brace decisions without merely being Martyr's suffering with armor art.
