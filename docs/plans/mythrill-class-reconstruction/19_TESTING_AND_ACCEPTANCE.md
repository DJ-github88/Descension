## Resource-specific invariants

- Gambler:20 unique cards conserved; escrow cannot be spent twice; stake chosen before outcome; zero Fortune is playable; observers cannot inspect hand.
- Fate Weaver:3 tokens partitioned into free/reserved/recovering; no self-loop/recursive redirect; total intended consequence conserved; no post-receipt rewrites.
- Chaos Weaver:6 tile identities conserved; choice precedes recorded draw; integer multiplicity/weights match preview; retries cannot choose new branch.
- Deathcaller:capacity/order/caster AP enforced together; every manifestation exact/versioned; no extra independent turns; defeat/dismissal cannot duplicate actors on reconnect.
- Lichborne:finite anchor integrity, trait/vulnerability pair, real anchor identity, no kill/reload free recharge or automatic full-HP return.
- Hunter:one exact quarry, distinct evidence, shared generation budget across solo/companion, no target-change reroll/refill.
- Druid (proposed anatomical economy):slot exclusivity, active-slot cap, transition budget, suspended trait unavailable, body-interface restrictions persist. Only its player-facing name is approved.

These proposed invariants must change when an approved design changes. Do not freeze tests around an unapproved creative choice and then treat test success as creator approval.
