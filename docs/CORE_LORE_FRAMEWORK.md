## 1. COSMIC ENTITY HIERARCHY

Six entities. Clear roles. No overlap.

### 1.1 — The Warden
- Not a god, not a person, not a force. The **language reality speaks**. The grammar of consequence.
- Every bargain has a price. The Warden does not *choose* the price — the price simply *is*. Naming Aex wasn't a decision; that's the exchange rate for caging a star.
- The Warden has no will, no agenda, no preference. It is the rule that a cost exists.
- Keth-Amar operates *within* the Warden's framework. Every Dark Bargain was legal under the Warden's system. Keth-Amar has never broken a bargain — it writes them better.

### 1.2 — Keth-Amar
- A cosmic predator that hunts stars in their vulnerable Deepening cycle. Ancient, patient, **cunning**.
- Not a mindless hunger. Keth-Amar *schemes*. It studies its prey — the civilizations orbiting the star, their fears, their folklore, their breaking points.
- It offered **Sol's own warmth** deceptively to the houses — not its own energy. It pretended it could free some heat while using the bargains to crack the seal.
- It writes bargains under the Warden's framework. It has never broken one. It doesn't need to.
- It is **bitter** that the 7th heir (Viridane) never agreed. It knows something is off. It cannot fully access Sol. One thread was left uncut.

### 1.3 — Sol
- A star. A cosmic/divine being. Not young, not weak — simply *vulnerable* in its Deepening cycle, which is when Keth-Amar preys.
- Bound beneath Sundale, wrapped in the corpse of its own firstborn (Aex). It did not consent to this — but it was done *to save it* from being eaten.
- In a state of suspended twilight — the Deepening interrupted mid-process, forced into torpor.
- Cannot rebirth. Cannot die. Cannot scream. It endures.

### 1.4 — Aex
- Sol's firstborn. A being of pure stellar radiance. **Willingly sacrificed** himself to cage Sol to save it from Keth-Amar.
- The embodiment of sacrifice for a greater good. Arthas-before-the-Lich-King tragic figure.
- **Flayed alive** by House Solvan wielding a blade of crystallized starlight. His hide woven into the binding seal.
- Not dead. **Lynched** — stretched across the vault in perpetual agony. Conscious. Aware.
- The 7 Monoliths are **different parts of Aex's body**. Each is a piece of him, scattered when the seal cracked.
- The false Monolith (Viridane's missing heir) means Keth-Amar has corrupted 6 of 7. If the false Monolith is also corrupted, the Wyrd reaches Aex's **heart** — transforming him into something worse. This is the world's ticking clock.
- Every pulse is Aex **screaming** — trying to reach Sol, trying to reunite his scattered body, trying to break free.
- The Augurs measure the scream's output. 40% → 0% across 65 pulses. Aex is exhausted. He has stopped screaming. The Monoliths are waking not because he wants them to, but because he can no longer hold them still.

### 1.5 — The Watcher in the Mist / The Keeper of the Last Threshold
- **One entity**, perceived differently by different cultures.
- In the Frostwood: the Watcher — a whisper in the mist, an ancient fae presence in moonlit groves.
- In the Bryngloom: the Keeper — the death-boundary entity that accepted the Neth's First Contract.
- The boundary between life and death, memory and oblivion. Older than Keth-Amar's interest in this system.
- Does not serve the Warden. Is not answerable to Keth-Amar. Has its own agenda.
- Reached Viridane before Keth-Amar could because it **enjoys balance**. Keth-Amar winning outright would be unbalanced.
- Its attention is wavering now — Keth-Amar's presence strains the boundary it oversees. It cannot fight Keth-Amar directly. It can only *weigh*.

### 1.6 — Scathrach the Ashen Sovereign
- A **fragment of Aex's flayed hide** that fell into Emberspire's deepest vent *during the Binding itself* (not the Breach).
- This fragment, corrupted by the Wyrd already leaking through Aex's stretched body, grew sentient.
- For centuries it was Keth-Amar's emissary — the Sun-Eater rooting through the wound, whispering Pyrofiend bargains.
- But Aex's nature was too strong. The fragment **rejected the corruption**. Not completely — it is still Wyrd-touched, still monstrous — but it developed its own will.
- Now it **despises Keth-Amar** for what it was made into. It calls in Pyrofiend debts not to serve the Sun-Eater, but to hoard power to wound it back.
- It is the pain of the flaying given form and malice. Aex's vengeful ghost — a ghost that has forgotten it was ever innocent.
- Keth-Amar cannot reach into that vent anymore. Scathrach sealed it from within. A permanent irritant in the Sun-Eater's side.

## 2. THE SEAL & THE BREACH

### 2.1 — The Binding (Year 3)
- Sol enters the Deepening. Keth-Amar arrives to feed.
- The 7 noble houses (Thalreth, Skalvyr, Tesshan, Solvan, Mereval, Ordavan, **Viridane**) go to the Warden.
- The Warden names the price: Aex, Sol's firstborn, willing.
- House Solvan wields the blade. Aex is flayed alive. His hide is woven into the binding seal. The seal is absolute: 7 bloodlines, 7 signatures, 7 heirs.
- The knife shatters. Fragments kept as relics.

### 2.2 — The Corruption Years (Years 3-11)
- Keth-Amar cannot breach. It whispers for 8 years.
- The Wyrd is its corruption breathed into rumors, tales, myths, folklore. Keth-Amar contaminates the cultural soil and the Wyrd grows whatever fears already exist there.
- It works on the houses individually, tailored to each region's specific fears and folk tales.
- **Keth-Amar did not offer its own energy.** It offered *Sol's* warmth — deceptively. "Let me crack the seal a little. I'll free some heat. The world won't freeze. You'll survive." This is how it got the heirs to agree.

### 2.3 — The Breach (Year 11)
- **6 of 7 heirs agree.** They march to the peaks. Keth-Amar consumes them as vessel-keys.
- The 7th heir — **Viridane's** — never agreed. The Watcher reached them first. They fled south.
- Because only 6 heirs fed Keth-Amar, the seal **cracked but did not shatter**. The vault opened partially, not fully.
- Emberspire erupts. The Wyrd bleeds through the cracks. The world's vents are the evidence of the cracks — Sol's life energy seeping out, Keth-Amar leeching it through the fractures.
- The seal split into **7 Monoliths** — 6 true fragments of Aex's woven hide, and 1 false (the 7th heir's signature was never real, so the Monolith corresponding to that house is a hollow echo).

### 2.4 — The Partial Seal (Year 11 to present)
- The seal is neither whole nor broken. Keth-Amar is not *inside* the vault. It is pressed against it, seeping through the cracks.
- It feeds on the energy bleeding through — Sol's life, Aex's pain — but cannot consume Sol whole.
- The 6 houses cannot admit 1 heir refused. They erase Viridane from every record, elevate House Morrath as substitute, and pretend the signature is real.
- The fog is not the Warden's doing. The fog **is the Warden's grief** — the universe weeping for Aex. Or it's the Watcher's protection. Or it's just what happens when a star's warmth fails. Scholars disagree.
- The true reason Viridane was erased: **Keth-Amar hunts through knowledge.** To remember Viridane is to leave a thread it can follow. The erasure protects them. The fog that eats memory completes the hiding.

## 3. THE MONOLITHS & THE PULSE

### 3.1 — What the Monoliths Are
- 7 fragments of Aex's woven hide. **Parts of his body.**
- Each corresponds to one of the 7 houses that signed the Binding.
- Each is hidden/buried/discovered in a different region of the world.
- Each Monolith is tied to the *flavor* of that region's bargain and noble house.
- The 7th Monolith (Viridane's) is **false** — a hollow echo where the signature was never real. Keth-Amar cannot find it. The Watcher hid it.

### 3.2 — The Pulse (The Aex Scream)
- Not a fixed 12-year clock. A **natural harmonic** — the frequency at which 7 scattered pieces of the same being naturally try to sync up.
- When the Monoliths align (gravitationally, ley-line-wise, metaphysically — the exact mechanism is debated), Aex's scattered consciousness briefly reunites.
- This creates a **resonance** felt across the world: earthquakes, geysers, lights in the sky, an audible hum.
- During alignment: the Wyrd recedes globally, Wyrd-creatures weaken, Chaos Pockets shrink. This is the only time the fog in the Frostwood truly thins. Militaries and expeditions move.
- After alignment: the **Draining** resumes. Keth-Amar presses close. The Wyrd regrows. Tensions escalate.
- The resonance is **not predictable**. Sometimes it comes every 12 years. Sometimes it skips. Sometimes it comes early if a Monolith is disturbed. The Augurs estimate. They are often wrong.
- Each pulse is different. Each pulse is weaker (Aex is running out of strength) — or *changed* (a corrupted Monolith warps the resonance). The next pulse may be Aex's last. Or Keth-Amar's first.

### 3.3 — Narrative Function of the Pulse
- The pulse is a **clock**, not a loot window.
- Players are active during the **Draining** — when the world is at its worst, Keth-Amar is pressing, the Wyrd is thriving.
- The pulse is the deadline: *before the next alignment, something will change forever.*
- What changes depends on what the players do:
  - A pulse could reveal a Monolith that was hidden.
  - A pulse could be the first silent one — Aex stops screaming.
  - A pulse could trigger Scathrach's next phase.
  - A pulse could be the one where Keth-Amar finally figures out where the false Monolith is.

## 6. THE DEAD MOON

- The moon was an **egg**. What laid it is unknown. It was not Keth-Amar.
- It shattered eons ago — before humans, before the Binding. What broke it is unknown.
- The largest fragment **fell in the Frostwood's moonlit groves**. The fae found it, nurtured it.
- From the nurtured fragments hatched the **lunar parasites** — void-adapted organisms that feed on memory, sensation, vitality.
- The Lunarch class bonds with these parasites. The parasites cannot survive outside the moon's corpse-ecology.
- The Watcher in the Mist may be connected — perhaps the Watcher was what laid the egg. Or perhaps it is a parasite that ate its parent. This is a mystery.
- The Astril's constellation-spirits remember the moon when it was whole — a second light in the sky, now gone. It appears in their oldest songs as a lament for a sibling that never hatched.
- Keth-Amar, arriving for Sol, *nested* in the corpse-moon. Not laid by it, not connected to it — simply using the largest dead thing in orbit as a perch.

## 7. THE DAWN VIGIL & THE EMBERTH FAITH

The conflict over Sol is a schism within a single faith — the Embers of Sol — now broken into three factions.

**The Emberth were the first faithful.** They emerged from the deep vents at the Breach to find a world in crisis. Their culture had always revered the heat and light below. They recognized Sol as the source. For centuries they were the sole keepers of the faith: quiet, patient, tending embers in subterranean shrines.

The Dawn Vigil broke from them 300 years later, when the 12th pulse (then still mostly on schedule) revealed how much weaker Aex had become. A faction of Emberth said *"waiting is failing"* — Sol will not return by itself. The sky-faithful (surface humans who had adopted Emberth teachings) joined them. The Vigil was born.

### The Three Factions

**The Dawn Vigil** — Fanatic, militarized theocracy under Hierophant Aethelgard. Public doctrine: "reassemble the Monoliths to restart Sol." The Vigil hunts Monolith Shards because they believe each piece brought closer together strengthens the seal — but the truth is **reassembling the Monoliths summons Keth-Amar**. The Vigil leadership knows this. They believe they can **bind** Keth-Amar as Aex was bound — sacrifice someone else to cage the predator, and force Sol to complete its Deepening unmolested. They are a unified Corperation — they will do what the houses failed to do. Most Vigil soldiers do not know this. They believe they are saving the world by hunting Shards and fighting the Wyrd. They are the Scarlet Crusade — wrong, often brutal, but not cartoonishly evil. Some of them are genuinely good people who will realize the truth too late.

**The Risen (Korr faithful)** — The old Emberth faith. Patience. Vigil. "Sol will return when it is ready. Keep the ember. Wait." They oppose the Vigil's violence openly — hiding Monolith Shards, guiding expeditions away from dangerous discoveries, sheltering dissenters. They are the Silver Hand — dwindling, old, seen as naive. But they are right about one thing the Vigil is wrong about: **you cannot cage what hunts cages.**

**The Scoured** — Heretics who cut their own forge-marks (the ritual scars of Emberth identity). They hunt Monolith Shards to **seal the Breach permanently** — not to restart Sol, but to kill it gently. "Sol is gone. She has been gone since Binding year 3. What bleeds through the vents is a ghost. Seal the door. Let the star die in peace rather than be eaten." They are scattered, hunted by both Vigil and Risen. They carry the truth even the Vigil won't admit: **Aex's sacrifice was for nothing if Sol is still trapped.** Let go. The Scoured are tragic, desperate, and some of them have already made their own bargains with Scathrach.

### Relationship to the Cult of Forgotten Shadow
The Vigil is **self-corrupted** — Keth-Amar's Wyrd seeping into existing zealotry, not a separate infiltration. The Cult of Forgotten Shadow, if it exists in this setting, is a Vigil splinter that went too far — openly worshipping Keth-Amar as inevitable rather than as an enemy to be bound.

## 8. THE SEVEN MONOLITHS

### 8.1 — What They Are
Each Monolith is a **fragment of Aex's physical body**, woven into the seal when House Solvan flayed him. When the seal cracked at the Breach, these fragments broke loose and fell to different regions of the world. Each is tied to the noble house whose signature anchored that piece of the binding.

They are not *all* body parts in the literal sense — some are pieces of his woven hide, some are bones, some are tissue that crystallized under the pressure of the Binding. The common people call them Monoliths because that's what they look like: standing stones of strange, flesh-veined crystal that hum at a frequency that aches in the teeth.

### 8.2 The True Six

| # | House | Body Part | Region | Location | Character |
|---|-------|-----------|--------|----------|-----------|
| 1 | **Thalreth** | Right hand (skin) | Frostwood | Deep in a mist-choked valley, hidden by fog that has never lifted. The Watcher's territory. | The Fog-Hand — closed in a fist that never opens. Thalreth's bargain fog hides it, but also traps it. Pulses through it feel muffled. |
| 2 | **Skalvyr** | Forehead/skull fragment | Nordhalla | Embedded in a glacier that has grown around it like a cyst. Visible as a dark shape beneath the ice. | The Ice-Crown — pulsing blue light visible under the ice during winter nights. Skalvyr's eternal winter froze it in place. The frost-tithe feeds Keth-Amar through this contact. |
| 3 | **Tesshan** | Rib bone | Cragjaw | In a crevasse on the highest unclimbed peak, wrapped in perpetual blizzard. | The Wind-Bone — the blizzard-veil that Tesshan traded visibility for circles this peak, isolating the Monolith. The blizzard *is* the Wyrd guarding it. |
| 4 | **Mereval** | Lungs | Iceheart Sea | At the bottom of the deepest trench, where the perpetual storm above creates crushing pressure. | The Depth-Breath — it breathes. Underwater, miles down, the lung-tissue still expands and contracts. Storm above it cycles with its breathing. |
| 5 | **Ordavan** | Spine | Sundrift Vale | Half-buried in the endless grass, visible as a ridge of black crystalline vertebrae running for a mile. | The Grass-Spine — the eternal darkness overhead comes from Keth-Amar drinking light through this Monolith. The grass is black where it grows over bone. |
| 6 | **Solvan** | Heart (false) | Sundale | In the ruins of the oldest Solvan keep, beneath the floor of the hall where Aex was named to the Warden. | The Still-Heart — it beats once per hour. Each beat sends a tremor through the ground for a mile. Solvan keeps it as a reliquary, guards it with their last silence. |

### 8.3 The False Monolith (Viridane's)

| House | Body Part | Region | Location | Nature |
|-------|-----------|--------|----------|--------|
| **Viridane** | **None — hollow echo** | Frostwood (shifting) | Moved by the Watcher. Currently in a grove that exists between heartbeats of the pulse. | Not a piece of Aex. A **void cast** — the shape of a signature that was never made. It feels like a Monolith. It hums like a Monolith. Examine it closely and you realize it hums in *anticipation*, not pain. It is waiting. |

Keth-Amar cannot find the false Monolith. The Watcher hides it. If Keth-Amar ever does find it, consuming it will not give access to Aex's heart — but it will reveal that Viridane's thread was never cut. It will know it was tricked. And it will be **furious.**

### 8.4 Narrative Hooks Per Monolith

1. **Thalreth (Fog-hand)** — The players could dispel the fog (freeing the Monolith but exposing Viridane's descendants) or reinforce it (protecting the false Monolith's secret but letting Keth-Amar continue leaching the hand).

2. **Skalvyr (Ice-Crown)** — The Monolith is growing. The glacier is cracking. Something is about to be exposed. Does the party let the Vigil reach it first, or try to reach it through a blizzard and Skalvyr warriors?

3. **Tesshan (Wind-Bone)** — Somehow the most intact Monolith. Tesshan's bargain was the most precise. Find it, and you might find the others. But the blizzard-veil is also the most direct Wyrd-channel.

4. **Mereval (Depth-Breath)** — The lungs still breathe. Could you *stop* them from breathing? What would that do to the pulse? Ritual at the bottom of the sea — requires breathing water, enduring pressure, facing what lives in the dark.

5. **Ordavan (Grass-Spine)** — The most exposed. The Vigil already knows where it is. They have been trying to retrieve it for 40 years. The Vale is a war zone. The Wyrd is strongest here because Keth-Amar feeds best through the spine.

6. **Solvan (Heart-But-Not)** — The heart that beats once per pulse. The Solvan keep it as a shrine and a prison. They know it is not truly the heart. They guard the truth because they are ashamed. The only Monolith surrounded by *people* — a political puzzle, not a dungeon.

7. **False Monolith** — The party could find it by accident. Or the Watcher could lead them to it. Or Keth-Amar could find it, and the world changes forever. It is the world's greatest secret.
