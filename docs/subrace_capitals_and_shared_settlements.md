# Mythrill VTT — Master Subrace Capitals & Shared Settlements Guide

> **Visual Directive & Worldbuilding Mandates**:
> 1. **Complete World Geography**: Covers **all 25 Subraces** across the **10 Core Races**.
> 2. **Dual Settlement Paradigm**: For every subrace, this guide defines:
>    - **Part A: The Capital City / Native Metropolis**: The sovereign cultural, architectural, and spiritual center of that subrace.
>    - **Part B: The Shared Lesser Settlement / Crossroads Outpost**: A vital frontier town, trade roadstead, or canal hub that they co-inhabit and manage alongside **1 to 3 other subraces**.
> 3. **Inter-Subrace Synergies**: Every shared settlement articulates the exact economic, tactical, and ecological dynamics that unite the co-existing subraces (e.g. engineering + navigation, metallurgy + scouting, agriculture + banking).
> 4. **Samwise ABC 1995 Style Prompts**: Every single settlement includes a ready-to-copy concept art prompt featuring foreground subrace characters actively living in their element, set against monumental monochromatic architecture on off-white watercolor paper with a single signature watercolor explosion.
> 5. **Anti-Text & Anti-Artifact Guardrails**: `STRICTLY NO TEXT, NO WORDS, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO LABELS, NO SIGNATURES, NO WATERMARKS, NO BORDERS, NO FRAMES`.
