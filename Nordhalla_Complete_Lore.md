> **Status:** Canonical Master Lore Update. Source of truth for Nordhalla launch content.
> **App Launch Focus:** Nordhalla is the **sole, self-contained launch region** for the app map and initial gameplay release. Other continents will be added in future expansions.
> **Three subregions:** **Rime-spire Peaks** · **Skaldfjord Dal** · **Frostfang Wastes**

## PART I: THE LAUNCH REGION (NORDHALLA)

### Geography
Nordhalla is a **brutalist cathedral of frozen black fjords, towering glaciers, and jagged basalt peaks** in the far north. It serves as the self-contained initial launch setting for the Mythrill VTT campaign.

Three geographic forces shape the land:
- **Glaciers** — pressed in from the north and west, halted in place by the Glacier Bargain.
- **Geothermal vents** — volcanic fissures and craters punching warmth and sulfur-steam through permafrost bedrock.
- **Black fjords** — deep, basalt-walled marine inlets carved out by the pre-Star primordial landscape.

### Folklore Textures & Native Immune System
Nordhalla's folklore is deeply rooted in **Finnish, Greenlandic, and Norse mythology**. The region is inhabited by:
- **Landvaettir** — ancient land spirits bound to stone, peak, and tundra moraine.
- **Qalupalik** — predatory, child-snatching sea-hags dwelling beneath pack ice and black firths.
- **Jutul** — colossal frost-giants of the high glacial cirques and pre-Star ossuaries.
- **Glacier Wyrms** — primordial elemental serpents coiled deep within the creeping ice sheets.
- **Fossegrim** — waterfall and fjord spirits playing enchanting water-music in glacial meltwater.

These creatures and spirits are Nordhalla's **original owners** and act as the land's natural immune system, actively resisting mortal encroachment and unnatural intrusions.

### Culture & Combat
* **Common Skalds & Hunters (Pre-Binding Mortals):** Commoners, skalds, and outlaw hunters rely on **matchlock firearms, black powder, cold-iron traps, archery, and folk taboos/traditions** to appease and survive native beasts and land spirits. Because saltpeter is scarce in the permafrost, hunters produce volatile **Barrow-Powder** by mixing vent-sulfur with crushed, fossilized *Jutul* bone marrow.
* **Infused House Leaders:** Noble clan leaders and Infused bloodlines channel **celestial Rime magic** and wield ancestral relics at the shield-wall, manipulating the Glacier Bargain's power against the encroaching cold.

### Climate
**Eternal winter.** The Glacier Bargain traded summer for halted glaciers. There is no spring and no summer—only a 150-Year Ice Age (Year 475) with brief, precarious geothermal pockets of survival.

### Government
Ruled by **King-Jarl Halvar Skalvyr, called Jarn-Tand ("Iron-Tooth")** of **House Skalvyr**. He unified the settled clans by force, constructed the **Sunder-Wall**, and established the **Icechamber Syndicate** trade monopoly. The **Cleansing of the Hearth** — his state-enforced suppression of tribal Animists — is ongoing across all settled river-steads.

### The Three Pillars of Skalvyr Power
1. **The Frozen Archive** — institutional control of history, clockwork relics, glacier-mummies, and prophecy.
2. **The Icechamber Syndicate** — economic monopoly on coal, iron ore, whale oil, and mammoth hides.
3. **The Sunder-Wall** — military fortification regulating migration, taxing trade, and walling off the Øsling nomads.

## PART II: PRE-STAR MYTHRILL & THE CELESTIAL STAR-FALL

### Pre-Star Mythrill (Primordial Anima & Ancient Magic)
Mythrill was never a barren rock; it was an animistic world where native behemoths (*Jutul*, *Glacier Wyrms*, *Thrumm*), land spirits (*Landvaettir*, *Vaettir*, *Fossegrim*), and ancient races (**Fexrick**, **Myrathil**, **Neth**, **Groven**) commanded raw primordial magic. 

While ancient mortal disciplines (**Animists**, **Berserkers**, **Wardens**, **Shapers**, **Minstrels**) communed with the land's primal resonance, common folk survived strictly through forged steel, tactical engineering, black powder matchlocks, archery, and strict cultural taboos.

### The Celestial Star-Fall and the Bloodline Pacts (Year 0)
Celestial Parents (**AEX**, the Mother, and **AETHIL**, the Father) fled across the cosmos to hide their living, unhatched infant sun (**SOL**) in Mythrill's volcanic core from the cosmic predator **KETH AMAR**. Aethil sacrificed his absolute divine status to anchor the celestial barrier around the atmosphere; Aex sacrificed her divine form to entomb and protect Sol in the molten core, sealing the vault with cosmic wards and **Bloodline Pacts** with ancient mortal lineages. Each pact is a **Blood Seal**. The Sister Moon (**Selunis**) — the never-quickened moon-daughter egg — was frozen in orbit as a thermal buffer to anchor **Rime Magic**; Keth Amar nested in her unquickened shell during its approach.

### The Bloodline Pacts & the Bound
Ancient mortal lineages struck Bloodline Pacts with Aex, witnessed by Aethil. The ward holds only while each bound bloodline survives and keeps its oath. The pact changed them: the **Bound** became altered human sub-races.
- **Infused Bloodlines:** Mortal dynasties supercharged with celestial power (*Thalren*, *Skald*, *Solvarn*, *Solari*, *Astril*).
- **The Forest Exiles:** The bloodline that refused the Whisper was saved by the Sleeping Soul of Mythrill and transformed in moonlit groves into the **Florae** (**Viridian** and **Oken**) — the lineage history erased along with its name.
- **Common & Wild Folk:** Preserved traditional black powder, matchlocks, and melee combat, or drew upon the Natural Wyrd of the Sleeping Soul (*Moraine Clans*).

### The Whispered Purge, the Blind Strike and the Freezing Era (Years 300–325 – Present / Year 475)
Over three centuries, severed Blood Seals cracked Aex's aegis, each dead lineage venting a little more of Sol's warmth. From **Year 300**, unable to breach Aethil's ward, Keth Amar spewed **Predatory Wyrd** onto Mythrill and orchestrated the **Whispered Purge** — twenty-five years posing as Aethil, snapping the remaining seals through whispers, wars and engineered tragedies. In **Year 325** came the **Blind Strike** (mortal name: Blizzard's End): Keth Amar crashed into the planetary ward. The **Secret Aegis** — the hidden lineage that erased itself from history — held; Keth Amar was severely wounded and repelled into the void. The impact fractured Aex's continental ward; crystalline **Aex Shards** — the greatest of them the Seven Sundered Monoliths, one of which is the Predatory Wyrd Counterfeit — rained across the continents. Sol vented volcanic fire through Emberspire. Mythrill is now trapped in a 150-Year Ice Age (Year 475), fighting over glowing shards for warmth and fuel.

## PART III: THE FROST-TITHE & THE RIME-TOUCHED

The **Frost-Tithe** (Morð) is the metaphysical burden of the Glacier Bargain, collected on each new generation born into frost-touched bloodlines. When the glaciers were halted, a price was set: the cold extracts a heavy toll of vitality and warmth from families dwelling in the north.

### The Rime-Touched (Status, Not a Separate Race)
Children born under the cold's metaphysical shadow carry the mark of the northern pact:
- Blue-tinted skin and frosted hair.
- Frost-touched hands completely immune to thermal burns.
- Supernatural cold running in their veins.
- Slowed aging and immense endurance against sub-zero blizzards.
- Vision sensitivity and headaches when near intense heat or bright firelight.

They work the red-hot forges of the Bloodhammer Sump with bare hands, staff outer ice garrisons, and serve as Berserkers—the living testament to Nordhalla's stubborn survival.

## PART IV: THE BERSERKER SACRIFICE

### The Class
A **Berserker** is a northern warrior who has learned to channel the cold's metaphysical strain through **Blóð** (boiling adrenaline and fury). By weaponizing physiological meltdown, the Berserker pushes muscles and bone past mortal limits.

### The Ritual of Intervention
When Morð claims a mother during a difficult birth:
1. The Berserker kneels at the birthing mother's side.
2. They place both bare palms flat against her sternum.
3. They **transfer their own ember** — the Rime-Touched fire that is the Berserker's vital spark — directly into the bargain.
4. The bargain claims the Berserker's flame in place of the mother's life.
5. The mother lives. The child is born. **The Berserker dies shortly after.**

### The Ember Passes
"Transferring the blood of the berserker" means the bargain claims the Berserker's flame, and the newborn inherits the ember in their marrow. Each Berserker's sacrifice births the next generation of Berserkers. The class perpetuates itself through death.

### The Cost & Coldfire
- Berserkers die young; their bodies burn out from internal thermal shock.
- The Rage, once ignited in battle, can become self-consuming.
- A Berserker who absorbs too many Morð burdens without dying becomes a **Coldfire** — an aberration of living contradiction, half roaring flame, half creeping glacier.

### The Morð Cradle-Camps
Staffed by Berserkers who have volunteered for terminal duty. The camp near Snowcall is part-birthing-ground, part-funeral-pyre. 
- **Environmental Texture:** The camp's perimeter is fenced with charred wooden birthing-stools lashed together with frozen sinew. Iron nails are driven into the crossbeam for every birth: black iron for surviving mothers, and heat-warped copper nails for Berserkers whose embers were spent. The camp is surrounded by deep beds of gray funeral ash that never freeze.

## PART V: SVÁLGHJARTA AND THE HUNGRÍD

### The Artifact — Sválghjarta (The Swallow-Heart)
When Keth Amar tore pieces from Aex, he infused each piece with **Wyrd** — cosmic corruption. **Sválghjarta** is Aex's heart, frozen in a ring of ice deep beneath the northern glacier. It does not beat; it hungers. 

The heart feeds on **unrealized potential** — the roads not taken, the futures aborted. It is building a colossal **carcass** around itself—a shell of ice, mammoth bones, and stolen stone to protect it while it feasts. It selectively hungers for the children of Morð—those marked with dormant celestial and animist potential.

### The Founder — Sylvén & The Brother-Murder
Sylvén was a fisherman from **Hvalhavn**. While hunting wolves with his brother Úlfrekr, Sylvén missed a shot, fell into a glacial crevice, and discovered the pulsing ring of ice. The Heart whispered, promising salvation. Sylvén consumed the ring. 

When Úlfrekr returned to rescue him, Sylvén—overcome by the insatiable spiritual void—bit his brother's neck, broke his spine, and devoured him on the frozen plateau. Sylvén returned to Hvalhavn claiming wolves killed his brother and that the gods had blessed him with a holy hunger to save the north.

**Sylvén is a tragic, self-deluded prophet.** He genuinely believes the Heart chose him to deliver humanity from the cold. He believes the children he takes are being transformed rather than consumed, because facing the truth would mean admitting he murdered his brother for nothing.

### The Hungríd Hierarchy of Hunger

| Rank | Title | Who | Powers | Knowledge |
|------|-------|-----|--------|-----------|
| **The Hollow King** | Sylvén | Founder & Heart-Eater | Rime-infused; instant-freeze chest touch | Believes Heart's whispers; thinks he is saving mankind |
| **The Frostborn** | Inner Circle (5-7) | Earliest marked disciples | Rime-infused; freezing touch with effort | Know children are eaten; stay silent to retain power |
| **The Tooth** | Enforcers | Kidnappers & bounty-hunters | Enhanced strength & cold endurance | Believe children are sent to a holy deity |
| **The Hollow** | Scouts / Watchers | Town infiltrators | Stealth & mild cold resistance | Believe the sacrifice story; track pregnant mothers |
| **The Starved** | Rank and File | Marked followers | Frost-touched skin; no cold pain | Believe the children walk the ice as demigods |

### The Whispering Pine Deforestation
The Hungríd operate logging camps across the Whispering Pine to build the Heart's carcass. Every ancient pine felled destroys the forest's memory. In clear-cut zones, an eerie **acoustic dead-zone** forms where shouting produces no echo and snow patterns freeze into concentric shock-rings.

## PART VI: THE SUNDER-WALL & BORDERS

Built by King-Jarl Halvar Skalvyr to regulate trade, tax the nomad migration, and isolate the Øsling clans.
- **Inside the Wall (Skaldfjord Dal):** The **Vaegfolk** (settled Skald farmers and fishermen) under Skalvyr law.
- **Beyond the Wall (Frostfang Wastes):** The **Øsling** clans, surviving through mammoth-hunting and forbidden songs.
- **The Foundation (Historical Stratification):** The Sunder-Wall rests on the **Mammoth-Dike**—a prehistoric trench of packed glacial moraine, frozen beast marrow, and cold-iron spikes raised by primordial clans to deflect Glacier Wyrms.
- **Structural Fragility:** High taxes have driven smugglers to excavate sub-glacial tunnels beneath the wall, causing periodic tremors and wall fissures stuffed with emergency wooden wedges.

## PART VIII: PEOPLES & CASTES

### 1. The Skald
The dominant human population of Nordhalla. Hardy, pale, weathered saga-bearers. They set their dead upright in glacier ice as eternal witnesses.
- **Divisions:** Vaegfolk (settled valley dwellers), Skald Navy, Rune Keepers, Ice-Trackers, and Øsling nomads.

### 2. The Corvani (Cursed Former Humans)
Highland raiders who robbed an ancient hermit in a glacier crevice during pre-Binding famines. Cursed to become the physical embodiment of their greed: raven-winged scavengers and hoarders.
- **The Tragic Loop:** They pile shiny objects at mountain cairns (Rook’s Promontory) believing it will buy back their humanity. Because hoarding and scavenging feeds the curse, the ritual itself locks them into their cursed state.
- **The Mothers' Mystery:** Corvani females possess far fewer feathers, smaller talons, and clearer voices because they remain at the nest to trade and rear young, starving the curse of theft and greed.
- **Architecture:** Roosts built from salvaged naval ironclad rigging, anchor chains, and canvas sails.

### 3. The Øsling Clans
Highland survivors who refused the Glacier Bargain or were exiled beyond the Sunder-Wall. They hunt mammoths, navigate the Hunger Glaciers on bone skis, and sing the forbidden oral histories.

### 4. The High Neth (Snowcall Diaspora)
Immigrant Neth scholars from Bryngloom Forest who manage contract-magic and draft the Syndicate’s charters.
- **Tactile Texture:** Dressed in layered black wool stained with yellow sulfur rims at the hems; frantic scribes desperately use chilling salts to prevent their frozen ice-block ledgers from melting.

### 5. The Pale Neth (Saltgrinn Smugglers)
Reclusive, dark-adapted Neth outcasts who control the Eldøyane volcanic tidal pools. They live in salt-stained cliff huts, build fish-pens out of inverted longship hulls, and broker black-market passage.

### 6. The Astril Scholar-Quarter (Frostholm)
Quiet, alien scholars who remember the cosmic truth of Keth Amar. Burdened by guilt for failing to warn the Skaldic houses during the Star-Fall, they quietly observe the Doom-Choir and avoid speaking Keth Amar's true name.

### 7. The Fexric Slaves (The Sorte Market)
Victims of Frigga Skalvyr’s illegal maritime slave pipeline. Clockwork and Caustic Fexric labor in steam-choked sweatshops beneath Frostholm, maintaining heat-conduits and forging clockwork gear with heat-scorched spanners.

## PART IX: FACTIONS & SECRET SOCIETIES

### House Skalvyr
- **Halvar (Jarn-Tand):** King-Jarl ruling through military force and the Sunder-Wall.
- **Sigurd:** Jarl of the Archive; hoards forbidden historical texts and glacier relics.
- **Frigga:** Mastermind of the Sorte Market slave trade operating through Xardin's Hearth.

### The Icechamber Syndicate
A ruthless commercial monopoly controlling whale oil, mammoth ivory, coal, and Sunder-Wall toll collection.

### The Doom-Choir
Led by **Malakor the Archivist** in the Frozen Archive. Having mathematically calculated the world's end, the Choir chants the remaining count 24/7, refusing to permit municipal disaster aid that might interfere with their calculated cosmic timeline.

### The Unlit Veil
A clandestine network of information thieves led by **The First Liar**, dedicated to stealing suppressed histories from the Frozen Archive and distributing them to the Øsling and common folk.

### The Warden School (Secondary Seat)
Surgeons at the Frozen Archive who graft recycled industrial chains, whaling harpoon wire, and cold-iron links directly into living flesh to anchor ward-magic.

### The Animist Lineage (Theron’s Root)
Tribal practitioners who carve ancestors' names directly into living skin. Persecuted by Skalvyr inquisitors, they maintain a crumbling sanctuary at Rún.
