# Ascension Barbarian Spells & TTRPG Adaptations

This document serves as an evolving design repository of unique class abilities, talents, and passives extracted from Ascension WoW's **Barbarian** class (`class=12`). Each entry includes its original WoW context and a converted tabletop RPG (TTRPG / 5e-style VTT) mechanics adaptation to inspire custom class design in our world.

---

## 1. Core Combat & Brutality (Melee / Physical)

### Brutal Swing
* **Ascension Original**: Basic low-cooldown attack dealing physical damage to primary target and cleaving adjacent enemies.
* **TTRPG Translation**:
  * **Action Type**: Action / Weapon Attack
  * **Target / Area**: 1 target + 5ft Cleave
  * **Mechanics**: Make a melee weapon attack. On hit, deal normal weapon damage to the main target, and deal half of that damage to one adjacent creature within 5 ft of the primary target.

### Barbaric Whirl
* **Ascension Original**: Spins rapidly with all weapons equipped, striking all nearby enemies within a short radius.
* **TTRPG Translation**:
  * **Action Type**: Action (Recharge 5–6 / Resource Cost: 2 Stamina)
  * **Target / Area**: 10 ft radius centered on self
  * **Mechanics**: All creatures within 10 ft must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, they take 3d6 + Strength modifier Slashing damage (or half on a success).

### Impale
* **Ascension Original**: Viciously thrusts a heavy weapon into a target, dealing weapon damage and causing them to Bleed over time.
* **TTRPG Translation**:
  * **Action Type**: Action
  * **Mechanics**: Make a melee weapon attack. On hit, the target takes normal damage and is **Impaled** for 3 rounds. While impaled, at the start of each of their turns, the target takes 1d8 Bleed damage. The target or an adjacent ally can use an Action to pull out the weapon (ending the bleed).

### Rancor
* **Ascension Original**: Converts incoming damage or taken blows into increased attack power and furious retaliation.
* **TTRPG Translation**:
  * **Action Type**: Passive Feature / Reaction
  * **Mechanics**: Whenever you take damage from a melee attack, you gain 1 stack of **Rancor** (max 3). Each stack adds +1 to your damage rolls. When you make a weapon attack, you can consume all stacks to deal an extra +1d6 physical damage per stack.

### Decapitate / Gutspiller
* **Ascension Original**: Brutal execution finisher targeting severely wounded foes.
* **TTRPG Translation**:
  * **Action Type**: Action (Melee Attack)
  * **Mechanics**: Make a melee weapon attack against a target below half HP (50%). If the attack hits, it automatically deals maximum weapon damage + 2d10 bonus slashing damage.

### Smash & Face Smasher
* **Ascension Original**: A heavy crushing blow with two-handed bludgeons that shatters armor and dazes enemies.
* **TTRPG Translation**:
  * **Action Type**: Action
  * **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack with a heavy weapon. On hit, deals weapon damage + target's AC is reduced by 2 until the start of your next turn (Armor Shatter).

### Outrage & Rampage!
* **Ascension Original**: Consumes accumulated rage to unleash a rapid frenzy of wild multi-hits.
* **TTRPG Translation**:
  * **Action Type**: Action (Resource: Full Rage / 1/Short Rest)
  * **Mechanics**: Make 3 immediate melee weapon attacks against targets within reach. You have Disadvantage on saving throws until your next turn due to reckless abandonment.

### Wrecking Crew & Broken Bones
* **Ascension Original**: Passive enhancements granting crushing critical impact that weakens enemy damage output.
* **TTRPG Translation**:
  * **Action Type**: Passive Feature
  * **Mechanics**: Whenever you land a Critical Hit with a melee weapon, the target's physical attack damage is reduced by 1d6 for 1 minute.

## 2. Headhunting & Ranged Tactics (Spears & Axes)

### Impaling Rush & Leroy's Legacy
* **Ascension Original**: Charges toward a target enemy, pinning them and closing distance instantly.
* **TTRPG Translation**:
  * **Action Type**: Bonus Action / Movement Feature
  * **Range**: 30 ft line toward an enemy
  * **Mechanics**: Move up to your speed directly toward a target creature. If you make a melee attack immediately following this movement and hit, the target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be knocked **Prone** and restrained until the end of your turn.

### Javelin Toss & Headhunter's Spear
* **Ascension Original**: Hurls a heavy javelin at a distance, impaling and slowing the target.
* **TTRPG Translation**:
  * **Action Type**: Action (Ranged Weapon Attack)
  * **Range**: 30/60 ft
  * **Mechanics**: Range 30/60 ft. Deal 1d10 + STR Piercing damage. The target's movement speed is reduced by 15 ft until the end of their next turn due to being pinned/impaled.

### Axe Volley & Axe Twirling
* **Ascension Original**: Flings multiple axes in a cone/fan in front of the caster, slicing all enemies caught in the hail.
* **TTRPG Translation**:
  * **Action Type**: Action (Ranged AoE)
  * **Range**: 15 ft Cone
  * **Mechanics**: Hurl handaxes in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in the cone must make a DEX save (DC 8 + Prof + STR). On fail, 2d8 Slashing damage; half on success.

### Headbutt & Skull Smash
* **Ascension Original**: A sudden brutal headbutt that interrupts spellcasting and dazes the target.
* **TTRPG Translation**:
  * **Action Type**: Bonus Action (Melee Range)
  * **Mechanics**: Target a creature within 5 ft. Target takes 1d4 + STR Bludgeoning damage. If the target is concentrating on a spell, they have disadvantage on the Concentration saving throw. If not concentrating, they must succeed on a CON save or be **Dazed** (cannot take Reactions until next turn).

## 3. Ancestry, Frost & Shamanic Brews (Buffs & Support)

### Ancestral Roar
* **Ascension Original**: Unleashes a deep primordial bellow that demoralizes enemies nearby and boosts allies' resolve.
* **TTRPG Translation**:
  * **Action Type**: Action / 30 ft Emanation
  * **Mechanics**: Foes within 30 ft must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have **Disadvantage** on their next attack roll. Allies within 30 ft gain temp HP equal to your Proficiency Bonus + STR modifier.

### Trip to the Alehouse / Ancestral Keg / Cold Brew
* **Ascension Original**: Spawns or drinks a sacred ancestral brew/keg, granting damage reduction, temporary vigor, and refreshing resource levels.
* **TTRPG Translation**:
  * **Action Type**: Bonus Action (1/Short Rest)
  * **Mechanics**: Take a swig of ancestral ale. For 1 minute (or 3 rounds), you gain resistance to Non-Magical Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage, but have disadvantage on Intelligence checks for the duration.

### Hodir's Wrath / Icebound Ancestry / Frostbite
* **Ascension Original**: Channels primordial northern frost into weapons and roars, slowing enemies and dealing frost-physical hybrid damage.
* **TTRPG Translation**:
  * **Action Type**: Bonus Action / Weapon Infusion
  * **Mechanics**: For 1 minute, your melee attacks deal an extra 1d4 Cold damage. When you hit a target, their movement speed is reduced by 10 ft until the start of your next turn.

### Warband & Clanlord's Totem
* **Ascension Original**: Plants a war banner that empowers all nearby allies with increased critical strike chance and attack speed.
* **TTRPG Translation**:
  * **Action Type**: Action (Concentration, up to 1 min)
  * **Mechanics**: Plant a banner within 5 ft. While within 20 ft of the banner, all friendly creatures land critical hits on a roll of 19 or 20, and gain +10 ft to movement speed.

### Ancestral Spirit & Power of the Ancestors
* **Ascension Original**: Summons ancestral spirits to fight alongside the warrior or bolster their health pool.
* **TTRPG Translation**:
  * **Action Type**: Action (1/Long Rest)
  * **Mechanics**: Call upon an Ancestral Spirit for 1 minute. The spirit hovers near you and can use its Reaction once per round to grant an ally within 15 ft +2 AC or deal 1d6 Spirit damage to an attacking enemy.
