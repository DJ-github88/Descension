# MYTHRILL — CANON & CLASS HERITAGE BLUEPRINT

**Decision date:** 2026-09-30

**Authority:** Creator-selected principles followed by the creator's standing instruction to adopt recommended directions and complete the review without further questionnaire prompts.

**State:** Selected canon and design targets, **implementation in progress**. Foundational, ancestry core and class access/provenance passes are recorded in `LORE_IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md`; detailed transmission/consumer reconciliation, resources, and numerical heritage effects remain pending. New connective rules below are deliberate design decisions under that delegation, not claims that every game source already contains them. The original audit remains an evidentiary snapshot.

## 0. The five confirmed foundations

1. **Living Cosmos.** Native creatures exist independently of mortal belief. Folklore records experience, preserves survival knowledge, and can conceal the truth. It does not generate species. Wyrd is a distinct cosmic medium/ecology, not the source of every supernatural thing.
2. **Layered Heritage.** Distinguish a power's source, an individual's acquisition, and a culture's transmission. Inventing a discipline, founding an institution, and discovering a pre-existing phenomenon are different events.
3. **Culture-first, bounded exceptions.** Native paths need historical reasons. Outsiders need actual training, exposure, surgery, bonds, or initiation. Reserve prohibitions for incompatible states, not stereotypes such as “too pragmatic” or “too mechanical.”
4. **Shared chassis, meaningful tradeoffs.** A heritage retains its class's core resource and tactical role. It receives a narrowly bounded edge and a corresponding limitation. Heritage is neither a cosmetic label nor a second complete class.
5. **Arcanoneer: elemental grammar.** Nethien syntax, Clockwork regulation, Caustic experimentation, and Stargazer timing reach the same underlying elemental interactions through different interfaces.

**Protected prior creator choices:**

- **Gambit:** Merryn / Veldun / Caustic Fexric are its three native heritage traditions.
- **Arcanoneer:** Nethien / Clockwork Fexric / Caustic Fexric / Stargazer Astril are its four native traditions.
- **Pyrofiend:** Hollow-Solari / Waste-Solari / Clockwork Fexric are its three native traditions, belonging to two peoples.
- **Martyr:** preserve Hollow-Solari / Waste-Solari / Morgh / Ithran / Skald, including **Skald Ironclads**. The prior explicit restoration of Skald Martyrs takes precedence over stale “two peoples” summaries.

## 1. The franchise's narrative spine

> **Mythrill shelters a living sun beneath a dying world. Its peoples survive through inherited powers, dangerous discoveries, and institutions that decide who pays for survival.**

Three questions recur without producing the same answer in every class:

- **What did you inherit?** A body, an oath, a craft, a memory, a wound, a political position.
- **What can you choose?** Which tradition to enter, whose authority to recognize, which cost to accept, whom to protect.
- **Who gets to call your sacrifice necessary?** A parent, a guild, a state, a creditor, a teacher, a parasite—or yourself.

The world needs tenderness, craft, humor, discovery, and victories alongside horror. A Minstrel can make beautiful music. A Broken Mimir can enjoy freedom. A principled Fexric can repair a stranger's heat-line. Dangerous powers must not make every practitioner incapable of friendship, pleasure, or a life outside combat.

### 1.1 Author truth versus in-world claims

Every major entry must distinguish:

| Layer | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Author truth | The actual causal rule used by writers and systems | Keth Amar can lie; the Counterfeit is its instrument |
| Established observation | Reliable evidence with a defined scope | Archive augury accuracy fell in a measured cohort |
| Institutional doctrine | A group's interpretation or political position | The Doom-Choir says extinction is inevitable |
| Rumor / testimony | An attributed account, possibly mistaken | A sailor says the House has never lost |
| Deliberate unrevealed mystery | A bounded fact withheld from audiences | The identity of the Unwritten family |

Mystery is not permission for contradictory author facts. A withheld identity still has fixed properties. A later reveal must explain the evidence rather than replace it with an unrelated premise.

### 1.2 Authority and maintenance

- This blueprint specifies the selected target while reconciliation is pending.
- The reconciled world bible will own cosmology, ancestry origins, chronology, institutions, and the author-truth ledger.
- Structured class/race data will carry those approved facts into the application. Class mechanics and their executable resource engines own numerical behavior.
- Tooltips, account labels, origin cards, resource flavor, and books consume that material; they do not introduce independent canon by hardcoding it.
- Art prompts derive from anatomical and location specifications. An art document's “canonical lock” banner does not override the actual specification.
- Preserve historical audits as history. Do not silently rewrite their evidence to make old findings disappear.

## 2. Cosmology: the selected author truth

### 2.1 Native powers and the Wyrd

**Native life has its own origins.** Beasts, land spirits, sapient peoples, native gods, constructs, and alchemical creations are not all one creature layer. They exist before or independently of Keth Amar and do not vanish when someone forgets a story.

**The Sleeping Soul** is the world's primordial planetary power. It does not converse, choose champions, or personally rescue families. Its existence anchors the native world; it is not a mechanism by which mortal imagination manufactures life.

**Old Native Gods** are real, distinct powers. Their stories reflect how peoples encounter them. Mael-Zhul is not created by gambling folklore; the Quiet is not created by fear of silence. They are neither masks of Keth Amar nor automatically Ancient Cosmic Wyrdkin.

**Wyrd** is the wider cosmic medium/ecology. Keth Amar can exploit it, carry or attract organisms, manipulate channels, corrupt hosts, and generate narrower local spawn. It neither invented that ecology nor owns every entity within it. Use four bestiary provenance categories consistently:

1. Native Mythrill life/constructs, with an actual local origin.
2. Ancient Cosmic Wyrdkin, whose existence predates their arrival here.
3. Direct Keth-spawn/Wyrdspawn, produced locally through active corrupted channels.
4. Wyrd-touched natives, where corruption is a condition and the original identity remains meaningful.

**Retire “Natural Wyrd” as the authoritative generative-Loom explanation.** Retain legacy keys/references as aliases or historical-theory entries until migration is complete. “Predatory Wyrd” means Keth-associated corruption and hostile channels, not “all native magic infected.” Mechanics tagged `wyrd` are effect classifications, not proof that every tagged racial trait originated with Keth.

### 2.2 The celestial family

| Entity | Canonical identity | Limits and relationships |
|---|---|---|
| **Aex** | Sol's living celestial mother; she surrendered divine form to protect him in the core | Her fractured aegis supplies genuine shards. Martyr resonance is an echo of her sacrifice, not a verdict that a user is morally righteous |
| **Aethil** | Sol's personal celestial father, now maintaining the sky-ward | He witnesses the Bloodline Pacts and cannot abandon his ward without consequences. Universal consequence is not an impersonal replacement for this person |
| **Sol** | Living, unhatched, **conscious** infant sun in the core | He can produce heat and thermal impressions without being hatched. Entombed does not mean dead or asleep; his inability to act freely is part of the tragedy |
| **Selunis** | Sol's sister, a never-quickened celestial moon-egg frozen in orbit as a buffer | She remains dormant. Astril interpret dreams and signs; she has not literally awakened to give spoken quests. She is not a separate fallen star called Vael |
| **Keth Amar** | Intelligent cosmic star-predator; scheming, fallible, capable of deception | It followed Astril traces and cannot freely enter the warded world in its full body. It is not omniscient, not an honest universal creditor, and not the parent of all horrors |

The sky-ward and core protection are **distinct but coupled**. Keth's attack relied on the mistaken belief that the inner support had failed. The surviving Secret Aegis sustained the defense under impact. Smaller signals, infected material, lesser organisms, and breaches can cross limits that stop the full predator; “warded” does not mean hermetically closed to every meteor, refugee, or whisper.

The celestial non-intervention rule applies to celestial status and direct descent. It does not prohibit native gods from acting locally. Neither parent becomes an unlimited intervention device after paying the price: their remaining forms and duties constrain them.

### 2.4 Seals, houses, shards, and the Counterfeit

- The original public binding houses are **Thalreth, Skalvyr, Solvan, Mereval, Tesshan, Ordavan, Viridane**.
- A **Blood Seal** is a continuing living obligation. Both the oath-bearing line and its commitment matter. A surviving cadet branch or political successor can retain a surname without retaining an intact seal. Extinction is one way to break a seal; repudiation is another.
- Viridane refused Keth's summons and survived through the Green covenant. That rescue replaced the obligations of its public binding identity; its **old public seal did not remain an additional intact Aex seal**. The refusal saved the family but did not save its original ward-duty. This is a chosen connective rule, with real moral complexity, not a claim that the repository already states it.
- **The Unwritten** are a separate hidden family maintaining the sole uninterrupted Secret Aegis. Do not identify all Florae with that family. Their unrevealed identity is a protected franchise mystery; their existence, duty, and distinction from Viridane are fixed.
- **House Morrath** was elevated into Viridane's public political seat. Its native contract/regency authority can be real while its supposed original Aex pedigree is false. It was not an original Bloodline Pact signatory.
- Political seats, living seals, and large shard objects are **different counts**. Do not force a one-house/one-continent/one-shard identity.
- **The Seven Sundered Monoliths** is the accepted seven-seat census: **six genuine great Aex shards and one Counterfeit**. Lesser genuine shards also exist. There is no hidden “true seventh Monolith” acting as a cleansing key.
- Keth forged the counterfeit object. Mortal institutions fabricated or accepted its political pedigree. Those are different acts; the six houses did not manufacture Keth's physical Wyrd forgery.
- The Masked Acolytes tend the counterfeit network. **Vespera's Crown** is their stronghold, not proof that the Bryngloom-positioned false seat is physically in Nordhalla.
- The artifact is not meant to trick Keth about its own work. It tricks mortal claimants and supplies a hostile channel.
- Reassembling fragments may affect heat distribution or wound containment; it cannot substitute an artifact for a living oath. The Dawn Vigil's Reforging is an institution's dangerous project, not an automatically valid world-saving recipe.

### 2.5 The celestial attack sequence

Keth's **Whispered Purge** is the preparation: false promises, engineered wars, assassinations, accidents, and mortal atrocities break the public seals. Its **Blind Strike** is the later kinetic assault that fractures the aegis but fails to claim Sol.

Retain Sera's murdered/sacrificed child and the memory of the heirs. Their deaths were enacted by mortals under the false father's promises. Do not make Keth's full body descend through the still-held ward to eat them at the summit. A boast such as “I devoured them” can be attributed predatory rhetoric, not the authoritative mechanism that shattered Aex.

**Blizzard's End** is the mortal name for the night/site associated with the Blind Strike. A parent concealing Sol is not Keth “stealing the sun.”

## 3. Chronology, scale, and meaningful unknowns

### 3.1 Era-relative event order

| Order | Era/event | What must remain true |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Long Before** | Native peoples, gods, ecology, engineering, spirit practices and older bargains exist. Most ordinary mortals lack free spellcasting; “all mortals had zero magic” is retired |
| 2 | **The Celestial Star-Fall & Long Pacting** | The parents protect Sol; public Bloodline Pacts and the secret pact are struck. Astril arrive with offworld knowledge. Solar radiance is concealed and the Long Cold begins |
| 3 | **The Slow Cracking** | Some public seals fail through local events before the mass hostile infiltration. Heat vents follow geological networks, not a rigid one-house/one-region equation |
| 4 | **Keth's Arrival & Years of Whispers** | Hostile Wyrd channels spread; the predator impersonates Aethil, manipulates survival bargains and destroys remaining public support. Viridane's refusal/Green refuge and erasure belong here |
| 5 | **The Blind Strike / Blizzard's End** | The Secret Aegis holds. Keth is repelled; genuine shards fall; Emberspire ruptures and the first Pyrofiend crucibles form |
| 6 | **The Freezing Era** | Industrial shard use, coercive states, trade monopolies and transmitted disciplines develop. Shard power does not mean the older native world was invented at this point |
| 7 | **Late Freezing Era / campaign present** | The institutions and crises described in class dossiers coexist, with defined triggers and regional scopes |

Vaelis's First Contract predates the Star-Fall. Valerius's **Grand Nomenclature** is a later formalization of elemental combination, not the creation of all elemental power. The imported Stargazer charts can be older than the local Quiet Observatory building; do not call that building older than peoples that existed before Astril arrived.

Class discovery and institutional founding are tracked separately. Older hunting, singing, smithing, or burial does not mean Sylas, Lyris, Nesta, or Kora invented every related activity. A living modern founder does not need to be the first person in an ancient ancestry to encounter that phenomenon.

### 3.2 Time conventions

- Use era/phase labels in player prose and UI. Internal numeric sort keys may remain hidden.
- Biological lifespan ranges, ages necessary to characterize a person, distances, ritual counts, and measured details are not historical calendar dates. Preserve significant concrete detail where consistent.
- Remove conflicting Toll War/First Thermal War year ranges rather than arbitrarily choosing an obsolete numbering system. Preserve causal order: emancipation precedes later reclamation schools; the First Thermal War precedes the War of Thousand Screams; postwar reconstruction enables the Nethien Chronarch adaptation.
- Exact “three weeks until all practitioners die” events are **campaign arc triggers**, not an always-running global clock forced on every new game.
- Scene/turn rules do not silently operate every few seconds throughout ordinary life. Lunarch combat channeling accelerates its feeding cycle; ordinary low-channel life follows the slower lunar rhythm. A fatal active Pyrofiend debt-call cannot be paused by merely closing the combat UI.
- Possible futures are not automatically inhabited parallel universes. Lyra experiences possible deaths; every card override does not require a genocide of real alternate worlds.

### 4.2 Anatomical locks and inherited traits

- **Arch Mimir:** ancient heartwood/storm-glass lineage masks, including defined partial/domino coverage. Retire universal “no face has ever been seen” and sealed-porcelain locks. Mask benefits concern inherited archives and ritual anchors, not proof that beautiful faces are literally unviable.
- **Broken Mimir:** unmasked faces, demon-fae ears/scarring and personal Motes. They can wear ordinary equipment but their heritage does not demand a lineage mask. Move mask-dependent benefits/penalties out of shared inheritance; assign Mote mechanics to Broken, not every Arch.
- **Mask-making:** physical mask craft survives with Tallen. The lost Mask-Mother craft concerns special ancestral imprinting, not the ability to carve any new physical mask. Newly carved masks do not fabricate centuries of ancestral memories.
- **Mimir travel:** Arch archives/masks need care; that is not a universal death timer outside one forest. Broken Mote self-memory is not dependent on wearing an ancestral archive. Travelling practitioners require appropriate care, not an unplayable geography lock.
- **Florae:** living timber/thorn anatomy. Oken inhabit ground-floor roots and lower trunks; Viridian's Thornhollow uses intentional botanical architecture. Wild/Shorn are lifestyles, not extra subraces. Pruning does not remove Viridian's permanent contract-barbs or turn wood into human flesh. Disguise means obscured features/credentials, not perfect unaided anatomical impersonation.
- **Florae reproduction:** remove the shared claim that every youngling is planted; scope it to Oken. Viridian's thorn emergence at adolescence remains its distinct developmental rule.
- **Oken Toxicologists:** use resin/sap and cultivated reagents, not automatically Viridian thorn-blood.
- **Solari:** brown-beige flesh dusted with mineral ash; black light-absorbing eyes, burn-mark lineage, heat-adapted lungs. Basalt skin, lava fissures and smoking horns belong only to a specifically documented conversion state, especially Pyrofiend, not ordinary race anatomy. No ordinary forge-flame metabolic dependence.
- **Thaeron:** preserve the Sun-Speaker's sixty-year service; reject the ordinary three-hundred-year sovereign biography. An image of a historic figure does not appoint them current ruler.
- **Nethien bloodline eyes:** solid black pools; **Veldun:** coin-silver; **Withered:** pale grey/haunted. Race-wide summaries must not override bloodline anatomy.
- **Clean Vreken:** inherited stable Deep-Glow biology, not a purification merit badge. Their default described rust-amber light and monastic anatomy must be distinguished from watercolor palette colors. Treat addiction without turning every Marked individual into Clean or erasing a people.
- **Marked Vreken:** more intrusive Ghost-Mycelium and vulnerability to the hush; being Marked does not mean already addicted, criminal, or incapable of kindness.
- **Groven:** preserve source-specified Morgh/Ithran dimensions and silhouettes; “chunky” art style is not a license to turn short Morgh into giant-sized mechanics. Obtain size/range from the subrace definition.
- **Astril:** Stargazer/Brutish are current labels; suppression of Lumia belongs to particular disciplines/ideologies, not proof that every Brutish child is an evil jailer of a star. They see stored/internal stellar patterns under Sundrift's starless sky; a visible local constellation must not appear by convenience.
- **Myrathil:** air/water breathing, water-dependent care, species-appropriate pressure adaptation. Underwater buoyancy is not literal zero gravity. “Ancestor,” “mother,” and “grandmother” in their classes mean finder, teacher, chosen kin, or community predecessor; no biological womb is implied.

### 4.3 Nethien contract physiology and severance

**Vaelis** drafted the mortal argument; Morvane ratified the partnership and the magical First Contract grew into living heartwood. **Valerius** later formalized elemental combination as the Grand Nomenclature. Preserve both people and their distinct accomplishments.

The Well's faithful limited use and raw over-drawing explain the Nethien/Veldun split. Withered are a later history of deliberate severance. Their purported “ancestral sin” is pact-authority condemnation, not author truth that all their souls were stolen by a separate catastrophe.

**Pact law has scope.** Nethien/Veldun cannot knowingly make false pact-enforced assertions; withholding, conditional statements and literal loopholes are different. Morvane's First Contract does not automatically govern every unrelated cosmic patron or every nation's civil law.

**Withered:** no preservation, trance sustenance, Archive-Tether, Contractual Lock, or Fraying track from the pact. They eat, drink, sleep and slowly age. Nethien jurisdiction cannot invoke their removed contract signatures; that does not make them invisible to ordinary eyes, universally immune to magic, or exempt from death. Their trained null arts can defeat **First Contract-keyed** detection, not every sensor in existence. Ordinary trade outside that jurisdiction remains possible.

Use **one Fraying track**, not two:

| Stage | Canonical condition |
|---|---|
| 0 | Preservation stable |
| 1–2 | The pact loosens; brief clarity/freedom with the recorded early benefits |
| 3–4 | Thinning, social/thread difficulties and reduced tether tolerance |
| 5–6 | Fading: preservation fails, ordinary bodily needs return |
| 7–8 | Memory/skill erosion |
| 9 | Meaningful restoration effort or deliberate Severing |
| 10 | Dissolution |

Remove the competing per-breach Charisma stack/three-breach “Fading” model. Recovery means ten substantive, verifiable obligations, not ten trivial receipts manufactured in one scene; the stage-nine Return retains its sustained-compliance requirement. Fraying applies to pact-bound Nethien/Veldun, not Withered.

**Archive distance** is a separate cause of preservation failure: the existing travel-distance boundary and 3/7/14-day escalation remain. Returning to an authentic copy and the documented recovery trance resolves distance-induced failure, not unresolved deliberate breaches. Shared symptoms do not stack twice. Range is measured against ordinary regional travel, not enlarged by a temporary speed buff.

## 5. Heritage design and implementation contracts

### 5.1 Three layers, four access states

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Power source | Actual phenomenon/entity/material; no unsupported generic “cosmic” explanation |
| Acquisition | What happened to the individual, what training/bond makes it usable |
| First discovery | The earliest established encounter or practice, with attribution |
| Institutional founding | Who organized the modern discipline; distinguish discovery from school formation |
| Cultural transmission | Teacher/contact/refugee/war/trade vector for every native/adopted branch |
| Heritage edge | One narrowly bounded class-specific advantage |
| Heritage cost | A genuine corresponding limitation, not a cost the base class already pays with no additional consequence |
| Common chassis | Resource identity, baseline kit and class-wide risk remain recognizable |
| Institution/leader/site | Registered IDs and a coherent current state |
| Knowledge status | Author truth, observation, doctrine, rumor, or bounded mystery |

Access states:

- **N — Native:** an established original/cultural tradition.
- **A — Adopted native:** a defined transmitted tradition now taught in the receiving community. Some A rows below are deliberate additions selected by this review and must be written/implemented.
- **E — Exceptional:** a specific individual route, with qualification and recorded consequences; no automatic native bonus or new default branch.
- **B — Incompatible state:** not compatible while that actual bodily/metaphysical condition persists. This is narrower than a permanent species stereotype. A transformation can create a genuinely different state, not a free checkbox bypass.

Every outsider uses the **class-specific acquisition route** in its dossier. “There is no native teacher here” is a training barrier. “The First Contract preserves this neural identity against the required organism” is a possible state barrier. They are not the same field.

### 5.2 Rules for every variant prototype

The tables below are **design contracts**, not claims of shipped rules or new numerical balance approval:

- One heritage edge and its paired cost; no extra complete resource pool.
- Conditional edges require an actual checkable condition: position, declared recipe, medium, equipment, named bond, or current state.
- No permanent unlimited immunity, infinite range, automatic resurrection, guaranteed prophecy, or “nearly omnipotent” home-territory power.
- No heritage negates its class-wide collapse, resource cap, death-call, or companion dependency.
- Baseline class functionality survives travel outside the favored environment. A terrain-specific edge can disappear; the entire class cannot become useless there.
- Exact tuning uses the existing engine's units, existing conditions, and a written effect schema. Benefit/cost events must be emitted, displayed and tested before copy advertises them as available.
- Origin, heritage, and combat specialization are different axes. An **Augur — Harbinger path** is not the **Harbinger class**. Native Skald Martyrs use the Ironclad method; learning a Witness method is an explicit extra training route. Non-Skald furnace training is likewise not implied by a selector label.

## 8. Languages, scripts, and modular origins

### 8.1 One language model, not competing canon inventories

- Keep the catalogue entries as the starting census, with language versus collection distinctions made explicit. Do not hardcode the display count after the registry changes.
- **Astril defaults:** Wayfarer's Cant and **Echo-Song**. “Lumian” is an archival/colloquial reference to their celestial/Lumia-related speech, not an undefined additional language grant. Synod-Speak is their learned formal institutional register; Veilspeech covers spirit communication where actually learned.
- Default racial languages describe the upbringing of an adult player character, not instant newborn universal literacy. Ulvir's unusual early speech is compatible with later learned Wayfarer's Cant/Tide-Speech.
- Grants use subrace overrides **or** base-race inheritance. An absent override never means silently deleting Wayfarer's Cant, Fexric, Thorn-Song, Span-Speech, or Echo-Song from a selected character.
- Known speech normally includes its ordinary associated literacy as the existing game expects. Special ciphers, professional scripts and magical decoding require their own actual knowledge; ordinary language fluency does not confer every covert key or supernatural reading technique.
- Learned secret/exotic tongues require an origin reason—teacher, initiation, institutional access or an actual appropriate covenant. A racial upbringing or a relevant background can supply it. Not every exotic tongue requires an invented supernatural debt.
- **Grove-Sign** means the guarded code of grove initiates; it is not a promise that a missing Druid base class exists. **Skin-Tongue** concerns actual shape-changing communities/creatures, not a new eleventh playable ancestry.
- The dead scholarly scripts (**Archive-Writ**, **Span-Runes**, **Keth-ash**) are learned one at a time like any other tongue; there is no bundled "all ancient languages" grant that solves every dead-script mystery at once.
- Language magic gives **temporary comprehension/speech according to the actual effect**. It need not require permanent fluency in the unknown language; it does not permanently teach the language, unlock a secret cipher key, or transmit every cultural referent.
- Unknown legacy strings remain explicitly unmapped until a real correspondence is confirmed; never silently replace them all with Common.

### 8.3 Modular character origins

**Masked Acolyte background** supplies actual Crown work, relationships, cult access and a taught register. **Hollow Sight** is a separate acquired parasitic graft: a former Acolyte can retain it; an uninitiated errand-runner does not automatically possess it. This is a selected creation addition, not an existing implemented background.

Hollow Sight's design contract:

- One narrow, range-limited occult-perception use, with a defined action/recharge and visible state.
- When used, the counterfeit channel can relay what the graft actually observes to its patron; it does not disclose the whole world, every thought or every hidden seal.
- It neither grants Morvane's full sight nor automatically makes the character loyal to Keth. Preserve agency and free will.
- Acquire the graft through an explicit origin or later story event; do not put it into every race's shared traits.

**Rime-Touched/Frostbound** are lineage conditions/origins with defined biological/social effects. They do not add a new base race or replace a person's cultural heritage. A treatment can prevent future harm without retroactively deleting living people, their history or their culture.

### 8.4 Economy and ordinary competence

The **hearth-winter** is an accounting unit for a household's winter provision, not a new class resource. Existing metal denominations and regional barter can settle bills quoted in that unit. A shard can provide continuing heat yet still be owned, taxed, fade under use and be vulnerable to theft; “cannot be besieged” is not universal invulnerability.

Ordinary crafts remain ordinary possibilities: a Fexric can brew, a Skald can sing, a Thalren can hunt, and a Withered can trade. Possessing a profession does not grant its corresponding specialized class initiation. Conversely, cultural identity does not make an unrelated common skill physically impossible.

## 9. The causal weave: connected stories without one universal villain

### 9.1 Five overlapping story systems

| Story system | Concrete cause / pressure | Connected people, classes and places | What characters can actually change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The warmth economy** | Concealed solar radiance, failing native machinery, fading/hoarded shards and taxed routes | Fexric/Vurath, Tessen keeps, Icechamber/Steam-Line monopolies, Crusader and Pyrofiend forge needs | Restore a heat-line, change access, break a concession, build maintained infrastructure |
| **The memory commons** | Damaged native channels, hostile mimicry and institutions monopolizing records | Morvane/Root-Veil, Nethien archives, Mimir masks/Motes, Florae oral history, Animists and Revenants | Verify a voice, rescue a record, expose a false identity, preserve a community without accepting its ruler |
| **Dangerous discovery** | Reproducible but costly interfaces, incompatible methods, corrupted evidence | Proving Grounds/Arcanoneers, Chronarch engines, Augur observations, Harbinger rifts, Distillery chemistry | Compare methods, constrain a failure, share a protocol, decide who owns and pays for a repair |
| **Consent versus procurement** | States and guilds treating practitioners as equipment | Martyr brigades, Wardens, Shaper retrievals, Clockwork licenses, Dawn Vigil commands | Free a cohort, establish voluntary teaching, expose a contract, refuse a harmful deployment |
| **Cosmic ingress** | Keth's counterfeit channels plus independently motivated Wyrdkin and organisms | Acolytes/Hollow Sight, Voice cells, Listener Below, parasite broods, Astril refugee knowledge | Break an anchor, distinguish organisms from patrons, stop a local return, investigate the Counterfeit |

Ecological damage and hostile corruption can amplify each other without sharing one author. The Root-Veil can recoil because of drainage, disease, misuse or intrusion. A guild can cause a crisis through hoarding. The same observed symptom does not prove Keth deliberately caused every factory failure.

### 9.2 Four franchise-scale dramatic knots

1. **The Proving Grounds:** a shared craft has four interfaces and no single cultural owner. Its successes depend on collaboration; its disasters expose how liability is passed to people who did the dangerous work. This links Arcanoneer, Spellguard, Clockwork/Caustic politics, and Nethien law without making them one class.
2. **The failed span:** a Groven ancestor is physical infrastructure, a sacred memory and a priced route. A Chronarch delays collapse; a Martyr carries evacuees; a Warden holds a beast; a Gambit tries to finance repair; a Shaper reclaims the biology once used to make servitors. Their answers differ, and one rescue can shift the political balance.
3. **The returned voice:** an Animist hears familiar words, an Augur records an impossible prediction, a Revenant's anchor changes its answer, and an Inquisitor cannot classify the signature. Nethien contracts, Broken self-memory and Astril heritage provide different tests. A cosmic mimic and a corrupt bureaucracy can both benefit without being the same actor.
4. **The Reforging:** a Crusader's real radiance supports a possibly disastrous state project. An Ash-Witness protects a claimed Pyrofiend sibling; a Fexric governor buys time; the cult supplies a convincing false shard. The conflict is not “faith versus science”—it is evidence, duty, love, coercion and who gets to spend somebody else's life.

### 9.3 Expansion boundaries

- **Nordhalla remains the sole launch region.** Other native traditions appear through distinct residents, outposts, envoys or visitors. A pickable origin is not proof its people originated in Nordhalla.
- The initial region can tell complete stories with local victories; it is not only a prologue to an unavoidable final boss.
- Broad cosmic revelations require evidence and an earned arc. They must not turn every Old God into Keth, every class into a pact debtor, or every native creature into a disguised invader.
- Place catastrophic conclaves/collective calls on explicit campaign milestones. Keep the ordinary setting viable between those arcs.
- Protect the Unwritten identity, the Voice's full agenda, Selunis's awakening conditions and Scathrach's ultimate revenge as bounded reveal spaces. They already have fixed limits and causal roles; “unrevealed” does not authorize future contradictory origins.
- Body, culture, civic allegiance, personal morality and class initiation are distinct. A brutal institution does not make a whole subrace evil at birth.
- Maps use consistent directional coordinates. A sunless sky does not erase north/south from cartography; the loss of local stars changes navigation, not geometry.

## 12. Reconciliation plan and acceptance conditions

### 12.1 Preserve data compatibility

- Keep canonical base class IDs, race/subrace save keys, `LoreLink` IDs and resource aliases. Display corrections do not require destructive save renaming.
- Record full class ID + heritage/tradition + origin qualification separately. Cultural prefixes are not new resources or base classes.
- Existing saved unusual combinations are retained with explicit legacy/exception qualification; do not delete characters because the new native matrix changes a picker.
- Preserve custom lineages and campaign edits. A shipped default canon change is not permission to rewrite a GM's custom world.
- Normalize legacy `dexterity`/`wisdom` authoring references to Agility/Spirit through explicit compatibility handling. Show a before/after derived breakdown; never silently apply the same bonus twice.
- Migrate language grants without removing legitimately learned languages. Recompute inherited grants from the selected race/subrace and maintain their source provenance.
- A title change, absent field or malformed variant object must not grant a permissive fallback class/body match. An E route is a recorded class-specific qualification.
