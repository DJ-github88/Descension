# Seelie Accord — 4 Candidate Directions (for user pick)

The Seelie lore has been parked pending your direction. The Revel entry currently uses "fae courts" generically to sidestep the issue. Below are 4 distinct directions, each with: core idea, lore implications, mechanical implications, ties to existing canon, and pros/cons.

Pick one to develop, or specify your own direction.

---

## Direction A — EXTINCT PRE-BREACH COURT (the "lost golden age" model)

**Core idea:** The Seelie Accord was a real, unified fae court that existed before the Breach. It was wiped out / dissolved during the cataclysm. Modern fae are scattered remnants with no central authority. The Revel's "Seelie celebration" origin is a literal echo of an actual tradition that no longer exists.

**Lore implications:**
- Pre-Breach fae society was unified under a single court (the Seelie)
- The Breach destroyed the court's structure (broke the fae-contracts that held it together)
- Modern fae entities (Pooka, Bri-Yrn, the Revel puppets) are remnants/echoes/hermits
- No current "King/Queen of the Seelie" — the throne is empty
- Sets up a potential quest hook: heirs / restoration / artifacts of the old court

**Ties to existing canon:**
- Bri-Yrn ("the Grimm-Mother") becomes one of the last surviving pre-Breach fae
- The Florae fae-contract (with Bri-Yrn as broker) becomes a relic of the old Seelie framework
- The Revel's "Seelie celebration" origin reads literally
- Connects to the Watcher/Morvane boundary (the Seelie existed *before* the current cosmic order)

**Mechanical implications:** None for current data. Future content could include "Seelie relic" items, "last of the Seelie" NPCs.

**Pros:** Clean, bittersweet, fits the post-apocalyptic tone of Mythrill
**Cons:** Removes the Seelie as a current faction (can't be a present-day political force)

---

## Direction B — SPLINTERED INTO MULTIPLE FACTIONS (the "shattered court" model)

**Core idea:** The Seelie Accord was the original fae court, but it shattered during the Breach into multiple competing factions. Several groups today claim Seelie heritage — the Revel puppets, the Fair Folk hermits (Bri-Yrn), the Pooka, possibly others. No single faction is the "true" heir.

**Lore implications:**
- Multiple present-day fae factions each with their own claim
- Each faction interpreted the Seelie tradition differently
- Political intrigue possible between factions
- Allows for "true Seelie" vs "corrupted Seelie" vs "splinter Seelie" faction play

**Ties to existing canon:**
- Bri-Yrn as broker of the Florae contract = one Seelie splinter faction's work
- The Revel = corrupted echo of one Seelie tradition
- The Pooka = another Seelie remnant that "stayed playful"
- Sets up future faction conflicts (Bri-Yrn vs other fae leaders)

**Mechanical implications:** Could support a "fae faction reputation" system in future

**Pros:** Rich, supports ongoing storylines, multiple faction hooks
**Cons:** More complex; requires defining multiple fae factions; risks diluting focus

---

## Direction C — WYRD-CORRUPTED CURRENT COURT (the "dark Seelie" model)

**Core idea:** The Seelie Accord still exists as a unified court, but it has been Wyrd-corrupted. It's now an antagonistic force — the dark fae court that makes bargains aligned with Keth-Amar's interests. Mortals who deal with the modern Seelie are dealing with something poisoned.

**Lore implications:**
- The Seelie Court is a present-day political power
- Its current state is corrupted/antagonistic (parallel to how the Wyrd corrupted the land)
- Sets up the Seelie as a recurring threat / rival power
- The pre-Breach Seelie was the "good" version; the current is the "fallen" version

**Ties to existing canon:**
- Bri-Yrn could be one of the few uncorrupted fae (a hermit who fled the corrupted court)
- The Revel becomes the corrupted Seelie's weaponized celebration
- Strong thematic parallel to the Cult of Forgotten Shadow (humans who serve Keth-Amar) — Seelie could be the fae equivalent
- The Wyrd's corrupting influence gets a major faction-level example

**Mechanical implications:** Seelie could be a major antagonist faction with bargains that always have a hidden cost

**Pros:** Strong antagonist potential; high stakes; ties into Wyrd corruption theme deeply
**Cons:** Darker tone; reduces fae to "enemy" role; limits fae-as-ally stories

---

## Direction D — MYTH / NEVER EXISTED (the "mortals misremembered" model)

**Core idea:** "Seelie" was always just a story mortals told about the fae — a romanticized projection of unified authority that never actually existed. There was never a single fae court. The Revel's "Seelie celebration" origin is itself a corrupted myth, a mortal misunderstanding of fae nature that the Wyrd then literalized.

**Lore implications:**
- The fae were always diverse, decentralized, individualistic
- Mortal cultures invented "the Seelie" to make sense of fae encounters
- The Wyrd can literalize beliefs — so the mortal myth of "Seelie celebration" became the Revel
- Bri-Yrn is just a powerful old fae, not a remnant of any court

**Ties to existing canon:**
- The Revel's "Seelie celebration" line becomes a mortal-belief echo, not a historical fact
- Connects to the Wyrd's nature (manifests fears/beliefs — see gref, gambrel, etc.)
- Removes the need for a unified fae political structure
- Emphasizes that "the world's beliefs shape reality" theme

**Mechanical implications:** None direct. Future content could explore "mortal myths that became real"

**Pros:** Clever subversion; ties into the Wyrd's belief-manifesting nature; simplifies the fae political landscape
**Cons:** Removes an entire faction's worth of story potential; may feel like a "letdown" to players who hoped for Seelie content

---

## My recommendation

**Direction A (Extinct Pre-Breach Court)** — best fit for the world's tone. Mythrill is a post-Breach world where everything ended 800 years ago. An extinct golden-age fae court fits that frame better than a present-day faction. It also:
- Preserves Bri-Yrn's role as "the last / one of the last" — gives her melancholic weight
- Lets the Revel be a literal echo without complications
- Doesn't require defining new fae political structures
- Leaves room for future "rediscovery" content if you ever want it

Direction C is the strongest alternative if you want a present-day antagonist faction. Direction B is best if you want maximum story density. Direction D is the most "literary" but reduces story potential.
