## 1. COSMIC ENTITIES (6 + Dead Moon)

- **The Warden** — NOT a god/person/force. The *language reality speaks*, the grammar of consequence. No will, no agenda, no preference. Every bargain has a price; the Warden doesn't choose the price, the price simply *is*. Keth-Amar operates WITHIN the Warden's framework and has never broken a bargain.
- **Keth-Amar** — A cosmic predator that hunts stars in their vulnerable Deepening cycle. Ancient, patient, CUNNING (schemes, studies prey). Offered SOL'S OWN WARMTH deceptively (not its own energy). Bitter that the 7th heir (Viridane) never agreed. Cannot fully access Sol.
- **Sol** — A star/divine being, vulnerable in its Deepening cycle. Bound beneath Sundale, wrapped in the corpse of its firstborn Aex. Did NOT consent but it was done to save it. Suspended twilight / torpor. Cannot rebirth/die/scream — it endures.
- **Aex** — Sol's firstborn, being of pure stellar radiance. WILLINGLY sacrificed at the Binding. Flayed alive by House Solvan (blade of crystallized starlight); hide woven into the binding seal. **Consented to the clean Binding (7 houses/7 signatures) — did NOT consent to the Breach.** He sang during the flaying (Sol's frequency); agony started at the BREACH, not the Binding. Now conscious, "lynched" across the vault. The 7 Monoliths are different parts of his body. The scream: 65 pulses declining 40%→0%. **Now silent — not dead, LISTENING.** The knife shattered when the seal completed (price paid in full).
- **The Watcher in the Mist / The Morvane (the Keeper)** — ONE entity perceived differently. Frostwood: the Watcher (whisper in mist, fae). Bryngloom: the Keeper (death-boundary entity that accepted Neth's First Contract). Boundary between life/death, memory/oblivion. Older than Keth-Amar's interest. World's conscience — impartial, all-knowing, QUIESCENT (rarely acts). Did NOT outmaneuver Keth-Amar — acted at the last possible moment for Viridane, didn't know if it would work. **v4**: Hid House Morrath's false shard in the deep bogs of the Bryngloom (folding it into forgotten memory; now CANNOT find it either — the erasure worked too well), AND sheltered Viridane's true seventh fragment in the Frostwood moonlit groves with the Florae — but now struggles to locate the exact grove where the true fragment sleeps, a splinter in its own territory it cannot extract without unmaking the protection that hides it. Cannot fight Keth-Amar directly. Fracturing under pressure. Does NOT serve the Warden, NOT answerable to Keth-Amar.
- **Scathrach the Ashen Sovereign** — A fragment of Aex's flayed hide that fell into Emberspire's deepest vent DURING THE BINDING (not the Breach). Corrupted by leaking Wyrd, grew sentient. For centuries Keth-Amar's emissary, then REJECTED the corruption (still Wyrd-touched/monstrous but has its own will). Now DESPISES Keth-Amar. Calls in Pyrofiend debts to hoard power to wound Keth-Amar back. Aex's vengeful ghost. Sealed the vent from within.
- **The Dead Moon** — NOT a moon; a DORMANT STAR / ancient sleeping deity. Unconscious for eons (withdrew awareness; shell remains). What sent it to slumber is UNKNOWN — NOT Keth-Amar (arrived later). Largest fragment fell in Frostwood's moonlit groves; fae nurtured it; lunar parasites hatched from it (Lunarch class bonds with these). Keth-Amar NESTED in the corpse-star (perch to study Sol), not laid by/connected to it.

1. **Wyrd-Taint** — passive corruption in the land (blackened flora, ash-taste).
2. **Wyrd-Wisp** — manifest fear (moving shadows, whispers) — felt, not animated.
3. **Wyrd-Spawn** — animated nightmare monsters, unique per region's fears.
4. **Wyrd-Channel** — Keth-Amar's direct attention (boss-tier: Wyrd-priests, Monolith guardians, surrendered cult leaders).
The Wyrd is Keth-Amar's AGRICULTURE — it seeds the soil and waits.

## 3. SEAL & BREACH TIMELINE
- **The Deepening** (capital D) = Sol's vulnerability cycle that Keth-Amar exploited. "deepening" (lowercase) = universal star cycle.
- **The Binding (Year 3):** Sol enters Deepening; Keth-Amar arrives. 7 noble houses go to the Warden. Price = Aex, willing. Solvan wields the blade. Seal absolute: 7 bloodlines, 7 signatures, 7 heirs. Knife shatters.
- **Corruption Years (Years 3–11):** Keth-Amar cannot breach, whispers 8 years. Offers Sol's warmth deceptively. Works on houses individually.
- **The Breach (Year 11):** 6 of 7 heirs agree; march to peaks; consumed as vessel-keys. 7th heir (Viridane) never agreed — Watcher reached them first, they fled south. Seal CRACKED but did not shatter. Emberspire erupts. **Seal split into 8 stones (v4 ratified 25 Jul 2026):** 6 true corrupted Aex fragments for the houses that broke, 1 false shard fabricated by those 6 to cover Viridane's vacant seat (House Morrath — no binding power), and 1 true seventh fragment for Viridane (uncorrupted, never breached). The world officially counts 7 because the false shard substitutes for Viridane's seat and the true seventh is hidden and forgotten.
- **Partial Seal (Year 11 → present):** Seal neither whole nor broken; Keth-Amar pressed against it, seeping through cracks. 6 houses erase Viridane, elevate House Morrath as substitute 7th. Fog = Watcher's grief/protection (scholars disagree). Erasure protects: to remember Viridane is to leave a thread Keth-Amar follows.
