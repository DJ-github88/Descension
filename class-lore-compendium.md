# MYTHRILL CLASS LORE COMPENDIUM

## Complete Lore Reference for All 21 Playable Classes

> **Status:** Canonical Master Lore Reference (475-Year Epoch Master Timeline).  
> **Master Framework:** Every class discipline draws power from the 8 primal magics (Ember, Blight, Sacred, Arcane, Storm, Rime, Primal, Wyrd), glowing Aex Shard catalysts, ancestral blood pacts, or mechanical engineering.

---

## CLASS HERITAGE TAXONOMY

Every class discipline in Mythrill is rooted in one of four distinct historical and physical origins:

1. **Pre-Binding Classes (Traditional / Mortals):** Disciplines forged when mortals had zero magic. Built on cold-forged steel, black-powder matchlocks, bear traps, archery, and anatomical conditioning (*Berserker*, *Apex*, *Minstrel*, *Warden*, *Toxicologist*).
2. **Celestial Bargain Classes (Infused Houses):** Elemental control granted through the blood pact struck with the celestial guardians Aex and Aethil (*Spellguard*, *Arcanoneer*, *Chronarch*, *Shaper*, *Crusader*).
3. **Vreken Extortion (Blight Magic):** The fungal cave-houses extorted divine desperation for raw power, receiving Blight magic that rotted their noble bloodlines into sulfurous bogs (*Blight-Weaving and Decay disciplines*).
4. **Cosmic Collision Classes (Post-Keth Amar):** Forged when the void predator Keth Amar shattered the celestial shell at Blizzard's End:
   * **Pyrofiend:** Magma-blood pacts forged with Scathrach in the volcanic vents.
   * **Harbinger:** Doom-arithmetic converting reality tears into kinetic force.
   * **Plaguebringer:** Void decay grafted directly into living bacterial hosts.
   * **Revenant:** Souls anchored between volcanic embers and frozen stasis.
   * **False Prophet:** Psychic channels tuned to the screaming silence of dead stars.
   * **Gambit:** Stolen luck and severed probability threads wagered against maritime spirits.
   * **Lunarch:** Cold-starlight parasites nesting inside the bone marrow.
   * **Martyr:** Sympathetic pain-channels converting physical trauma into holy shields.
   * **Inquisitor:** Occult investigators using silver needles and bound Wyrd mimics.
   * **Animist:** Walking archives carrying the physical mutations of three dead traditions.
   * **Augur:** Gutter seers carving fresh animal entrails to read the next ten seconds of combat.

# ANIMIST

**Origin:** Three centuries ago, three survivalists from different corners of the world developed identical blood-magic by accident. In the southern plains, an Ordan scout sat in the dirt for three seasons until calcified bone spurs tore through his elbows, tuning his body to wind spirits. In the northern fungal bogs, a Vreken herbalist inhaled glowing spore dust to hear her dead mother’s voice. In the frozen archives, a Skald scholar carved clockwork equations into his ribs. When the three crossed paths at a trading post, their bone spurs vibrated to the same acoustic frequency. They realized their separate traditions were dialects of a single language spoken by the dead.

**Identity:** A walking archive of three dead cultures, etched in jagged scar tissue and calcified bone spurs. Animists do not chant incantations; their bodies violently mutate. Sharp bone totems erupt through their forearms. Phosphorescent glyphs ignite across their skin, and ancestral voices scream through continuous waking hallucinations. Every shred of borrowed power accelerates Spirit Erosion—the more ancestral resonance you channel, the faster the spirits devour your sanity.

**Role in World:** Living siege anchors and wilderness guides. Animists drive heavy bone totems into the dirt, lace defensive perimeters with glowing runic nets, and call down curses on enemy lines. Frontier outposts pay gold for their warding rites, but civilian towns bar their gates against them: the bone spurs, skin glyphs, and loud arguments with invisible ghosts terrify common folk.

**Cultural Affiliation:** Practiced by Ordan steppe-riders, Vashir crystalline refugees, Clean Vreken gatherers, Skald runic scholars, and Velun contract-jurists.

*Six Cultural Variants:*
* **Ordan ("The Steppe-Throat"):** Sings ancestral voices through double-tone throat singing, weaving ghost-voices into migration horsehair.
* **Vashir Astril ("The Heritage-Conduit"):** Channels the biological memories of a shattered alien world preserved in crystalline blood cells.
* **Clean Vreken ("The Spore-Inhaler"):** Inhales bioluminescent cave spores, allowing ancestral memories to ride fungal dust into the lungs.
* **Marked Vreken ("The Debt-Bound"):** Summons specific ancestors whose unpaid bank loans still accrue interest against the living family.
* **Skald ("The Rune-Keeper"):** Carves the names of dead warlords into scar tissue, using raised skin as a permanent dwelling for the spirit.
* **Velun Neth ("The Ledger-Summoner"):** Files formal bureaucratic citations in high court to requisition ancestral aid as an outstanding legal claim.

**Fatal Flaw / Crisis:** Channelling 15+ Resonance causes physical collapse: fire burns twice as hot against their skin, conventional medical healing fails, and demanding spirits tear 1d6 Wyrd damage from their marrow every turn. Today, the three ancestral dialects are drifting out of alignment; young Animists trying to speak all three simultaneously suffer catastrophic brain hemorrhages.

**Founder(s):** Kael the Herd-Runner, Nyssa the Herbalist, and Theron the Scholar—all long dead. Current leader: Convenor Sera Three-Scars, the only surviving practitioner capable of holding all three ancestral dialects in her throat at once, a feat that is actively rotting her lungs.

# APEX

**Origin:** Founded during the early decades of the freeze by Sylas, a frontier scout who sliced open his own eardrums to track invisible predators through the freezing fog. By surrendering his hearing, Sylas trained his nerves to feel the ground shudder when a beast took a breath fifty paces away. He hunted a mist-stalker through the ironwood for seven days, matching its breathing until he drove a shadow-glaive through its heart.

**Identity:** A ruthless pack hunter who pairs a heavy bladed glaive with a trained war beast. The Apex fights without wasted movement, slipping through enemy flanks while their hunting beast tears open throats. Through the Mark system, the Apex marks targets for coordinated pack strikes—building lethal momentum not through brute force, but through relentless flanking pressure.

**Role in World:** The primary vanguard against wilderness horrors. Frontier logging outposts and wooden wall garrisons hire Apex rangers to track maneaters and scout through blinding blizzards. They communicate strictly through hand signs and weapon taps, rarely speaking or entering heated taverns.

**Cultural Affiliation:** Practiced by the wood-carving Mimir and northern Skald rangers.

*Seven Cultural Variants:*
* **Masked Mimir ("The Mask-Hunter"):** Uses a carved wooden face-mask to anchor their sanity while their senses range miles through the mist.
* **Woven Mimir ("The Sentinel-Tracker"):** Treats every patrol as a live combat watch along the ironwood palisades.
* **Unwoven Mimir ("The Fog-Walker"):** Dissolves their physical silhouette into cold vapor to ambush beasts from inside the mist.
* **Skald ("The Glacier-Stalker"):** Reads cracked glacier ice like a book, tracking beast weight across packed snowdrifts.
* **Clean Vreken ("The Glow-Trailer"):** Follows glowing fungal trails along subterranean root networks.
* **Marked Vreken ("The Mycelium-Scent"):** Uses fungal filaments beneath their skin as a physical tripwire network to feel prey moving overhead.
* **Ordan ("The Steppe-Scent"):** Tracks game across open grasslands by reading crushed steppe-grass and thermal wind currents.

**Fatal Flaw / Crisis:** The freezing fog has begun acting with predatory intelligence. Beast packs are actively erasing their tracks, and Unwoven scouts who dissolve into the vapor are vanishing entirely. Sylas has realized that the fog-horrors are no longer fleeing his hunters—they are hunting him.

**Founder(s):** Sylas the Silent—alive, completely deaf, and commanding the Silent Hunt through hand-signals from deep inside the ironwood forest. Notable figure: Maeve of the Canopy, who led the first column of civilian refugees through the memory-eating fog.

# ARCANONEER

**Origin:** Four centuries ago, an archivist named Valerius realized that casting raw elemental magic was rotting the minds of elven sorcerers. To tame the volatile energy, he wrote spells as rigid, airtight legal contracts with balanced penalty clauses. To absorb the brutal mechanical kickback of these legal incantations, he bolted a heavy pig-iron cylinder directly into his forearm bones, inventing the first mana-cannon.

**Identity:** A cold, calculating contract-sniper whose spells are legal decrees fired through a heavy forearm cannon. Arcanoneers do not cast through emotion or raw faith; they dictate terms. Using **Immutable Syntax**, they enforce precise magical clauses that lock enemies in place or detonate kinetic charges. Firing the heavy cannon fractures ribs from recoil and pins their boots into the dirt, but delivers absolute kinetic devastation.

**Role in World:** The ruling jurists and artillery elite of Atropolis. Arcanoneers sit at the head of high courts, arbitrate maritime trade contracts at deep-water docks, and shatter charging war-beasts with heavy cannon broadsides during fortress sieges.

**Cultural Affiliation:** Practiced almost exclusively by the silver-blooded Velun Neth.

*Three Cultural Variants:*
* **Velun Neth ("The Contract-Caster"):** The classical tradition. Every spell is a filed lawsuit, crystallizing volatile mana into razor-sharp legal shrapnel.
* **Kessen Neth ("The Clause-Weaver"):** Tracks the glowing silver threads of obligation connecting debts between living souls, snapping the threads to deal kinetic shock.
* **Drun Neth ("The Severed-Caster"):** Outlaws who burned their own names off the grand ledger. They cast by quoting broken contracts, causing volatile scar-shards to detonate in erratic explosions.

**Fatal Flaw / Crisis:** The Grand Legal Ledger is collapsing. The entity that underwrites their contracts has begun rejecting clauses without explanation. Mana-cannons are jamming in mid-combat, and legal backfires are turning the casters' blood into jagged glass.

**Founder(s):** Valerius the Scriptor—whose consciousness fused into the legal archive centuries ago. Current leader: Ledger-Prime Vel-Otharen, whose flesh has crystallized into seventy percent solid mana-glass. Notable figure: Kaelen the Unbroken, who held the harbor docks against an assault of sea-serpents.

# AUGUR

**Origin:** Founded during the first freezing eclipse by Cassia, a mountain star-watcher whose bronze telescopes frosted over when the sun went dark. Desperate for answers, she butchered a sacrificial glacier-elk with a scaling knife and spread its steaming intestines across the ice. Reading the purple veins of the liver, she predicted the exact minute the temperature would drop thirty degrees. The temporal feedback burned away her memories, leaving her blind to her own past.

**Identity:** A combat prophet who reads the next ten seconds of bloodshed in fresh gore and splintered bone. Augurs do not predict grand royal destinies; they tell a warrior which way an enemy axe will swing, which stone will give way, and where an arrow will land. Every combat prophecy exacts a sharp toll in physical stamina or mental clarity.

**Role in World:** High-ranking tactical advisors in mountain fortress archives and war rooms. Generals use Augurs to position shield walls before an ambush springs, though common soldiers avoid sharing mess halls with them due to their blood-stained knives and unsettling muttering.

**Cultural Affiliation:** Practiced by Skald mountain-haruspexes, Astril crystalline seers, and Vreken peat-bog diviners.

*Six Cultural Variants:*
* **Skald ("The Glacier-Haruspex"):** Cassia’s original line. Slits open arctic beasts to read tactical movements in steaming viscera before the frost sets in.
* **Vashir Astril ("The Star-Viscera Reader"):** Reads future fractures through the glowing cracks in their own crystalline skin.
* **Silath Astril ("The Suppressed Oracle"):** Forces prophetic screams from suppressed alien memories locked inside their bone marrow.
* **Tessen ("The Keep-Prophet"):** Predicts military breaches by reading stress fractures spreading through stone fortress walls.
* **Clean Vreken ("The Bog-Gore Diviner"):** Pulls preserved corpses from peat bogs to extract historical warnings from ancient dead tissue.
* **Marked Vreken ("The Mycelium-Haruspex"):** Reads incoming danger through the flashing light pulses of subterranean root mushrooms.

**Fatal Flaw / Crisis:** Prophetic accuracy has plummeted from 93% to 41% across the continent. Entrails are showing impossible, contradictory futures. Something in the upper atmosphere is warping the flow of time, creating timeline echoes that are causing seers to choke on conflicting visions.

**Founder(s):** Cassia the Star-Eyed—her ancient body sits preserved upright inside a glacier vault, eyes frozen wide open as she whispers predictions to scribes. Current leader: Archive-Mistress Skadi Glass-Eye, who takes dictation from a founder who no longer remembers her own name.

# BERSERKER

**Origin:** Founded during the great southern migration by Grum the Smith, who led his freezing clan into the volcanic caldera of Sundale. When an armored ice-wyrm smashed through their stone barricade, Grum dropped his smithing hammer, inhaled the sulfurous smoke from a magma fissure, and charged. His veins boiled with so much adrenaline that his bicep tendons snapped, yet he crushed the monster's skull with his bare fists.

**Identity:** A walking engine of biological destruction. Berserkers do not wield spells; they force their own anatomy past human limits through boiling rage and chemical fury. This physical state is the Rage—muscles tear themselves from bone to strike with bone-crushing force, while the heart races like a runaway steam boiler.

**Role in World:** Frontline shock troops and heavy tunnel-breakers. Mercenary companies hire Berserkers to smash through reinforced iron gates and clear cave-in rubble, though frontier taverns ban them on sight due to their lethal barroom brawls.

**Cultural Affiliation:** Practiced by massive Skald mountain-clans, renegade Emberth forge-smiths, and vat-grown Groven laborers.

*Three Cultural Variants:*
* **Skald ("The Hunger-Pact Sworn"):** Inherits ancestral fury from survivors who ate their fallen comrades during the Hunger Winter to keep the bloodline alive.
* **Thrask Emberth ("The Caldera-Forged"):** Inhales basalt dust and magma fumes, fighting as if carrying on a personal fistfight with the volcano.
* **Morgh Groven ("The Vat-Woken"):** Engineered for docility, these heavy clay-clones weaponize dormant growth chemicals in their veins to tear off their own shock-collars.

**Fatal Flaw / Crisis:** While in Rage, Berserkers ignore all incoming medical healing. Pushing past 100 Heat triggers total metabolic burnout, causing heart valves and lungs to seize. In the mountains, a new generation of berserkers is igniting Rage without performing the ancestral rituals, sparking a bloody civil war among clan elders.

**Founder(s):** Grum Bloodhammer—dead for centuries, his colossal smithing hammer rests beside an active lava vent in Sundale. Current leader: Blood-Priest Hark Ash-Hammer, who guards the ancient anvil and orders the execution of any warrior who loses control of the fire.

# CHRONARCH

**Origin:** Three centuries ago, an engineer named Nesta realized that stone braces could not stop a moving glacier from crushing her subterranean holdfast. In desperation, she bolted a brass clockwork gear-engine directly into her sternum and linked its mainspring to her heartbeat. By forcing her own timeline into a localized loop, she stalled the falling ice for three days until her clan evacuated, permanently trapping her body in chronological friction.

**Identity:** A living temporal anchor whose spells stitch broken timelines back together. Chronarchs carry brass hourglasses not as ornaments, but as vital gauges measuring the heavy metabolic cost of their craft. When they rewind a companion’s bleeding chest wound, the kinetic recoil etches deep bruises into the Chronarch's own ribs.

**Role in World:** Emergency siege engineers and field medics. Mining guilds pay fortunes for Chronarchs to freeze falling mine shafts and reverse explosive cave-ins, though church inquisitors hunt them down for delaying the natural death of the sun.

**Cultural Affiliation:** Practiced by guild-licensed Fexric tinkerers and long-limbed Groven bridge-engineers.

*Three Cultural Variants:*
* **Kethrin Fexrick ("The Gear-Stitcher"):** Treats time as a mechanical gear-train with swappable teeth, maintaining their chest-engines with oil cans and spanners.
* **Ithran Groven ("The Bone-Calibrator"):** Uses their extended clay-bone limbs as physical lightning rods to ground temporal shockwaves across wide areas.
* **Tessen ("The Keep-Anchor"):** Anchors their life force to old fortress foundations, freezing time inside castle walls while the outer world ages.

**Fatal Flaw / Crisis:** Chronarchs suffer permanent 50% vulnerability to Arcane and Blight energy. Forced movement drops their evasion to zero and causes temporal backlash. Worse, Nesta is actively disappearing from historical records—written books bearing her signature are going blank, threatening to erase all living Chronarchs in a single temporal paradox.

**Founder(s):** Nesta the Clockwork Engineer—vanishing from physical reality. Current leader: Conclave-Prime Fex-Vestara, an engineer racing against the clock to build a replacement master-engine before Nesta fades completely from history.

# CRUSADER

**Origin:** Founded during the first decade of the freeze by Lady Vane of House Solvan, who refused to abandon the border keeps when the sun grew cold. She stripped the gold trim from her family armor, welded raw, glowing celestial shards into her breastplate, and led a vanguard into the blizzard to hunt down winter abominations. Her radiant fervor burned so hot that frost melted from her greatsword mid-swing.

**Identity:** A heavy-armored juggernaut fueled by the dying warmth of the celestial sacrifice. Crusaders do not hide behind fragile wards; they plant heavy iron boots in the snow, raise two-handed greatswords, and build **Fervor** with every crushing blow. When their fervor peaks, they discharge blazing starlight waves that shatter enemy plate armor and burn Wyrd horrors to ash.

**Role in World:** The armored frontline fist of the Dawn Vigil. Crusaders spearhead expeditions into infested ruins, hold bridgeheads against swarming abominations, and execute corrupted nobles. While respected for their iron discipline, common folk fear their unyielding zeal and quickness to condemn suspects to the pyre.

**Cultural Affiliation:** Practiced by Solari sun-loyalists, Skald heavy champions, and Marked Vreken penitents.

*Three Specialization Paths:*
* **Solvan Executioner:** Frontline shock breaker wielding colossal greatswords. Converts Fervor into armor-melting smites and creates permanent zones of consecrated fire.
* **Dawn Bastion:** An immovable shield-bearer who channels starlight into heavy tower plates, absorbing hits for allies and raising glowing barricades against missile fire.
* **Harmonic Inquisitor:** A specialized anti-mage who uses starlight lightning like a tuning fork, interrupting enemy spellcasts and shattering magical wards.

**Fatal Flaw / Crisis:** Crusaders depend entirely on continuous melee momentum. If a Crusader is bogged down or separated from the front line, their Fervor dissipates rapidly, leaving their heavy plate vulnerable to flanking strikes and kinetic artillery.

**Founder(s):** Lady Vane the Unbroken—fell defending the southern passes, her radiant broadsword still stuck upright in the permafrost. Current leader: High Judiciar Sol-Bannor, an unyielding commander who demands total obedience and views all who question the Dawn Vigil as traitors.

# FALSE PROPHET

**Origin:** Founded two centuries ago by Li Wei, a nomadic horse-herder who tracked a falling meteor into an impact crater. Instead of iron ore, he uncovered a cracked black monolith humming with an empty psychic vacuum. When he stared into the dark crystal where the sun's reflection should have been, his mind shattered under the voices of dead stars. He climbed out of the crater with milk-white eyes, preaching the gospel of the cosmic silence.

**Identity:** A psychic instigator who channels the cold vacuum between stars. False Prophets do not pray; they tear open psychic channels into the minds of others. Accumulating **Madness** through apocalyptic sermons and mental assaults, their spells grow increasingly devastating as their own mental stability crumbles toward total collapse.

**Role in World:** Charismatic cult leaders and underground revolutionaries. Hunted by city watches and burned as heretics by church knights, False Prophets operate in squalid refugee camps and mining slums, building devoted followings among starving workers by promising that the cold universe will swallow their masters whole.

**Cultural Affiliation:** Practiced by desperate human outcasts, crystalline Astril refugees, and impoverished Vreken debtors.

*Three Cultural Variants:*
* **Vashir Astril ("The False Star"):** Uses the natural glow of their crystalline skin to trick crowds into believing they are walking deities.
* **Silath Astril ("The Gagged Evangelist"):** Channels the psychic screams of their own suppressed alien heritage into weaponized sonic shocks.
* **Vreken ("The Debt-Preacher"):** Sells spiritual absolution from bank debts, using congregation belief as an alternate currency to break financial contracts.

**Fatal Flaw / Crisis:** Accumulating 20 Madness points triggers a violent psychic convulsion, stunning the Prophet. In recent months, the voice in their heads has begun giving precise, chilling orders: *"March to the mountain vaults. Break the iron seals. Let the cold inside."*

**Founder(s):** Li Wei the Blind—alive, bedridden, and continuously whispering cosmic coordinates. Current leader: Cell-Mother Mor-Vereth, a former slum-debtor who manages the secret underground network of preaching cells around Li Wei’s fevered breathing.

# GAMBIT

**Origin:** Born from the merger of two desperate wagers during the Great Freeze: Jax, a sea captain who rolled bone dice against an ocean storm-spirit to save his icebound ship, and Lyra, an elven bookkeeper who severed alternate timelines to pull her caravan out of a collapsing mountain pass. Jax won the favorable wind but lost the warmth in his blood forever; Lyra saved her friends but experienced fifty alternate deaths simultaneously, shattering her sanity.

**Identity:** A probability surgeon who trades in stolen luck and severed fate-lines. Gambits do not play cards for tavern coin—they gamble for their next heartbeat. By blending raw luck-siphoning with precise cartomancy, they twist odds in real time: turning an enemy's lethal arrow into a misfire while ensuring their own dagger finds a chink in full plate armor.

**Role in World:** High-stakes trade brokers, maritime navigators, and underworld kingpins. Gambits run the docks at Merrowport and arbitrate black-market contracts across mining towns, though frontier marshals banish them to prevent riots over rigged gambling tables.

**Cultural Affiliation:** Practiced by Merryn sailors, Shore Myrathil divers, Kessen contract-lawyers, and Groven bridge-builders.

*Four Cultural Variants:*
* **Merryn ("The Sea-Omen Gambler"):** Rolls carved whale-bone dice on ship decks, forcing ocean storms to delay their fury.
* **Shore Myrathil ("The Tide-Reader"):** Reads future probability in shifting foam patterns and changing skin pigmentation.
* **Kessen Neth ("The Clause-Gambler"):** Treats every card game as a high-stakes legal hearing, setting up bets so that losing pays double.
* **Ithran Groven ("The Span-Better"):** Bets on which stone bridge arches will buckle under siege engines, timing explosive charges to the exact second of collapse.

**Fatal Flaw / Crisis:** Running out of Fortune triggers **Cosmic Bankruptcy**, dealing 2d10 necrotic damage to the caster and stripping all defenses. Accumulating 13 Karmic Debt points triggers total reality collapse. The founder Jax walked into the freezing ocean to settle his debts, while Lyra’s radical faction is threatening to detonate the timeline entirely.

**Founder(s):** Jax the Storm-Wagerer (lost at sea) and Lyra the Probability-Watcher (in hiding). Current leader: Harbor-Master Merr-Cael, a ruthless maritime broker who has kept the guild solvent for thirty years through strict ledger discipline.

# HARBINGER

**Origin:** Founded during the middle freezing era when two apocalyptic scholars joined forces: Xyris, who tore open the first permanent gravity rift in the southern steppes, and Malakor, who calculated the cooling rate of the planetary core in an observatory tower. By proving mathematically that the planetary freeze was permanent and irreversible, they concluded that trying to keep the world alive was dragging out a painful death. They decided to speed up the end.

**Identity:** A doomsday mathematician who speaks reality-crushing equations into existence. Shadows bend toward a Harbinger, and the air around them distorts with gravity ripples. They do not cast spells through prayer; they accelerate the molecular breakdown of matter, causing armor to rust instantly and stone walls to shatter under localized gravity wells.

**Role in World:** Wanted fugitives and feared siege-breakers. Outlawed in every major keep, Harbingers live in remote yurt camps or deep mining sumps, hired under the table by desperate warlords who need fortress gates torn off their hinges in seconds.

**Cultural Affiliation:** Practiced by crystalline Vashir Astril, Solari sun-heretics, and Skald mountain scholars.

*Four Cultural Variants:*
* **Vashir Astril ("The Entropy-Symphony"):** Channels the acoustic death-rattle of their shattered homeworld into vibrating sonic pulses.
* **Silath Astril ("The Suppressed Catastrophe"):** Burns the suppressed memories of alien ecosystems as fuel for localized firestorms.
* **Solari ("The Dying-Light Doomsayer"):** Calculates the final extinguishment of the sun, drawing more power as the winter nights grow longer.
* **Tessen ("The Extinction-Architect"):** Plans battlefield massacres like architectural blueprints, staging structural collapses with surgical precision.

**Fatal Flaw / Crisis:** Reaching 100 Mayhem points triggers an uncontrollable wild surge that causes molecular dissociation in nearby allies. In the southern plains, their gravity rifts have become permanent tears in reality, sucking in topsoil and accelerating the regional freeze.

**Founder(s):** Xyris the Rift-Spanner (killed when her own portal collapsed) and Malakor the Finite (alive, commanding from an underground redoubt). Malakor views human hope as a mathematical rounding error and continues calculating the exact date of planetary extinction.

# INQUISITOR

**Origin:** Two centuries ago, two monster-hunting orders swore a blood oath to purge subterranean horrors. In the southern bogs, a hunter named Orven forged cold-iron blades to execute his own mutated kin. In the northern forests, a field surgeon named Elias opened his own forearm veins to lure face-stealing mist-mimics into physical flesh where they could be impaled. When the horror incursions tripled, the two survivors merged their disciplines into the Barbed Vow.

**Identity:** A grim occult hunter who uses cold iron, silver needles, and bound void-mimics to destroy horrors. Inquisitors wield **Authority**, a combat momentum generated by parrying magical strikes and breaking occult wards. Their bodies are maps of self-inflicted scars, silver-poisoned veins, and bound parasites held under absolute mental control.

**Role in World:** The ultimate defense against occult incursions. Frontier keeps grant Inquisitors full legal authority to execute suspects and purge contaminated livestock, though villagers bolt their shutters when an Inquisitor rides into town.

**Cultural Affiliation:** Practiced by Marked Vreken spore-trackers, Thalren frontier rangers, and Unwoven Mimir fog-hunters.

*Four Cultural Variants:*
* **Marked Vreken ("The Mycelium-Hunter"):** Tracks spiritual rot by plugging silver probes directly into subterranean fungal roots.
* **Clean Vreken ("The Glow-Auditor"):** Inspects bioluminescent slime trails to identify which demon crossed a threshold.
* **Thalren ("The Salt-Scarred"):** Elias’s original line. Baits shape-shifting mist monsters by deliberately cutting their own arms to bleed in the dark.
* **Vreken ("The Contract-Severer"):** Uses legal annulment knives to slice through demonic pacts and magical curses.

**Fatal Flaw / Crisis:** Only 47 licensed Inquisitors remain alive. New horrors are emerging from deep glacier cracks that possess no blood to poison and no contracts to sever. A fungal rash is spreading across every active Inquisitor's hands, marking them as prey for the very beasts they hunt.

**Founder(s):** Orven the Still-Handed (missing in the deep bogs) and Elias the Salt-Scarred (erased from town records). Current leader: High-Severer Vrael the Forty-Seventh, a scarred veteran who has begun teaching recruits how to graft living horrors directly into their limbs to balance the odds.

# LUNARCH

**Origin:** Founded during the great northern flight by Lady Selene, who fled a fortress massacre into the freezing moonlit woods. Desperate to guide her starving family through the dark, she struck a bargain with an ancient starlight entity nested in the treetops. She allowed a cold lunar parasite to burrow into her spinal cord. The entity lit her path with radiant moonlight, but drained the body heat from her blood, leaving her flesh as cold as river ice.

**Identity:** A living incubator for an alien starlight parasite. The Lunarch is not a gentle lunar priest; they are host to a cold, predatory entity that feeds on fading starlight. The **Lunar Cycle** dictates their combat abilities: every three rounds, the parasite shifts phases, rewriting the host’s nervous system to grant blinding shields, freezing beams, or life-draining touch.

**Role in World:** Respected spirit-guides among forest tribes, but hunted as heretical abominations by northern lords. City councils outlaw Lunarchs within stone walls, fearing that the parasites will spread to the civilian population through touch.

**Cultural Affiliation:** Practiced by the thorn-blooded Florae and the wood-carving Mimir.

*Six Cultural Variants:*
* **Viridian Florae ("The Thorn-Bound"):** The parasite's original bloodline. The lunar entity is bound directly into their living thorn-plate skin.
* **Oken Florae ("The Timber-Born"):** Passes as ordinary humans in cities, feeding their internal parasite in darkened cellars.
* **Masked Mimir ("The Moon-Masked"):** Uses the lunar parasite to replace a broken heartwood mask, maintaining their sanity in deep fog.
* **Woven Mimir ("The Sentinel-Moon"):** Uses glowing starlight veins to light frontier palisades during winter blizzards.
* **Unwoven Mimir ("The Moon-Saved"):** Uses the cold parasite to hold their dissolving physical body together against the mist.
* **Thalren ("The Fog-Heresy"):** Outcast humans whose parasites feed directly on memories erased by the freezing fog.

**Fatal Flaw / Crisis:** Lunarchs suffer +25% vulnerability to blunt force trauma, and standard healing spells deal Wyrd burn damage to their flesh. The dormant star in the upper atmosphere is waking up, and older parasites inside senior Lunarchs have begun seizing control of their hosts' vocal cords to chant in an alien tongue.

**Founder(s):** Selene of the Thorns—alive, but completely catatonic, whispering ancient coordinates from a hollow tree sanctuary. Current leader: Regent Bri-Vessela, a hardened warrior holding the splintered forest clans together through iron discipline.

# MARTYR

**Origin:** Founded in the aftermath of the celestial sacrifice by Sera, a grieving mother who refused to let her executed son’s name be wiped from town records. She carved his name into her forearm bone with a sliver of volcanic glass, swearing an oath to absorb the pain of every wounded soldier on the battlefield. Her grief opened a sympathetic blood-channel, allowing her to pull bleeding wounds off others and take them onto her own skin.

**Identity:** A frontline damage-absorber who catches weapons meant for companions. Martyrs do not carry heavy wooden shields; they throw their own flesh between an enemy blade and an ally. By taking wounds willingly, they build **Devotion**, converting their own bleeding flesh into emergency healing waves and radiant armor plates.

**Role in World:** Conscripted by military garrisons as emergency trauma anchors. A squad of soldiers accompanied by a Martyr can fight through lethal artillery bombardments, though commanders treat them as expendable battlefield tools rather than soldiers.

**Cultural Affiliation:** Practiced by Solari sun-loyalists, Korr furnace-dwarves, and Skald ironclad champions.

*Four Cultural Variants:*
* **Solari ("The Sun-Vigil"):** Sera’s original line. Every absorbed strike is treated as an offering to keep the ember of the infant sun alive.
* **Korr Emberth ("The Vault-Silent"):** Stands motionless over geothermal vents, absorbing explosive steam bursts to protect miners.
* **Velun Neth ("The Contract-Martyr"):** Absorbs damage based on formal legal agreements, invoicing the party for every drop of blood spilled.
* **Skald ("The Ironclad"):** Welds heavy iron plates directly onto their ribs, using internal heating pipes to convert absorbed impacts into steam pressure.

**Fatal Flaw / Crisis:** Failing to take damage or sacrifice health for one round causes **Devotion Collapse**. If Devotion decays three times, the Martyr becomes Faithless: all healing received is cut in half and they cannot intervene for allies. Feudal lords have begun kidnapping peasant children to forcibly brand them into conscripted martyr battalions.

**Founder(s):** Sera the Scarred—dead for centuries, her mummified forearm is preserved beneath a cathedral furnace in Sundale. Current leader: Vigil-Mother Sol-Kaessen, who wrestles with the morality of managing the army’s forced-conscription martyr program.

# MINSTREL

**Origin:** Founded during the first ice storms by Lyris, a merchant sailor who sang rhythmic sea shanties to calm the freezing waves of the northern sea, allowing her ice-locked cargo ship to make port. An ocean spirit granted her ship safe passage, but tore the spoken voice from her throat: she can only communicate through melodic flute notes or acoustic whispers. Attempting to yell causes her lungs to fill with saltwater.

**Identity:** An acoustic combatant who uses resonance, percussion, and grief-tuned instruments to tear holes in physical reality. The Minstrel does not play cheerful tavern songs; they use sound waves to shatter enemy armor, disrupt breathing, and force enemy nervous systems to match the rhythm of a dying heartbeat.

**Role in World:** Essential naval navigators and siege signalers. Port towns celebrate Minstrels for guiding fishing fleets through blizzard gales, though tavern keeps make them sign contracts promising not to shatter glassware with high-frequency strumming.

**Cultural Affiliation:** Practiced by Merryn sea-sailors and amphibious Myrathil divers.

*Four Cultural Variants:*
* **Merryn ("The Storm-Singer"):** Lyris’s original tradition. Uses brass horns to coordinate fleet movements through roaring hurricanes.
* **Shore Myrathil ("The Shore-Conductor"):** Controls crashing surf along rocky beaches, using breaking waves to knock down boarding parties.
* **Brook Myrathil ("The Freshwater-Voice"):** Carries acoustic magic up inland river networks to communicate between distant mining towns.
* **Deep Myrathil ("The Abyss-Resonant"):** Plays subsonic bass notes that rattle enemy bones from within, completely bypassing standard ear protection.

**Fatal Flaw / Crisis:** The northern sea has gone dead silent. The natural wave frequencies have stopped, and Lyris disappeared on the night the silence began. Deep-sea Myrathil are fleeing the abyssal trenches, reporting that something colossal on the ocean floor is learning their songs and singing them back.

**Founder(s):** Lyris the Tide-Singer—missing. Her driftwood lute was found floating in the harbor with a final warning carved into the varnish. Current leader: Choir-Mistress Mer-Lyrisa, who stands on the docks every midnight listening for a sea that makes no sound.

# PLAGUEBRINGER

**Origin:** Founded three centuries ago by Vespera, a cave alchemist who watched her underground clan die of respiratory mold. Desperate for an antidote, she gathered rotting bog-moss from a sunken ruin and injected the caustic bile directly into her arm. She stopped the infection, but her body became a living incubator for active decay—leaving her skin gray, her veins black, and her breath toxic.

**Identity:** A biological weapon who cultivates lethal bacterial colonies inside their own organs. Plaguebringers do not study magic in clean libraries; they deliberately infect themselves with balanced strains of parasites and virulent fungi. By bleeding into glass flasks, they fling caustic rot and flesh-eating spores that melt enemy armor and decompose charging infantry.

**Role in World:** Feared and strictly quarantined. Settlements force traveling Plaguebringers to camp outside stone walls and conduct business through iron drop-boxes, though logging barons hire them under the table to rot away disputed forests overnight.

**Cultural Affiliation:** Practiced by outcast Drun Neth exiles and desperate Marked Vreken cave-dwellers.

*Four Cultural Variants:*
* **Drun Neth ("The Silence-Host"):** Being legally dead, their cold flesh offers no immune resistance, making them ideal hosts for aggressive bacterial cultures.
* **Vreken ("The Desperation-Cultivator"):** Cultivates lethal rot as the one weapon feudal tax collectors cannot seize or tax.
* **Clean Vreken ("The Glow-Culture"):** Cultivates glowing bioluminescent mold, mapping the spread of infections across a battlefield in glowing green light.
* **Marked Vreken ("The Mycelium-Vector"):** Injects viral spores into underground tree roots, killing an enemy encampment miles away by poisoning their water source.

**Fatal Flaw / Crisis:** The master bacterial strain hosted inside Vespera’s body for three centuries is dying. The diseases carried by all Plaguebringers are mutating out of control, turning on their hosts and melting their own lungs.

**Founder(s):** Blight-Mother Vespera—bedridden and coughing black fluid, kept alive only by the toxic cultures in her blood. She has ordered her apprentices to engineer a successor strain before her heart stops.

# PYROFIEND

Pyrofiends do not cast fireballs through careful study; they crack their own knuckles to let liquid fire spray from their skin. **Cultural Affiliation:** Practiced by Thrask forge-dwarves and sun-worshipping Solari.

**Fatal Flaw / Crisis:** Reaching Inferno Level 9 leaves the Pyrofiend with exactly 3 combat turns before their body incinerates into a pile of ash. The entity Scathrach is currently whispering through the flames, calling all Pyrofiends to march into the central volcano for a final harvest.

**Founder(s):** The First Cabal—all seven incinerated by their own magic. Current leader: Last-Ember Sol-Vareths, an ancient caster whose body is ninety percent charcoal and who knows the exact hour his heart will turn to slag.

# REVENANT

**Origin:** Founded during the freeze when two death-casters refused to let the dead rest. In the southern bogs, Kora bargained with underground roots to anchor the spirits of six generations in her blood. In the northern archives, the dying scribe Vesper bound his soul into a black stone jar, freezing his own heart to stop lung-rot. **Identity:** A grim death-caster suspended between a warm heartbeat and the freezing grave. **Role in World:** Feared as grave-robbers, but hired by desperate commanders to turn enemy casualties into fresh frontline reinforcements during fortress sieges.

**Cultural Affiliation:** Practiced by Clean Vreken ancestral speakers and Drun Neth exiles.

* **Drun Neth ("The Contract-Expired"):** Legally dead and unbound by worldly laws; death cannot claim what is not on the ledger.

**Fatal Flaw / Crisis:** Peat-bog corpses are rising without commands, marching toward the sundered monoliths in silent columns. Twelve Revenants have been found dead, their blood completely drained without a mark on their skin.

**Founder(s):** Kora the Veil-Speaker (dead, her voice surviving in the ancestral choir) and Vesper the Scribe (his frozen heart beats once per hour in an underground vault). Current leader: Threshold-Keeper Kor-Vasseth.

# SHAPER

# SPELLGUARD

# TOXICOLOGIST

**Origin:** Founded during the early freezing decades by Varis, a frontier medic who needed a way to defend his scout patrols from giant mist-predators. Realizing that steel swords bounced off chitinous beast-hides, he began milking venom from dead horrors and concentrating the bile over coal braziers. Decades of boiling acidic secretions destroyed his sense of taste, stained his fingernails purple, and left him with severe chronic hand tremors—yet he can still measure a lethal drop of venom without spilling.

**Identity:** A self-poisoned combat chemist who weaponizes lethal toxins, gas bombs, and flesh-rotting catalysts. Toxicologists do not use clean healing magic; they turn their own blood into a toxic solvent. Every poison vial thrown costs chemical reagents and physical vitality, turning the surrounding ground into smoking acid pools.

**Role in World:** Frontier monster-slayers and black-market alchemists. Forestry companies hire Toxicologists to clear predator nests with toxic smoke, though tavern owners refuse to serve them drinks in civilian glassware.

**Cultural Affiliation:** Practiced by Thalren frontier scouts, Tethered Mimir brewers, Grave Neth, and Florae thorn-gatherers.

*Six Cultural Variants:*
* **Thalren ("The Fog-Distiller"):** Varis’s original line. Distills long-lasting area-denial poisons that linger in damp hollows for weeks.
* **Tethered Mimir ("The Floor-Brewer"):** Harvests decomposing tree bark to make slow-acting paralytic oils.

**Fatal Flaw / Crisis:** All healing received by a Toxicologist is permanently halved. Fire damage ignites the chemical residue on their clothes, causing their own vials to detonate. In the northern woods, the freezing fog is altering the chemical properties of venom, causing standard antidotes to spoil into lethal neurotoxins.

**Founder(s):** Varis the Trembling—ancient, hands shaking, still working in his canopy laboratory. He views the changing fog chemistry as a personal insult and refuses to leave his distillation coils until he has solved the new formula.

# WARDEN

**Origin:** Founded during the great underground riots by Alaric, a mine guard stationed in the deep tunnels. He held the beast for three days until it suffocated. When rescuers offered to cut the rusted chain out of his arm, he refused.

**Identity:** A heavy-armored lockdown specialist who pins colossal beasts in place using heavy iron chains grafted directly into forearm bones and vertebrae. The Warden does not cast nature spells; they throw massive barbed chains around a monster’s neck, locking their boots into the earth to hold the horror still while their companions hack it to pieces.

**Role in World:** The unbreakable backbone of mountain mining holds and frontier gates. Mining cartels pay double wages for a Warden to stand watch at tunnel heads, knowing that when a cave horror charges, the Warden will chain themselves to it rather than let it reach the miners.

**Cultural Affiliation:** Practiced by subterranean Morgh Groven, Fexric chain-smiths, and mountain Skald hunters.

*Seven Cultural Variants:*
* **Morgh Groven ("The Vat-Grounded"):** Invented the flesh-graft chain. Wearing the chains voluntarily is their ultimate declaration of freedom from the alchemists who engineered them.

* **Drall Fexrick ("The Gear-Tension"):** Uses ratchet winches bolted to their armor to mechanically reel in hooked monsters.
* **Kethrin Fexrick ("The Guild-Jailer"):** Treats chaining as a regulated trade, using certified links with documented tensile strength.
* **Skald ("The Glacier-Chain"):** Uses cold-treated iron chains that shrink and bite deeper as the temperature drops.
* **Clean Vreken ("The Glow-Tether"):** Uses glowing chains to illuminate the position of invisible horrors in the dark.
* **Marked Vreken ("The Mycelium-Leash"):** Threads barbed chains through subterranean tree roots, anchoring beasts to bedrock miles away.

**Fatal Flaw / Crisis:** Extreme winter cold is making standard iron brittle, causing chains to snap under the weight of charging horrors. Alaric refuses to let his disciples touch the whispering metal, even as broken-iron casualties mount.

**Founder(s):** Alaric the Law-Keeper—alive, his ancient Thrumm biology keeping him alive centuries past his time. The original chain remains rusted deep into his forearm bone. Notable figures: The Fexrick Drall Smiths, who perform the surgical flesh-grafting rites in mountain forge-clinics.
